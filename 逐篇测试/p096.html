The Importance of Law

法律的重要性

AThe law influences all of us virtually all the time. It governs almost all aspects of our behaviour, and even what happens to us when we are no longer alive. It affects us from the embryo onwards. It governs the air we breathe, the food and drink we consume, our travel, family relationships, and our property. It applies at the bottom of the ocean and in space. Each time we examine a label on a food product, engage in work as an employee or employer, travel on the roads, go to school to learn or to teach, stay in a hotel, borrow a library book, create or dissolve a commercial company, play sports, or engage the services of someone for anything from plumbing a sink to planning a city, we are in the world of law.

法律几乎无时无刻不在影响我们每个人。它规范我们行为的几乎所有方面，甚至还规定我们去世后会发生什么。从胚胎阶段开始，我们便受到法律影响。它规范着我们呼吸的空气、摄入的食物和饮料、出行、家庭关系及财产。海底和太空中同样适用法律。每当我们查看食品标签，以雇员或雇主身份工作，在道路上出行，到学校学习或教学，入住酒店，借阅图书馆书籍，成立或解散商业公司，参加体育运动，或是请人提供服务——小到给水槽接装水管，大到规划城市——我们都置身于法律世界。

BLaw has also become much more widely recognised as the standard by which behaviour needs to be judged. A very telling development in recent history is the way in which the idea of law has permeated all parts of social life. The universal standard of whether something is socially tolerated is progressively becoming whether it is legal, rather than something that has always been considered acceptable. In earlier times, most people were illiterate. Today, by contrast, a vast number of people can read, and it is becoming easier for people to take an interest in law, and for the general population to help actually shape the law in many countries. However, law is a versatile instrument that can be used equally well for the improvement or the degradation of humanity.

法律也越来越广泛地被视为评判行为的标准。近代以来，一个很能说明问题的变化是，法律观念已经渗透到社会生活的方方面面。一件事能否为社会容忍，逐渐越来越取决于它是否合法，而不再取决于它是否一直被认为可以接受。早期，大多数人不识字。相比之下，今天有大量人口能够阅读，人们更容易对法律产生兴趣；在许多国家，普通民众也更容易真正参与塑造法律。然而，法律是一种用途广泛的工具，既可以促进人类进步，也同样可以使人类倒退。

CThis, of course, puts law in a very significant position. In our rapidly developing world, all sorts of skills and knowledge are valuable. Those people, for example, with knowledge of computers, the internet, and communications technology are relied upon by the rest of us. There is now someone with IT skills or an IT help desk in every UK school, every company, every hospital, every local and central government office. Without their knowledge, many parts of commercial and social life today would seize up in minutes. But legal understanding is just as vital and as universally needed. The American comedian Jerry Seinfeld put it like this, ‘We are all throwing the dice, playing the game, moving our pieces around the board, but if there is a problem, the lawyer is the only person who has read the inside of the top of the box.’ In other words, the lawyer is the only person who has read and made sense of the rules.

当然，这使法律处于非常重要的地位。在快速发展的世界里，各种技能和知识都很宝贵。例如，我们其他人都依赖那些掌握计算机、互联网和通信技术知识的人。如今，英国每所学校、每家公司、每家医院，以及中央和地方政府的每个办事机构，都有具备信息技术（IT）技能的人员或 IT 服务台。如果没有他们的知识，当今商业和社会生活中的许多部分会在几分钟内停摆。不过，法律知识同样至关重要，也同样是所有人都需要的。美国喜剧演员 Jerry Seinfeld 这样说道：“我们都在掷骰子、玩游戏、在棋盘上移动棋子；但如果出现问题，律师是唯一读过盒盖内侧说明的人。”换句话说，律师是唯一读过规则并弄懂规则的人。

DThe number of laws has never been greater. In the UK alone, about 35 new Acts of Parliament are produced every year, thereby delivering thousands of new rules. The legislative output of the British Parliament has more than doubled in recent times from 1,100 pages a year in the early 1970s, to over 2,500 pages a year today. Between 1997 and 2006, the legislature passed 365 Acts of Parliament and more than 32,000 legally binding statutory instruments. In a system with so much law, lawyers do a great deal not just to vindicate the rights of citizens and organisations but also to help develop the law through legal arguments, some of which are adapted by judges to become laws. Law courts can and do produce new law and revise old law, but they do so having heard the arguments of lawyers.

法律的数量从未如此之多。仅在英国，议会每年就会制定约35部新的议会法令（Acts of Parliament），由此产生数千条新规则。英国议会的立法产出近年增加到原来的两倍以上，从20世纪70年代初每年1100页，增至如今每年超过2500页。1997至2006年间，立法机关通过了365部议会法令和超过3.2万项具有法律约束力的法定文书（statutory instruments）。在法律如此繁多的制度中，律师不仅为维护公民和组织的权利做了大量工作，还通过法律论证推动法律发展，其中一些论证经法官调整后会成为法律。法院能够、也确实会制定新法律和修订旧法律，但这样做之前会听取律师的论证。

EHowever, despite their important role in developing the rules, lawyers are not universally admired. Anti-lawyer jokes have a long history going back to the ancient Greeks. More recently, the son of a famous Hollywood actor was asked at his junior school what his father did for a living, to which he replied, ‘My daddy is a movie actor, and sometimes he plays the good guy, and sometimes, he plays the lawyer.’ For balance, though, it is worth remembering that there are and have been many heroic and revered lawyers such as the Roman philosopher and politician Cicero, and Mahatma Gandhi, the Indian campaigner for Independence.

然而，尽管律师在完善规则方面发挥着重要作用，他们并未受到所有人的敬仰。嘲笑律师的笑话历史悠久，可以追溯到古希腊。近些年，一位著名好莱坞演员的儿子在小学被问到父亲从事什么工作，他回答说：“我爸爸是电影演员，有时演好人，有时演律师。”不过，为求公允，我们也应记得，古今都有许多英勇而备受尊敬的律师，例如罗马哲学家兼政治家 Cicero，以及争取印度独立的活动家 Mahatma Gandhi。

FPeople sometimes make comments that characterise lawyers as professionals whose concerns put personal reward above truth, or who gain financially from misfortune. There are undoubtedly lawyers that would fit that bill, just as there are some scientists, journalists and others in that category. But, in general, it is no more just to say that lawyers are bad because they make a living from people’s problems than it is to make the same accusation in respect of nurses or IT consultants. A great many lawyers are involved in public law work, such as that involving civil liberties, housing and other issues. Such work is not lavishly remunerated and the quality of the service provided by these lawyers relies on considerable professional dedication. Moreover, much legal work has nothing to do with conflict or misfortune, but is primarily concerned with drafting documents. Another source of social disaffection for lawyers, and disaffection for the law, is a limited public understanding of how law works and how it could be changed. Greater clarity about these issues, maybe as a result of better public relations, would reduce many aspects of public dissatisfaction with the law.

有时，人们的评论会把律师描绘成把个人报酬置于真相之上的专业人士，或是从他人的不幸中获利的人。毫无疑问，有些律师符合这种描述，正如有些科学家、记者和其他人也属于这一类。不过，总的来说，因为律师靠处理人们的问题谋生就说律师很坏，并不比用同一理由指责护士或信息技术顾问更加公正。大量律师从事公法工作，例如处理公民自由、住房及其他问题。这类工作的报酬并不丰厚，而这些律师提供的服务质量，有赖于他们在专业上的高度投入。此外，许多法律工作与冲突或不幸毫无关系，主要涉及起草文件。社会不满律师、也不满法律的另一个原因，是公众对法律如何运作、又能如何改变了解有限。如果公众能更清楚地了解这些问题——或许可以通过改善公共关系来实现——公众对法律的许多不满便会减少。
