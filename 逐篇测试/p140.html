Sea Change for Salinity

盐碱化防治迎来重大转变

One of the most serious problems facing Australian farmers is an increase in the salt content in the soil. However, there are new weapons emerging in the fight against salinity.

澳大利亚农民面临的最严重问题之一，是土壤中的盐分不断增加。不过，抗击盐碱化的新武器正在出现。

ABeneath the flat, impassive surface of Australia lie hidden mountains, valleys and gorges – ancient traps and channels for the deadly salt that is stealthily killing so much of the Australian landscape. The war on salt is calling forth new weapons. A suite of high technologies used by geologists to see underground and prospect for gold and minerals is now being used to pinpoint the presence of salt beneath the landscape, and to predict where it might move.

澳大利亚平坦沉静的地表之下，隐藏着山脉、谷地和峡谷。它们是致命盐分古老的藏匿处和通道；这些盐分正悄然使澳大利亚大片土地失去生机。抗盐之战正在召唤新武器。地质学家用来透视地下、勘探黄金和矿物的一整套高科技，如今正被用于精确定位地表之下的盐分，并预测它可能流向何处。

BUnless this process is clearly understood, warns Chief of Exploration and Mining Dr Neil Phillips, the hard work now underway of planning and tree-planting on the surface may be rendered ineffective: salt can still sneak past and erupt, following one of the ancient river channels formed millions of years ago. The use of airborne electromagnetics to detect salt hidden beneath the landscape has been around for a decade, but the past two years have seen a major development in its precision and powers of detection. Like the use of radar in battles, it has the potential to turn the tide of the struggle in favour of the defence by helping to pinpoint, plot and predict the movements of the foe.

勘探与采矿部门主管 Neil Phillips 博士警告，如果不能清楚理解这一过程，目前正在地表进行的艰苦规划和植树工作可能变得无效：盐分仍能悄悄绕过防线，沿数百万年前形成的某条古河道突然涌出。利用航空电磁技术（airborne electromagnetics）探测地表下隐藏盐分的做法已有十年历史，但过去两年里，其精度和探测能力取得了重大发展。如同战场上使用雷达，这项技术可以帮助精确定位、绘制并预测敌人的动向，有望让防守一方扭转战局。

CAngus Howell, who farms near Warrenbayne, in Southeast Australia, saw his first outbreak of salt in 1948. Over the ensuing decades the patches spread and multiplied until they consumed almost 100 hectares. By the late 1970s, Howell and his fellow farmers had decided it was time for action and established a government-funded “Landcare” group in a bid to save Australia’s farmland. But despite a mounting effort by scientists, farmers and governments, the “white death” continued to encroach. Small successes were eclipsed by larger defeats and fresh outbreaks.

在澳大利亚东南部 Warrenbayne 附近务农的 Angus Howell，1948年第一次看到盐害暴发。随后几十年里，盐斑不断扩散、增多，最终吞噬近100公顷土地。到20世纪70年代末，Howell 和其他农民决定必须采取行动，成立了一个由政府资助的“土地保护”（Landcare）团体，试图拯救澳大利亚农田。然而，尽管科学家、农民和政府不断加大努力，“白色死神”仍继续侵袭。小小的成功被更大的失败和新出现的盐害掩盖。

D“The technical solutions just aren’t there yet for dealing with broadacre salinity, nor are the social and economic solutions. How do you introduce the land-use changes that are needed when people still need to make a living?” Howell asks. There is no satisfactory solution yet. Part of the problem has lain in salt’s ability to mount ambushes, emerging somewhere new, sometimes unexpected and unexplained, beating plans to intercept it. Only now are scientists starting to really disclose its secret subterranean stores and passages.

Howell 问道：“应对大面积农田盐碱化的技术方案还没有出现，社会和经济方面的解决办法也没有。人们仍要谋生时，如何才能推行必要的土地利用变革？”目前仍没有令人满意的解决方案。部分问题在于，盐分会发动伏击，在新的地点冒出，有时既出人意料又无法解释，从而避开原本要拦截它的计划。直到现在，科学家才开始真正揭示盐分在地下秘密储存的位置和流动通道。

EThe need for such knowledge is pressing. Salt has already afflicted six million hectares of once-productive country. At present rates, it is predicted that, by 2050, it will have sterilised a total of 17 million hectares and the waters of Australia’s Murray River will regularly exceed the World Health Organisation’s salt limits for drinking water. Defeating this assault may take centuries, not decades.

人们迫切需要掌握这类知识。盐害已经侵袭600万公顷一度富饶的土地。据预测，若按目前速度发展，到2050年，盐害将使总计1,700万公顷土地失去生产能力，澳大利亚墨累河（Murray River）的河水也会经常超过世界卫生组织（World Health Organisation）规定的饮用水盐分上限。击退这场侵袭可能需要几个世纪，而不是几十年。

FElectromagnetic surveys measure the electrical conductivity of soil to reveal the distribution of salt and the nature and variability of the regolith – the weathered rock and sediment that may lie above the bedrock. Magnetic surveys measure small differences in the Earth’s magnetic field, enabling scientists to probe the deep past and reconstruct ancient landscapes – rivers, basins and faults now buried under tens of metres of sediment. These features help to reveal where groundwater is stored, dictate the direction of groundwater movement, and are critical to predicting or ruling out salinity hot-spots.

电磁勘测（Electromagnetic surveys）通过测量土壤导电率，揭示盐分分布，以及风化层（regolith）的性质和变化；风化层是可能位于基岩之上的风化岩石与沉积物。磁力勘测（Magnetic surveys）测量地球磁场的细微差异，使科学家能够探究遥远的过去，重建如今埋在数十米沉积物下的古代景观，包括河流、盆地和断层。这些地貌有助于揭示地下水储存在何处，决定地下水流动的方向，对于预测或排除盐害热点至关重要。

GRadiometric analysis is based on the detection of radiation emitted by elements contained in rocks and soils, allowing scientists to delineate landforms. These factors influence the mobility of salt through the soil profile and help determine where to plant particular crop species to tackle the problem.

放射性测量分析（Radiometric analysis）以探测岩石和土壤所含元素释放的辐射为基础，使科学家能够勾勒地貌。这些因素会影响盐分在土壤剖面中的移动能力，也有助于确定应在何处种植特定作物品种来解决问题。

HUsing data from the Murray River region, scientists have revealed a network of ancient drainage systems that channelled water beneath the current land-surface. These buried channels may carry salt and sometimes run at right angles to channels on the surface. This implies that the salt could move underground in quite a different direction to what one would expect by looking at surface slope and drainage. One of the biggest advances in detection, says Professor Neil Phillips, has come with the integration of different techniques such as magnetics, electromagnetics and radiometrics, and ground mapping. Individually, these technologies only gave clues to what was going on underground. Together they provide a far more revealing picture of the subsurface landscape, several hundred metres deep. Advanced airborne electromagnetics, in particular, enables scientists to take “slices” of the landscape at depths of five metres, ten metres, fifteen metres and so on, to determine where salt may be stored at depth. This is building up a four-dimensional picture of the subsurface landscape, enabling researchers to understand movements of salt in length, breadth, depth and time. From such technologies it will be possible to locate salt stores, identify how saline they are, look at man-made and natural changes to the landscape that may cause it to mobilise, and then predict where it will head to and over what time span. This in turn will give the salt warriors time to model various ways of containing or curbing the menace, see what works best and then try it out on the ground.

科学家利用墨累河地区的数据，揭示出一套古代排水系统网络；这些系统曾在如今的地表之下输送水流。被掩埋的河道可能携带盐分，有时甚至与地表河道成直角。这意味着，盐分在地下的移动方向可能与根据地表坡度和排水情况所预期的方向大不相同。 Neil Phillips 教授说，探测领域最大的进步之一，是把磁力、电磁、放射性测量和地面测绘等不同技术结合起来。单独使用时，这些技术只能提供地下状况的线索；结合起来后，它们便能呈现清晰得多的地下景观图像，深度可达数百米。尤其是先进的航空电磁技术，使科学家能够在五米、十米、十五米等不同深度对景观进行“切片”，从而确定盐分可能储存在地下何处。这正在构建一幅地下景观的四维图像，让研究人员可以从长度、宽度、深度和时间四个维度理解盐分的移动。 借助这些技术，人们将能够找到盐分储存区，确定其中盐分浓度，考察可能使盐分开始移动的人为和自然景观变化，继而预测盐分会流向何处、需要多长时间。这样一来，抗盐人员便有时间建立模型，比较控制或遏制这一威胁的不同方法，找出效果最佳的方案，再到实地试用。
