The plan to bring an asteroid to Earth

把小行星带到地球的计划

Moving in orbit around our Sun are millions of rocks known as asteroids. Now scientists have plans to capture one.

数百万块被称为小行星的岩石正在围绕太阳运行。如今，科学家计划捕获其中一颗。

ASend a robot into space, catch an asteroid and bring it back to Earth’s orbit. This, say scientists and engineers at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech), could be feasible. A four-day workshop was dedicated to investigating the feasibility of capturing a near-Earth asteroid, bringing it closer to our planet and using it as a base for future manned space-flight missions.

把机器人送入太空，捕获一颗小行星，再把它带回地球轨道。加州理工学院（California Institute of Technology，简称 Caltech）的科学家和工程师说，这或许可行。一场为期4天的研讨会专门研究这一设想的可行性：捕获一颗近地小行星，把它带到更靠近地球的位置，并将其用作未来载人航天任务的基地。

This is not something the scientists are imagining could be done someday in the future. It is possible with the technology we have today and could be accomplished within a decade. A robotic probe could anchor to an asteroid with simple magnets or grab it with specialised claws. Alternatively, a large spacecraft could use its gravitational field to shift the orbit of a larger asteroid, sending it towards Earth.

科学家并不是在设想未来某一天也许能做到这件事。利用我们今天拥有的技术，这已经可能实现，而且可以在10年内完成。机器人探测器可以用简单磁铁固定在小行星上，也可以用专用机械爪抓住它。另一种办法是，让大型航天器利用自身引力场改变一颗较大小行星的轨道，把它送向地球。

‘Once you get over the initial reaction — You want to do what?! — it actually starts to seem like a reasonable idea,’ says engineer John Brophy, who helped organise the workshop. In fact, many of these ideas have been on the drawing board for years as part of NASA’s planetary-defence programme against large space-based objects that might threaten Earth. And there’s no shortage of potential targets: NASA estimates there are 19 500 asteroids at least 100 metres wide within 45 million kilometres of Earth.

协助组织研讨会的工程师 John Brophy 说：“只要克服最初那种‘你想干什么？！’的反应，这个想法实际上便开始显得合乎情理。”事实上，作为NASA 抵御可能威胁地球的大型太空物体的行星防御计划的一部分，其中许多设想已经筹划多年。潜在目标也并不短缺：NASA 估计，距离地球4500万公里以内，有1.95万颗宽度不小于100米的小行星。

BThough rearranging the heavens may seem an excessive undertaking, this US mission has its merits. The US already has plans to send astronauts to a near-Earth asteroid, a mission that would mean confining them in a tiny capsule for three to six months and would involve all the risks of a deep-space voyage. Instead, robots could bring an asteroid close enough for them to get there in just a month.

重新安排天体的位置也许显得是一项过分庞大的工程，但这项美国任务自有其优点。美国已经计划把宇航员送往一颗近地小行星；这项任务意味着要把他们限制在一个狭小太空舱中3至6个月，还会面临深空航行的全部风险。另一种办法是由机器人把小行星带得足够近，让宇航员只需一个月便能到达。

An asteroid would provide a stationary base from which to launch missions further into space. There are several advantages to this. For one, launching missions carrying materials from Earth requires a lot of power, fuel and, consequently, money, because of our planet’s strong force of gravity. Since this would be far weaker on an asteroid, materials mined there could be more easily taken off the asteroid and shuttled around the solar system.

小行星可以提供一个固定基地，供人们从那里出发，执行前往更遥远太空的任务。这有几项优势。首先，由于地球引力强大，携带物资从地球出发执行太空任务，需要大量动力和燃料，因而也需要大量资金。小行星上的引力要弱得多，因此在那里开采的材料可以更轻松地运离小行星，再转运到太阳系各处。

Many asteroids have a lot to offer. Some are rich in metals, which can be mined and used to build space-based habitats, or brought back to Earth. Others are up to one-quarter water, which could be used for life-support or broken down into hydrogen and oxygen to make fuel. Astronomers would also have the chance to get a close-up look at one of the solar system’s earliest relics, generating important scientific data. ‘Executing the asteroid-retrieval plan would help demonstrate and greatly expand mankind’s space-based engineering capabilities,’ says engineer Louis Friedman, another co-organiser of the Caltech workshop. For instance, the mission would teach engineers how to capture an unco-operative target, which could be useful practice for planetary-defence missions in the event of a threat from a meteoroid or comet approaching our planet,’ he adds.

许多小行星拥有大量可利用资源。有些富含金属，可以开采后用于建造太空居住设施，或运回地球。另一些小行星含水量高达四分之一，这些水既可以用于维持生命，也可以分解成氢和氧来制造燃料。天文学家还将有机会近距离观察太阳系最早期的遗存之一，获取重要科学数据。加州理工学院研讨会的另一位共同组织者、工程师 Louis Friedman 说：“执行小行星回收计划，将有助于展示并大幅提升人类的太空工程能力。”他还说：“例如，这项任务可以教会工程师如何捕获一个不配合的目标；如果有流星体或彗星接近地球并构成威胁，这会成为行星防御任务的实用演练。”

CFormer astronaut Rusty Schweickart, co-founder of the B612 Foundation, an organisation dedicated to protecting Earth from asteroid strikes, points out that although it would be technically feasible, shifting such a hefty and substantial target would not be easy: ‘You’re moving the largest mother-lode imaginable,’ he says.

前宇航员 Rusty Schweickart 是 B612 基金会（B612 Foundation）的共同创办人，该组织致力于保护地球免遭小行星撞击。他指出，尽管从技术上看此事可行，要移动如此沉重庞大的目标却并不容易。他说：“你要移动的是能够想象到的最大矿藏。”

Engineers would need to be absolutely certain they could control such a potentially dangerous object. ‘It’s the opposite of planetary defence; if you do something wrong you have a Tunguska event,’ says engineer Marco Tantardini from the Planetary Society, referring to the powerful 1908 explosion above a remote Russian region thought to have been caused by a meteoroid or comet.

工程师必须绝对确定，自己能够控制这样一个可能十分危险的物体。行星学会（Planetary Society）工程师 Marco Tantardini 说：“这与行星防御正好相反；如果哪里做错了，就会引发通古斯事件（Tunguska event）。”他指的是1908年在俄罗斯一处偏远地区上空发生的强烈爆炸，人们认为该事件由流星体或彗星引起。

DStill, these obstacles only add to the appeal of the project for engineers, who love to go over every potential difficulty in order to solve it. And if the challenges posed by a large asteroid seem too daunting, researchers could always start with a smaller asteroid, perhaps two to ten metres across. Last year, Brophy helped conduct a study to look at the feasibility of bringing a two-metre, 1 000-kilogram asteroid — of which there might conceivably be millions — to the International Space Station. The study suggested the asteroid could be captured robotically in something as simple as a large bag. Of course, such a small object might not have the same emotional impact as a larger target. ‘NASA isn’t going to want to go to something that is smaller than our spaceships,’ says engineer Dan Mazanek from NASA’s Langley Research Center.

不过，这些障碍只会增加项目对工程师的吸引力，因为他们喜欢逐一研究每一种潜在困难，再设法解决。如果大型小行星带来的挑战显得过于艰巨，研究人员随时可以从较小的小行星入手，也许选择一颗直径2至10米的目标。去年，Brophy 协助开展了一项研究，考察把一颗直径2米、重1000公斤的小行星带到国际空间站（International Space Station）是否可行；这样的目标可能有数百万颗。这项研究提示，机器人或许只需一个大袋子那么简单的装置，就能捕获这颗小行星。当然，这么小的物体可能无法像较大目标那样激动人心。NASA 兰利研究中心（Langley Research Center）工程师 Dan Mazanek 说：“NASA 不会愿意前往一个比我们的飞船还小的东西。”

ENo matter the size of the asteroid, these plans would require hefty investments. Even capturing a small asteroid would consume at least a billion dollars. Convincing taxpayers to foot such a bill could be difficult. However, private industry might be interested in getting involved. One possibility would be to push the asteroid into near-Earth orbit and then invite anyone who wants to develop the capabilities to reach and mine the object.

无论小行星大小如何，这些计划都需要巨额投资。即使只捕获一颗小型小行星，也至少要花费10亿 dollars。要说服纳税人承担这笔费用可能很困难。不过，私营企业或许有兴趣参与。一种做法是把小行星推入近地轨道，然后邀请任何愿意开发相关能力的人前往并开采它。

However, although the undertaking might be scientifically exciting and provide great insight into the solar system’s formation, this is not enough on its own to justify the expense of bringing an asteroid to Earth. Investigations of asteroids can be done much more cheaply with an unmanned spacecraft, says chemist Joseph A. Nuth from NASA’s Goddard Space Flight Center. According to Brophy, ultimately we would be working towards bringing an asteroid closer to Earth in order to help humanity move out into the solar system.

不过，尽管这项工程在科学上可能令人兴奋，也能让人们深入了解太阳系的形成，仅凭这些还不足以证明把小行星带到地球所需的费用合理。NASA 戈达德太空飞行中心（Goddard Space Flight Center）化学家 Joseph A. Nuth 说，使用无人航天器能够以低得多的成本研究小行星。Brophy 表示，归根结底，我们努力把小行星带到更靠近地球的地方，是为了帮助人类向太阳系深处迈进。
