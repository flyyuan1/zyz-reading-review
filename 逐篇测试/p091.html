The dingo debate

澳洲野犬之争

Graziers see them as pests, and poisoning is common, but some biologists think Australia’s dingoes are the best weapon in a war against imported cats and foxes.

牧场主把澳洲野犬（dingoes）视为害兽，投毒也十分普遍；但一些生物学家认为，在对抗外来猫和狐狸的战争中，澳大利亚的野犬是最有力的武器。

AA plane flies a slow pattern over Carlton Hill station, a 3,600 square kilometre ranch in the Kimberley region in northwest Australia. As the plane circles, those aboard drop 1,000 small pieces of meat, one by one, onto the scrubland below, each piece laced with poison; this practice is known as baiting. Besides 50,000 head of cattle, Carlton Hill is home to the dingo, Australia’s largest mammalian predator and the bane of a grazier’s (cattle farmer’s) life. Stuart McKechnie, manager of Carlton Hill, complains that graziers’ livelihoods are threatened when dingoes prey on cattle. But one man wants the baiting to end, and for dingoes to once again roam Australia’s wide-open spaces. According to Chris Johnson of James Cook University, “Australia needs more dingoes to protect our biodiversity.”

一架飞机在卡尔顿山牧场（Carlton Hill station）上空缓慢地沿固定路线飞行。这座牧场面积3600平方公里，位于澳大利亚西北部金伯利（Kimberley）地区。飞机盘旋时，机上人员把1000小块肉逐一投向下方灌木地，每一块都掺有毒药；这种做法称为投放毒饵。 卡尔顿山牧场除了饲养5万头牛，还是澳洲野犬的栖息地。澳洲野犬是澳大利亚最大的哺乳动物捕食者，也是牛场主生活中的祸害。卡尔顿山牧场经理 Stuart McKechnie 抱怨，野犬捕食牛只，威胁着牧场主的生计。但有一个人希望停止投饵，让澳洲野犬再次自由游荡在澳大利亚辽阔的旷野。詹姆斯·库克大学（James Cook University）的 Chris Johnson 说：“澳大利亚需要更多澳洲野犬来保护生物多样性。”

BAbout 4,000 years ago, Asian sailors introduced dingoes to Australia. Throughout the ensuing millennia, these descendants of the wolf spread across the continent and, as the Tasmanian tiger disappeared completely from Australia, dingoes became Australia’s top predators. As agricultural development took place, the European settlers found that they could not safely keep their livestock where dingoes roamed. So began one of the most sustained efforts at pest control in Australia’s history. Over the last 150 years, dingoes have been shot and poisoned, and fences have been used in an attempt to keep them away from livestock. But at the same time, as the European settlers tried to eliminate one native pest from Australia, they introduced more of their own.

约4000年前，亚洲水手把澳洲野犬带到澳大利亚。此后数千年里，这些狼的后代遍布整片大陆；随着袋狼（Tasmanian tiger）从澳大利亚彻底消失，澳洲野犬成为当地的顶级捕食者。农业发展起来后，欧洲定居者发现，在野犬出没的地方无法安全饲养牲畜。由此，澳大利亚历史上持续时间最长的害兽控制行动之一拉开序幕。过去150年中，人们射杀、毒杀澳洲野犬，还架设围栏，试图让它们远离牲畜。不过，就在欧洲定居者企图从澳大利亚消灭一种本地害兽的同时，他们又带来了更多自己的害兽。

CIn 1860, the rabbit was unleashed on Australia by a wealthy landowner and by 1980 rabbits had covered most of the mainland. Rabbits provide a huge prey base for two other introduced species: the feral (wild) cat and the red fox. The interaction between foxes, cats and rabbits is a huge problem for native mammals. In good years, rabbit numbers increase dramatically, and fox and cat populations grow quickly in response to the abundance of this prey. When bad seasons follow, rabbit numbers are significantly reduced – and the dwindling but still large fox and cat populations are left with little to eat besides native mammals.

1860年，一位富有地主把兔子放入澳大利亚野外；到1980年，兔子已经遍布大陆大部分地区。兔子为另外两个外来物种——野猫（feral cat）和赤狐（red fox）——提供了数量庞大的猎物。 狐狸、猫和兔子之间的相互作用，给本地哺乳动物造成了巨大问题。年景好时，兔子数量急剧增加，狐狸和猫也因猎物丰富而迅速繁殖。随后遇上恶劣年景，兔子数量大幅减少；数量虽在下降但仍然庞大的狐狸和猫，除了本地哺乳动物外便几乎无物可食。

DAustralian mammals generally reproduce much more slowly than rabbits, cats and foxes – an adaptation to prevent overpopulation in the arid environment, where food can be scarce and unreliable – and populations decline because they can’t grow fast enough to replace animals killed by the predators. Johnson says dingoes are the solution to this problem because they keep cat and fox populations under control. Besides regularly eating the smaller predators, dingoes will kill them simply to lessen competition. Dingo packs live in large, stable territories and generally have only one fertile female, which limits their rate of increase. In the 4,000 years that dingoes have been in Australia, they have contributed to few, if any, extinctions, Johnson says.

澳大利亚哺乳动物的繁殖速度通常远慢于兔子、猫和狐狸。这是为防止干旱环境中种群数量过多而形成的适应性特征，因为那里的食物可能稀少而且供应不稳定。这些动物无法足够快地繁殖，以补充因捕食而死亡的个体，因此种群数量不断下降。Johnson 说，澳洲野犬是这个问题的解决办法，因为它们能控制猫和狐狸的数量。野犬不仅经常吃掉这些较小的捕食者，还会只为减少竞争而将它们杀死。野犬群生活在面积广阔而稳定的领地里，通常只有一只具备繁殖能力的雌性，这限制了种群增长速度。Johnson 说，澳洲野犬进入澳大利亚的4000年间，即使曾造成物种灭绝，数量也寥寥无几。

EReaching out from a desolate spot where three states meet, for 2,500 km in either direction, is the world’s longest fence, two meters high and stretching from the coast in Queensland to the Great Australian Bight in South Australia; it is there to keep dingoes out of the southeast. The fence separates the main types of livestock found in Australia. To the northwest of the fence, cattle predominate; to the southwest, sheep fill the landscape. In fact, Australia is a land dominated by these animals – 25 million cattle, 100 million sheep and just over 20 million people.

世界上最长的围栏从三州交界的一处荒凉地点向两边各延伸2500公里，高2米，从昆士兰（Queensland）海岸一直伸到南澳大利亚州（South Australia）的大澳大利亚湾（Great Australian Bight）；它的作用是把澳洲野犬挡在东南部以外。这道围栏把澳大利亚主要的两类牲畜分隔开来。围栏西北侧以牛为主，西南侧则遍地是羊。事实上，澳大利亚这片土地由这些动物占据主导：有2500万头牛、1亿只羊，而人口刚刚超过2000万。

FWhile there is no argument that dingoes will prey on sheep if given the chance, they don’t hunt cattle once the calves are much past two or three weeks old, according to McKechnie. And a study in Queensland suggests that dingoes don’t even prey heavily on the newborn calves unless their staple prey disappears due to deteriorating conditions like drought. This study, co-authored by Lee Allen of the Robert Wicks Research Centre in Queensland, suggests that the aggressive baiting programs used against dingoes may actually be counter-productive for graziers. When dingoes are removed from an area by baiting, the area is recolonized by younger, more solitary dingoes. These animals aren’t capable of going after large prey like kangaroos, so they turn to calves. In their study, some of the highest rates of calf predation occurred in areas that had been baited.

没人否认，只要有机会，澳洲野犬就会捕食绵羊。不过，McKechnie 说，当小牛的年龄已远超两三周时，野犬便不会再捕食它们。昆士兰的一项研究还提示，除非干旱等环境恶化使野犬的主要猎物消失，否则它们甚至不会大量捕食刚出生的小牛。 昆士兰罗伯特·威克斯研究中心（Robert Wicks Research Centre）的 Lee Allen 是这项研究的共同作者。研究提示，牧场主大规模投饵毒杀野犬，实际效果可能适得其反。投饵把野犬从一片地区清除后，更年轻、更惯于独处的野犬会重新占据这里。这些野犬没有能力捕杀袋鼠等大型猎物，于是转而捕食小牛。研究中，小牛遭捕食比例最高的一些地区，正是曾经投放毒饵的地区。

GMark Clifford, general manager of a firm that manages over 200,000 head of cattle, is not convinced by Allen’s assertion. Clifford says, “It’s obvious if we drop or loosen control on dingoes, we are going to lose more calves.” He doesn’t believe that dingoes will go after kangaroos when calves are around. Nor is he persuaded of dingoes’ supposed ecological benefits, saying he is not convinced that they manage to catch cats that often, believing they are more likely to catch small native animals instead.

一家管理着20多万头牛的公司总经理 Mark Clifford，并不信服 Allen 的说法。Clifford 说：“显而易见，如果我们减少或放松对野犬的控制，就会损失更多小牛。”他不相信身边有小牛时，澳洲野犬还会去追捕袋鼠。他同样不信服所谓的野犬生态效益。他说，自己不相信野犬能如此频繁地抓到猫，反而认为它们更可能捕捉本地小型动物。

HMcKechnie agrees that dingoes kill the wallabies (small native animals) that compete with his cattle for food, but points out that in parts of Western Australia, there are no foxes, and not very many cats. He doesn’t see how relaxing controls on dingoes in his area will improve the ecological balance. Johnson sees a need for a change in philosophy on the part of graziers. “There might be a number of different ways of thinking through dingo management in cattle country,” he says. “At the moment, though, that hasn’t got through to graziers. There’s still just one prescription, and that is to bait as widely as possible.”

McKechnie 承认，澳洲野犬会杀死与他的牛争夺食物的小袋鼠（wallabies，一类本地小型动物），但他指出，西澳大利亚州（Western Australia）的一些地区没有狐狸，猫也不多。他不明白，在自己所在地区放松野犬控制，怎么会改善生态平衡。 Johnson 认为，牧场主需要转变观念。他说：“在养牛地区管理澳洲野犬，或许可以有多种不同思路。不过，目前牧场主还没有接受这一点。现在仍然只有一种办法，那就是尽可能广泛地投放毒饵。”
