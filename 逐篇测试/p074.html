Investment in shares versus investment in other assets

投资股票与投资其他资产

Which Gives the Greater Gain?

哪一种收益更高？

How one university collected the data to try and answer this question

一所大学如何收集数据，试图回答这个问题

AIt all began in 1958 with a phone call from Louis Engel, a banker at Merrill Lynch, a US-based financial management company, who wanted to know how investors in shares had performed relative to investors in other assets such as low-risk investments with guaranteed returns. ‘I don’t know, but if you gave me $50,000 I could find out,’ replied Jim Lorie, a dean at the University of Chicago’s business school. Louis Engel soon agreed to provide the funding, and more. The result, in 1960, was the launch of the University’s Center for Research in Security Prices. Half a century later CRSP (pronounced ‘crisp’) data are everywhere. They provided the foundation of at least one-third of all empirical research in finance over the past 40 years, according to a presentation at a symposium held this month. They probably influenced much of the rest.

一切始于1958年的一通电话。美国金融管理公司美林（Merrill Lynch）的银行家 Louis Engel 想知道，与投资有回报保障的低风险资产等其他资产的人相比，股票投资者的表现如何。芝加哥大学（University of Chicago）商学院院长 Jim Lorie 回答：“我不知道，但如果你给我 $50,000，我就能查明。”Louis Engel 很快同意提供这笔资金，而且还不止这些。于是，1960年，该校成立了证券价格研究中心（Center for Research in Security Prices）。半个世纪后，CRSP 的数据无处不在。CRSP 读作“crisp”。据本月一场研讨会上的报告称，过去40年至少三分之一的金融实证研究都以这些数据为基础，而其余研究很可能也大多受到它们影响。

BGetting the CRSP data together was a tough process in what were then the early days of computers. Up to three million pieces of information on all the shares traded on the New York Stock Exchange between 1926 and 1960 were transferred from paper in the exchange’s archive to magnetic tape. A lot of time was spent adjusting prices to take account of complexities in the market. Lorie and his co-researcher, Lawrence Fisher, chose January 1926 as the start date because they wanted the data to span at least one complete business cycle from boom to bust, or vice versa.

在当时计算机尚处于早期发展阶段的情况下，汇集 CRSP 数据是一个艰难的过程。纽约证券交易所（New York Stock Exchange）档案中，1926年至1960年所有交易股票的资料多达300万项，它们从纸张转录到磁带上。研究人员还花费大量时间调整价格，以计入市场中的种种复杂因素。Lorie 和共同研究者 Lawrence Fisher 选择1926年1月作为起始日期，是因为他们希望数据至少涵盖一个从繁荣到萧条，或反向变化的完整商业周期。

CWhen these two economists published the first study based on the CRSP data in 1964, they reported that the annual compound return on the shares over the entire 35-year period was (depending on the tax status of the investor) between 6.8% and 9%. Acknowledging that good data on the performance of other assets were not available, the study claimed that the rate of return on shares was ‘substantially higher than for alternative investment media’, providing the first empirical support for the still-popular idea that shares outperform other investments over the long run. Fisher and Lorie also observed that many people chose to invest in assets with lower returns because they were cautious by nature and were concerned about the risk of loss inherent in investing in the stock market. Economists today call the amount of extra return that investors need to compensate them for this additional risk the ‘equity risk premium’, although they differ greatly on how big investors should expect it to be.

1964年，这两位经济学家发表第一项以 CRSP 数据为基础的研究时报告称，在完整的35年期间，股票的年复合收益率介于6.8%至9%之间，具体取决于投资者的税务状况。研究承认，当时没有关于其他资产表现的优质数据，但仍声称股票收益率“远高于其他投资工具”，从而首次以实证证据支持了如今依然流行的观点：从长期看，股票的表现优于其他投资。Fisher 和 Lorie 还观察到，许多人天性谨慎，担心投资股市固有的亏损风险，因而选择收益较低的资产。如今，经济学家把投资者为补偿这项额外风险而要求获得的额外收益称为“股权风险溢价”（equity risk premium），不过对于投资者应预期它有多大，他们的看法差异很大。

DAfter Fisher and Lorie’s 1964 report there was no stopping the love affair between financial economists and the data that studying these numbers produced. Myron Scholes, now a Nobel laureate, became director of CRSP in 1974, and ensured the database was both kept up to date and made readily available to academic economists everywhere. In turn, this resource became ever more useful as computing power became more affordable. The CRSP database has since been expanded to include a full range of different types of investments. It has been replicated across the world.

Fisher 和 Lorie 的1964年报告发表后，金融经济学家开始对分析这些数字所能得到的数据着迷，此后这股研究热情一发不可收拾。后来获得诺贝尔奖的 Myron Scholes 于1974年出任 CRSP 主任，并确保数据库不断更新，也方便世界各地的学术经济学家使用。随着计算成本下降，这项资源也变得越来越有用。此后，CRSP 数据库已扩展到涵盖各种投资类型，世界各地也建立了类似的数据库。

EOne of the earliest uses of CRSP data was by Eugene Fama, an economist at the University of Chicago, to support his ‘efficient-market hypothesis’. He found that over a lengthy period share prices tended to rise and fall randomly, without showing much of a pattern. Markets are efficient, he said, because all relevant information is reflected in share prices at any given moment, meaning there are no predictable movements in prices for smart investors to exploit. Fama did concede that there was some evidence of temporary short-term predictability in share prices, however. That stipulation has resulted in a vast number of papers based on discovering such ‘variations’ through data mining. In theory, such anomalies are potentially lucrative for investors, but as believers in efficient markets observe with satisfaction, it seems that no sooner are such anomalies discovered and reported in journals than they typically disappear.

CRSP 数据最早的用途之一，是芝加哥大学经济学家 Eugene Fama 用它来支持自己的“有效市场假说”（efficient-market hypothesis）。他发现，在很长一段时期内，股票价格往往随机涨跌，几乎不呈现固定规律。他说，市场之所以有效，是因为任何时刻的股价都反映了所有相关信息，这意味着不存在可供聪明投资者利用的、能够预测的价格走势。不过，Fama 确实承认，有些证据表明股价在短期内暂时具有可预测性。这项限定引出了大量论文，它们通过数据挖掘来发现这类“变动规律”。理论上，这类异常现象可能让投资者获利；但令有效市场的信奉者满意的是，此类异常似乎通常刚被发现并在期刊上报道，便会消失。

FHowever, the sheer volume of material means that financial economists are becoming increasingly specialised, which may have costs as well as benefits. Some economists worry that much of this statistical analysis is creating some interference that drowns out serious thinking about the big questions, such as why the financial system nearly collapsed in 2008 and how a repeat can be avoided. Robert Shiller, an economist at Yale University and a long-time sceptic about the efficient-market hypothesis, feels that with the creation of the CRSP database economists suddenly believed that finance had become scientific. According to Shiller, conventional ideas about investing and financial markets – and about their vulnerabilities – seemed out of date to the new empiricists. He worries that academic departments are full of economists who are so specialised in data analysis that they fail to see and understand the whole. They get a sense of authority from work that contains lots of data. To have seen the 2008 global financial crisis coming, he argues, it would have been better to ‘go back to old-fashioned readings of history, studying institutions and laws. We should have talked to grandpa.’

然而，资料数量极为庞大，意味着金融经济学家的专业分工越来越细，这既可能带来好处，也可能付出代价。有些经济学家担心，大量统计分析正在制造干扰，淹没对重大问题的严肃思考，例如金融体系为何在2008年几近崩溃，以及如何避免重演。耶鲁大学（Yale University）经济学家 Robert Shiller 长期质疑有效市场假说；他认为，CRSP 数据库建立后，经济学家突然相信金融学已经成为一门科学。Shiller 说，关于投资、金融市场及其脆弱性的传统观念，在新一代实证研究者看来似乎已经过时。他担心，大学院系里到处都是专攻数据分析、却看不见也不理解全局的经济学家。他们从包含大量数据的研究中获得一种权威感。他主张，若想预见2008年全球金融危机，最好是“回头读读传统的历史著述，研究制度和法律。我们本该和爷爷聊聊。”

GScholes responds to this criticism with the contention that the usefulness of this empirical analysis is proven by the fact that demand for it continues to grow. At CRSP’s 50th-anniversary symposium, plans were unveiled to publish indicators on an expanding range of investments, as well as for growth and value stocks. These indicators, CRSP claims, will be more academically rigorous and cheaper than existing ones. For believers in the efficiency of markets, that should be enough to ensure CRSP’s continuing success.

Scholes 回应这项批评时主张，市场对实证分析的需求持续增长，已经证明它确实有用。在 CRSP 成立50周年研讨会上，中心公布了计划：发布覆盖更多投资类别的指标，以及成长股和价值股指标。CRSP 声称，这些指标在学术上会比现有指标更严谨，价格也更低。对相信市场有效的人来说，这应当足以确保 CRSP 继续取得成功。
