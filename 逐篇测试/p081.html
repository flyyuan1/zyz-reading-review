Nanotechnology: the science of the very small

纳米技术：研究微小尺度的科学

ANanoscience and nanotechnology have become very fashionable in recent times. Their prominence has inspired science fiction and raised a lot of hope and hype about the possibilities. But what exactly is nanotechnology? Most definitions agree that it is the manufacture of structures that measure up to 100 nanometres (nm). The diameter of an atom is typically only two or three tenths of a nanometre while that of the human hair is around 100,000 nm. Essentially, we are now able to build new structures from atoms and molecules as if we were using the bricks of a children’s construction toy.

近来，纳米科学和纳米技术变得十分时髦。它们备受瞩目，激发了科幻创作，也让人们对各种可能性既满怀希望，又大肆炒作。但纳米技术究竟是什么？大多数定义都认同，它是制造尺寸不超过100纳米（nm）的结构。一枚原子的直径通常只有零点二或零点三纳米，而人类头发的直径约为100,000纳米。从本质上说，我们如今能够用原子和分子建造新结构，就像使用儿童拼搭玩具的积木一样。

BMost of the electronic consumer goods that we use today contain components that measure less than 100 nm. The lasers in our CD and DVD players make use of the properties of semiconductor layers that are just a few atoms in thickness, so we clearly are in the nanotechnology age. There are many other everyday items that we take for granted that have nanoparticles in them. One of these is sunscreen. In the 1990s there were safety issues with many of the sunscreen products available, so scientists at Oxford University set about finding a solution. The product they developed uses nanoparticles of titanium dioxide that are doped with the element manganese. This product is almost transparent to the eye when spread on the skin, and it is effective for much longer than many of the traditional preparations. In this case, we see an example of nanotechnology providing a solution to an existing problem rather than opening up new product areas.

我们今天使用的大多数消费电子产品，都含有尺寸小于100纳米的元件。CD 和 DVD 播放机中的激光器利用了只有几个原子厚的半导体层所具有的性质，因此我们显然已经身处纳米技术时代。许多习以为常的日常用品中也含有纳米颗粒，防晒霜便是其中之一。20世纪90年代，市面上许多防晒产品存在安全问题，于是牛津大学（Oxford University）的科学家开始寻找解决方案。他们研发的产品使用掺入锰元素的二氧化钛纳米颗粒。这种产品涂在皮肤上时，肉眼看起来几乎透明，有效时间也比许多传统制剂长得多。这个例子表明，纳米技术是在解决现有问题，而不是开辟新的产品领域。

CThe technique of using nanotechnology to adapt existing products can also be applied to garments, upholstery, paint, and plastics to improve their longevity. Bearing this in mind, it is interesting to ask whether nanotechnology can help with some of the world’s major current issues. The recent growth in climate change awareness calls a range of things into question. Can we use energy more effectively? Can we reduce the carbon dioxide in the atmosphere? Can we use and purify water more effectively? Can health care be improved? Nanotechnology may very well provide the solutions. There are few times in history when the application of science to improving the human condition has been so apparent.

利用纳米技术改良现有产品的方法，也可以应用于服装、家具软包材料、油漆和塑料，以延长其使用寿命。考虑到这一点，值得探问纳米技术能否帮助解决当今世界的一些重大问题。近来，人们越来越关注气候变化，由此引出了一系列问题：我们能否更有效地使用能源？能否减少大气中的二氧化碳？能否更有效地用水和净水？能否改善医疗保健？纳米技术很可能会提供答案。历史上，很少有哪个时期能如此清楚地看到科学应用正在改善人类的生活状况。

DWe are already making new types of solar cells and batteries by using our newfound ability to manipulate matter at the atomic and molecular scale. If we can capture some of the huge quantities of energy that arrive at the Earth’s surface from the Sun, and store it effectively, we can reduce the need for fossil fuel-based power stations. We are already using nanoparticles and nanoporous materials to capture harmful greenhouse gases, and we may possibly convert some of them back into fuel. There are, however, many technical problems involved, and it will be another decade or so before some of these ideas are deployed.

我们已经利用新近获得的、在原子和分子尺度操控物质的能力，制造新型太阳能电池和蓄电池。如果能够捕获抵达地球表面的部分巨量太阳能，并加以有效储存，我们就能减少对化石燃料发电站的需求。我们已经在利用纳米颗粒和纳米多孔材料捕获有害温室气体，或许还能把其中一部分重新转化为燃料。不过，这涉及许多技术难题，其中一些构想还要约十年才能投入应用。

EWater purification is increasingly challenging and many believe that water, which we take for granted, is going to become more valuable than oil. Currently we do not use water efficiently, and many industrial and food processes leave contamination in water that is potentially toxic and difficult to remove. Nanofiltration may provide the solution to these problems, and may also offer new routes to the desalination of brackish water and groundwater in some parts of the world.

净化水的难度越来越大，许多人认为，我们一直视为理所当然的水将变得比石油更珍贵。目前，我们用水效率不高，许多工业和食品加工过程还会在水中留下可能有毒、难以清除的污染物。纳米过滤或许能解决这些问题，并可能为世界一些地区的微咸水和地下水淡化提供新途径。

FFood production and processing are becoming more important as the world’s population increases. There are concerns that potentially harmful nano-products might enter the body via the gut. Such concerns are being addressed, but it is even more important to ask questions about how nanotechnology might increase the nutritious yield of food and be adapted to help preserve and package food. There is already evidence to show that it might also be helpful in the primary production of raw food materials, by way of better delivery of pesticides in the field and better extraction of protein and carbohydrates.

随着世界人口增加，食品生产和加工变得愈发重要。人们担心，具有潜在危害的纳米产品可能会经由肠道进入人体。这些担忧正在得到应对，但更重要的是探讨纳米技术能以何种方式提高食品的营养产出，以及如何加以调整，用于食品保存和包装。已有证据显示，它还可能有助于食品原料的初级生产，方法包括在农田中更有效地施用农药，以及更有效地提取蛋白质和碳水化合物。

GHealthcare is an area where the application of nanotechnology is already exceeding the expectations of ten years ago. It is happening across several fronts: better appreciation of what the ‘natural’ nanoparticles circulating in body fluid do; better methods of delivering drugs to the body via nanoparticles that target the diseased body part; improvements to the diagnostic imaging techniques of ultrasound, magnetic resonance imaging and X-ray computerised tomography. We are now at the threshold of a new subject, ‘theranostics’, whereby nanoparticles will first deliver the diagnostic information to the clinician and then, using the same particle, deliver a form of therapy.

在医疗保健领域，纳米技术的应用已经超出10年前的预期。进展同时出现在几个方面：更深入地了解人体体液中循环的“天然”纳米颗粒有何作用；改进通过纳米颗粒向人体输送药物的方法，使其精准到达患病部位；以及改进超声、磁共振成像和X射线计算机断层扫描等诊断成像技术。我们如今正站在一门新学科“诊疗一体化”（theranostics）的门槛上：纳米颗粒先把诊断信息传递给临床医生，再由同一个颗粒实施某种治疗。

HNanotechnology is a life-changing technology, making the quality of life better, enabling us to make use of our resources more effectively, thereby protecting our environment, and creating new business opportunities. There are many other examples in the world of ‘new materials’ that also make use of nanotechnology. The possibilities may well be limitless.

纳米技术是一项改变生活的技术：它提高生活质量，使我们能够更有效地利用资源，从而保护环境，并创造新的商业机会。“新材料”领域还有许多应用纳米技术的例子。其可能性或许是无限的。
