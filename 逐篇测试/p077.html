Learning from the Romans

向古罗马人学习

How an ancient building material is influencing modern construction

一种古代建筑材料如何影响现代建筑施工

AIn a quest to make concrete more durable and sustainable, an international team of geologists and engineers has found inspiration in the concrete used by the ancient Romans. The chemical secrets of Roman concrete have been uncovered in samples taken from a concrete Roman breakwater. A breakwater is a barrier that is built out into the sea to protect coasts and harbors from the force of waves. This particular breakwater has spent the last 2,000 years submerged in seawater. The international team of researchers that collected the samples was led by Paulo Monteiro, a professor of civil and environmental engineering at the University of California, Berkeley. Analysis of samples from the ancient underwater site in Pozzuoli Bay near Naples in Italy pinpointed why the best Roman concrete was superior to most modern concrete.

为了制造更耐久、更可持续的混凝土，一支由地质学家和工程师组成的国际团队从古罗马人使用的混凝土中获得了灵感。研究人员从一座罗马混凝土防波堤采集样本，揭开了罗马混凝土的化学奥秘。防波堤是一道伸入海中、保护海岸和港口免受海浪冲击的屏障。这座防波堤已经在海水中浸泡了2,000年。采集样本的国际研究团队由加州大学伯克利分校（University of California, Berkeley）土木与环境工程教授 Paulo Monteiro 带领。通过分析意大利那不勒斯附近波佐利湾（Pozzuoli Bay）古代水下遗址的样本，团队明确了最佳罗马混凝土优于大多数现代混凝土的原因。

BConcrete was the Roman Empire’s construction material of choice. It was used in land monuments such as the Pantheon in Rome, as well as in underwater and partially underwater coastal and harbor structures. Monteiro and his team were particularly interested in how the coastal and harbor structures endured the unforgiving saltwater environment. Chemical analysis of Roman concrete showed that it differs from the modern kind in several ways. One is the content of the cement that binds the material in the concrete. The most commonly used cement in recent decades has been Portland cement. Portland cement is a compound of calcium, silicates, and hydrates (C-S-H). However, analysis of Roman concrete shows that it contains a significantly different cement. Roman cement contains aluminum, which is not found in Portland cement, and less silicon than is found in Portland cement. The resulting calcium-aluminum-silicate-hydrate (C-A-S-H) is an exceptionally stable cement.

混凝土是罗马帝国首选的建筑材料。它既用于罗马万神殿（Pantheon）等陆上纪念建筑，也用于水下或部分位于水下的海岸和港口设施。Monteiro 及其团队特别想知道，这些海岸和港口设施如何经受住严酷的海水环境。化学分析表明，罗马混凝土与现代混凝土在几个方面有所不同，其中之一是黏合混凝土中各种材料的水泥成分。近几十年来最常用的是波特兰水泥（Portland cement），它是一种由钙、硅酸盐和水合物组成的化合物，即 C-S-H。然而，对罗马混凝土的分析表明，它所含的水泥明显不同。罗马水泥含有波特兰水泥没有的铝，而且含硅量低于波特兰水泥，由此形成的钙铝硅酸盐水合物，即 C-A-S-H，是一种极为稳定的水泥。

CThe recipe for the contents of Roman concrete was first described around 30 BC by Vitruvius, an engineer for the Roman Emperor Augustus. Volcanic ash was one of the ingredients, and it is now understood that this is crucial, as it is volcanic ash that contains the aluminum that ultimately gives Roman concrete its great durability. The Romans devised an efficient method of making concrete for coastal structures. They combined volcanic ash with lime, which added the calcium to the mix. This was then packed, together with stones and chunks of rock, into wooden molds, which were then immersed in seawater. The seawater instantly triggered a hot chemical reaction. The lime was hydrated by the seawater, which means that it incorporated water molecules into its structure, and reacted with the volcanic ash to cement the whole mixture together. This reaction formed the concrete that was used to build some of the most enduring structures in Western civilization.

公元前30年前后，罗马皇帝 Augustus 的工程师 Vitruvius 首次记述了罗马混凝土的配方。火山灰是其中一种原料；如今人们知道，这一点至关重要，因为火山灰中含有铝，而铝最终赋予罗马混凝土极强的耐久性。罗马人设计了一种高效制造海岸设施混凝土的方法。他们把火山灰与石灰混合，由石灰向混合物中添加钙；再把混合物连同石块和岩石碎块装进木模，浸入海水。海水会立刻触发放热的化学反应。海水使石灰水合，也就是让石灰把水分子纳入自身结构；石灰随后与火山灰反应，把整个混合物胶结在一起。这个反应形成的混凝土，被用于建造西方文明中一些最为经久不衰的设施。

DAccording to Marie Jackson, a member of the research team, Roman concrete is one of the most durable construction materials on the planet, and that was no accident. Shipping was the lifeline of political, economic, and military stability for the Roman Empire, so constructing harbors that would last was critical. ‘Over time, the Romans used less and less concrete.’ ‘As the Roman Empire declined, and shipping declined, the need for the seawater concrete declined,’ said Jackson. ‘You could also argue that the original structures were built so well that, once they were in place, they didn’t need to be replaced,’ she added.

研究团队成员 Marie Jackson 说，罗马混凝土是地球上最耐久的建筑材料之一，而这并非偶然。航运是维系罗马帝国政治、经济和军事稳定的生命线，因此建造经久耐用的港口至关重要。“随着时间推移，罗马人使用的混凝土越来越少。”Jackson 说：“罗马帝国衰落、航运减少后，对海水混凝土的需求也随之下降。”她补充说：“你也可以认为，最初的设施建得实在太好，一经建成便无须更换。”

EProducing Roman concrete also left a smaller carbon footprint than its modern counterpart. ‘It’s not that modern concrete isn’t good—it’s so good we use 19 billion tons of it a year,’ says Monteiro. ‘The problem is that manufacturing Portland cement accounts for seven percent of the carbon dioxide that industry puts into the air.’ Portland cement holds most modern concrete together. However, making Portland cement releases carbon dioxide into the atmosphere from burning fuel in order to heat a mix of limestone and clays to 1,450 degrees Celsius. The production of Roman concrete, however, was much cleaner, as less heat was needed. A temperature that was a third lower than that required for making Portland cement was sufficient for making Roman concrete.

生产罗马混凝土所留下的碳足迹也小于现代混凝土。Monteiro 说：“并不是现代混凝土不好；它太好用了，所以我们每年要使用190亿吨。问题在于，制造波特兰水泥产生的二氧化碳占工业排放到空气中的二氧化碳总量的7%。”大多数现代混凝土都靠波特兰水泥黏合。然而，制造波特兰水泥时，需要燃烧燃料，把石灰石和黏土的混合物加热到1,450摄氏度，这会向大气释放二氧化碳。相比之下，生产罗马混凝土要清洁得多，因为所需热量较少；比制造波特兰水泥所需温度低三分之一，就足以制造罗马混凝土。

FSome modern concrete no longer contains Portland cement; instead, fly ash is used, which causes fewer greenhouse gas emissions. Fly ash is a waste product from burning coal, and the researchers are investigating whether volcanic ash would be a good, large-volume replacement in countries that cannot access fly ash easily. ‘There is not enough fly ash in this world to replace half of the Portland cement being used,’ said Monteiro. ‘Many countries don’t have fly ash, so the idea is to find alternative local materials that will work, including the kind of volcanic ash that Romans used. Using these alternatives could replace 40 percent of the world’s demand for Portland cement.’

有些现代混凝土已不再使用波特兰水泥，而改用温室气体排放较少的粉煤灰（fly ash）。粉煤灰是燃煤产生的废弃物；研究人员正在调查，在难以获得粉煤灰的国家，火山灰能否成为一种优质的大批量替代材料。Monteiro 说：“全世界没有足够的粉煤灰来替代目前使用的半数波特兰水泥。许多国家没有粉煤灰，所以我们的构想是寻找可发挥作用的当地替代材料，包括罗马人使用的那种火山灰。使用这些替代材料，可替代全球需求量中40%的波特兰水泥。”

GCutting greenhouse gas emissions is one powerful incentive for finding a better way to provide the concrete the world needs; another is the need for stronger, longer-lasting buildings, bridges, and other structures. ‘In the middle 20th century, concrete structures were designed to last 50 years, and a lot of them are on borrowed time,’ Monteiro says. ‘Now we design buildings to last 100 to 120 years.’ Yet Roman harbor installations have survived 2,000 years of chemical attack from seawater and wave action. Stronger, longer-lasting modern concrete may be the legacy of a deeper understanding of how the Romans made their incomparable concrete.

减少温室气体排放，是寻找更好方式来供应全球所需混凝土的一项强大动力；另一项动力，则是建造更坚固、更耐久的楼宇、桥梁和其他设施。Monteiro 说：“20世纪中叶，混凝土结构的设计寿命是50年，其中许多如今都在勉强超期服役。现在，我们把建筑的设计寿命定为100至120年。”然而，罗马港口设施经受海水化学侵蚀和海浪冲击已有2,000年。更深入地理解罗马人如何制造出无与伦比的混凝土，或许会为后世留下更坚固、更耐久的现代混凝土。
