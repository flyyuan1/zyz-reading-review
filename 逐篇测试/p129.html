Looking at daily life in ancient Rome

探究古罗马的日常生活

In this preface to a history, the writer explains the factors affecting the scope of his study.

在这篇历史著作的序言中，作者解释了影响其研究范围的因素。

If our ideas on Roman life are not to become lost in confusion, we must study it within a strictly defined time. Nothing changes more rapidly than human customs. Looking at our own more familiar world, apart from the great scientific discoveries of recent centuries which have turned it upside down—steam, electricity, railways, motor cars and aeroplanes, for example—it is clear that the elementary forms of everyday life have been subject to increasing change. Potatoes, for example, were not introduced into Europe until the sixteenth century, coffee was first drunk there in the late sixteenth or early seventeenth, and the banana was used in desserts in Europe at the beginning of the twentieth. The law of change was not less operative in antiquity. It was a commonplace of Roman rhetoric to contrast the crude simplicity of the Republic (509BC–27BC) with the luxury and refinement of the imperial times which followed. There is no common measure, whether of home, or house, or furniture, between ages which are so different.

如果不想让我们对罗马生活的认识陷入混乱，就必须把研究严格限定在某一时期。没有什么比人类习俗变化得更快。看看我们更熟悉的当今世界，且不说近几个世纪令世界发生翻天覆地变化的重大科学发现——例如蒸汽、电力、铁路、汽车和飞机——显而易见，日常生活的基本形式一直在经历越来越多的变化。例如，马铃薯直到16世纪才传入欧洲；咖啡到16世纪末或17世纪初才开始在那里饮用；香蕉则到20世纪初才被用于欧洲的甜点。变化的规律在古代同样发挥作用。罗马修辞中有一个常见说法，就是把共和国时期（公元前509年—公元前27年）的粗朴简约，与随后帝国时代的奢华精致相对照。如此不同的时代之间，无论家庭、住宅还是家具，都没有共同的衡量尺度。

Since a choice of time must necessarily be made, this history will confine itself to studying the generation which was born about the middle of the first century AD, toward the end of the reign of Claudius (41–54AD) or the beginning of the reign of Nero (54–68AD), and which lived on into the reign of Trajan (98–117AD) or of Hadrian (117–138AD). This generation saw the Roman Empire at its most powerful and prosperous. It was witness to the last conquests of the Caesars: the conquest of Dacia, in modern-day eastern Europe, which brought vast mineral wealth into the Empire, and the conquest of Arabia, which helped to bring the riches of India and East Asia flooding into Rome. In the material domain, this generation attained the pinnacle of ancient civilisation.

既然必须选择一个时期，本书便把研究限定在这样一代人：他们大约出生于公元1世纪中叶，即 Claudius 统治末期（公元41—54年）或 Nero 统治初期（公元54—68年），并一直生活到 Trajan（公元98—117年）或 Hadrian（公元117—138年）统治时期。这一代人见证了罗马帝国最强盛、最繁荣的时代，也目睹了罗马皇帝们（Caesars）最后的征服行动：征服位于今天东欧的达契亚（Dacia），为帝国带来巨额矿产财富；征服阿拉伯（Arabia），则推动印度和东亚的财富大量涌入罗马。在物质领域，这一代人达到了古代文明的巅峰。

By a fortunate coincidence—all the more fortunate in that Latin* literature was soon to run nearly dry—this generation is the one whose records combine to offer us the most complete picture of Roman life that we possess. We have a profusion of vivid and picturesque descriptions, precise and colourful, in such works as the Epigrams of Martial, the Satires of Juvenal and the Letters of Pliny. In addition, the Forum of Trajan in Rome itself and the ruins of Herculaneum and Pompeii, the two prosperous resorts buried by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79AD, supply an immense fund of archaeological evidence. Later excavations have also restored to us the ruins of the city of Ostia, which date in the main from the time when the Emperor Hadrian created this great commercial city as a realisation of his town planning ideas. Fortune has favoured the historian of this time.

一个幸运的巧合是，这一代人留下的各类记录共同为我们呈现了现存最完整的罗马生活图景；考虑到拉丁文学*不久后便几近枯竭，这一巧合更显幸运。Martial 的《警句集》（Epigrams）、Juvenal 的《讽刺诗》（Satires）和 Pliny 的《书信集》（Letters）等作品，为我们提供了大量生动鲜明、准确而丰富多彩的描述。此外，罗马城内的图拉真广场（Forum of Trajan），以及赫库兰尼姆（Herculaneum）和庞贝（Pompeii）的遗址，也提供了极其丰富的考古证据；这两座繁荣的度假城镇在公元79年维苏威火山（Mount Vesuvius）喷发时被掩埋。后来的发掘还让奥斯蒂亚（Ostia）城遗址重见天日。遗址主体可追溯至 Hadrian 皇帝按照自己的城市规划理念，把这里建设成一座大型商业城市的时期。幸运眷顾了研究这一时代的历史学家。

* Latin: the language of ancient Rome.

* Latin：拉丁语，古罗马的语言。

It is not enough to focus our study of Roman life only on a fixed time. It would lack foundation and consistency if we did not also focus it in space—in the country or in the town. Even today when the facilities for communication bring something of the city into the smallest and most isolated country cottage, there remains a significant difference between rural existence and the excitement of city life: a much greater gulf separated the peasant from the townsman of antiquity. So large was the inequality between them that, according to the historian Rostovtzeff, it pitted one against the other in a fierce and silent struggle which pierced the wall protecting the Roman privileged classes from the barbarian flood from the north. When the barbarian forces began to invade Roman territory, the peasants decided to fight alongside them.

研究罗马生活，只把范围限定在一个固定时期还不够。如果不同时把空间范围限定在乡村或城镇，研究就会缺乏基础和一致性。即使在今天，交通通信设施已经让最偏远、最与世隔绝的乡间小屋也接触到一些城市生活，乡村生活与令人兴奋的城市生活之间仍有显著差异；而在古代，农民与城市居民之间的鸿沟要大得多。历史学家 Rostovtzeff 认为，两者之间的不平等如此严重，以至于他们展开了一场激烈而无声的对抗，最终冲破了保护罗马上层特权阶级、抵挡北方蛮族洪流的屏障。当蛮族军队开始入侵罗马领土时，农民决定与他们并肩作战。

The townsman, in fact, enjoyed all the goods and resources of the earth. The peasant knew nothing but unending labour without profit, and was unable to enjoy the activities available in even the poorest of cities: the liveliness of the sports field, the warmth of the public baths and the magnificence of public spectacles. In a work on the history of everyday life, we must give up any attempt to blend two such dissimilar pictures into one, and must choose between them. The time which we have chosen to describe day by day is that of those Roman subjects who spent their time exclusively in the town, or rather in The City, Rome, which they regarded as the hub and centre of the universe, proud and wealthy ruler of a world which seemed at that time to have been pacified for ever.

事实上，城市居民享用着大地提供的一切物产和资源。农民只知道无休无止却毫无收益的劳作，连最贫穷城市中可享有的活动也无缘参与：运动场的活力、公共浴场的温暖，以及公共演出的壮观。在一部日常生活史中，我们不能试图把这两幅差异如此巨大的图景合为一体，而必须在二者之间作出选择。我们所选择逐日描写的时代，属于那些只在城镇中生活的罗马臣民；更确切地说，是生活在“罗马城”的人。他们把罗马视为宇宙的枢纽和中心，视为一个当时似乎已永远归于太平的世界中骄傲而富有的统治者。

To perform our task well, we must first try to form an adequate picture of the surroundings in which our subjects lived, and by which their lives were coloured, freeing ourselves from any misconceptions concerning it. We must seek to reconstruct the physical nature of the great city and the social milieu of the various classes of the hierarchy by which it was governed. We must also investigate the moral background of thought and sentiment which can help explain both its strength and its weaknesses. The way in which the Romans of Rome employed their time can only be studied satisfactorily after we have plotted out the main lines of the framework within which they lived and outside of which the routine of their daily life would be more or less unintelligible.

为了完成好这项任务，我们首先必须对研究对象生活的环境形成充分认识；正是这种环境塑造了他们的生活，而我们也要摆脱对它的种种误解。我们必须设法还原这座大城市的物质面貌，以及支配这座城市的等级体系中各个阶层的社会环境。我们还必须考察思想和情感方面的道德背景，因为这有助于解释罗马的强大与弱点。只有先勾勒出罗马人生活框架的主要轮廓，我们才能充分研究罗马城居民如何安排时间；离开这一框架，他们的日常生活规律或多或少会变得难以理解。
