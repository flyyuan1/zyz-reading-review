Australia’s camouflaged creatures

澳大利亚的伪装动物

Many species of animal in Australia protect themselves by using camouflage — a way of ‘hiding’ by blending into the surroundings.

澳大利亚许多动物物种利用伪装来保护自己，也就是通过融入周围环境来“隐藏”自己。

AMost species use camouflage to some extent. If they are convincing, they survive to pass their genes on to future generations. After generations of natural selection, animals can develop astonishingly complex camouflage techniques, manipulating shape, colour and movement. ‘The principle of camouflage is to make it economically unviable for a predator to pursue a particular species of prey,’ explains Professor Mark Elgar of the University of Melbourne. ‘Camouflage increases the search time and, as a consequence, the predator will simply target another species, either because it doesn’t see the camouflaged individual or it just finds something more obvious to do.’

大多数物种都会在一定程度上利用伪装。只要伪装足够逼真，它们就能生存下来，并把基因传给后代。经过一代代自然选择，动物可以发展出极为复杂的伪装技巧，灵活运用体形、颜色和动作。墨尔本大学（University of Melbourne）的 Mark Elgar 教授解释说：“伪装的原则，是让捕食者追逐某一种猎物变得得不偿失。伪装会延长搜寻时间，因此捕食者会直接转向另一物种：要么因为它看不到伪装起来的个体，要么只是因为它发现了更显眼的目标。”

BThe easiest way for an animal to disguise itself is to be invisible in its surroundings. To that end, stick and leaf insects have evolved complex camouflage to hide themselves from predators. Many have the texture of sticks or dry leaves, while others imitate living foliage, even the veins in a leaf. Some insects develop blemishes to match the spots caused by disease. A convincing appearance only works if its owner also acts the part, so during the day the creature using this type of camouflage keeps motionless, or sways like a dead leaf in the breeze. If disturbed, it falls to the ground and stays still. Entomologist Paul Zborowski, who has spent decades photographing inconspicuous creatures, rates the desert insects of Central Australia as the most convincingly disguised creatures he’s seen. ‘It’s an incredibly old habitat, so the creatures have had a long time to adapt,’ Zborowski explains. Most of them behave like stones and don’t move all day, feeding only at night.

动物隐藏自己最简单的办法，就是让自己在周围环境中仿佛不存在。为此，竹节虫（stick insects）和叶䗛（leaf insects）进化出了复杂的伪装，以躲避捕食者。许多种类具有枯枝或干叶般的质感，另一些则模仿鲜活的叶片，甚至连叶脉也惟妙惟肖。有些昆虫还会长出斑痕，以模拟植物因疾病形成的斑点。逼真的外表只有配上相应的动作才有效，因此，采用这类伪装的生物白天会一动不动，或像枯叶一样随风摇摆。一旦受到惊扰，它便掉到地上，保持静止。昆虫学家 Paul Zborowski 数十年来一直拍摄不起眼的生物；在他见过的动物中，澳大利亚中部的沙漠昆虫伪装得最逼真。Zborowski 解释说：“这片栖息地极为古老，因此那里的生物有很长时间来适应环境。”其中大多数像石头一样，整天不动，只在夜间进食。

CA tawny frogmouth sitting motionless on a stump also illustrates the importance of pairing a persuasive costume with behaviour. Professor Gisela Kaplan of the University of New England in Australia says the frogmouth’s skill at camouflaging is learned behaviour. While adopting a pose may be a reflex of the bird, and can be observed in a hatchling’s first week, the ability to choose a backdrop which matches its colouration does not develop for 4–6 months. When the chicks land, they are usually highly conspicuous, and their parents try to signal to them to move to a safe location.

一只静止不动地停在树桩上的茶色蟆口鸱（tawny frogmouth），也说明逼真的外表必须与行为配合。澳大利亚新英格兰大学（University of New England）的 Gisela Kaplan 教授说，蟆口鸱的伪装本领是后天学会的。摆出特定姿势或许是这种鸟的反射行为，雏鸟出生第一周便可观察到；但选择与自身颜色相配背景的能力，要到4至6个月后才会形成。雏鸟落地时通常十分显眼，亲鸟会设法发出信号，让它们转移到安全地点。

DFixed camouflage is only good against a relatively unchanging environment, so some animals, such as the cuttlefish, have evolved an adaptable disguise. The cuttlefish can almost instantly change its colour, pattern and texture to match its surroundings, using specialised cells and muscles. On Queensland’s reefs, scientists have been studying another ocean dweller that uses colour change, although not to blend into the surroundings. Dr Karen Cheney, from the University of Queensland, says the bluestriped fangblenny alters its colouration to mimic other species of fish, allowing it to travel with them and benefit from safety in numbers.

固定不变的伪装只适用于相对稳定的环境，因此乌贼等一些动物进化出了可以调整的伪装。乌贼利用特殊细胞和肌肉，几乎能在瞬间改变颜色、花纹和表面质感，使自己与周围环境相配。在昆士兰（Queensland）的珊瑚礁上，科学家一直在研究另一种会变色的海洋生物，不过它变色并不是为了融入环境。昆士兰大学（University of Queensland）的 Karen Cheney 博士说，蓝纹尖牙鳚（bluestriped fangblenny）会改变体色来模仿其他鱼类，从而与它们一起游动，并借助群体优势提高安全性。

Its most impressive impersonation is of the black-with-neon-blue-striped cleaner wrasse, which eats the parasites on larger fish. Not only does the fangblenny benefit from the reduced predation that comes with the wrasse’s beneficial relationships with other fish, but the disguise also lets it get closer to prey. It darts out from the safety of the wrasse’s cleaning station to nip at unsuspecting fish passing by, but it doesn’t attack those coming to be cleaned.

蓝纹尖牙鳚最令人惊叹的模仿对象，是黑色身体上带有霓虹蓝条纹的清洁隆头鱼（cleaner wrasse）；这种鱼会吃掉大型鱼类身上的寄生虫。尖牙鳚不仅能借清洁隆头鱼与其他鱼类的互利关系降低遭捕食的风险，伪装还让它更容易接近猎物。它会从清洁隆头鱼“清洁站”的安全范围内猛冲出去，咬一口毫无防备的过路鱼，却不会攻击前来接受清洁的鱼。

EThe most famous form of mimicry, however, is for defence, not attack. Batesian mimicry refers to animals that gain protection from predators by imitating a dangerous organism, often using conspicuous colours. The nineteenth-century naturalist Henry Bates first suggested this camouflage technique after noticing that several Amazonian butterfly species looked the same. The technique was later called after him.

不过，最著名的拟态形式是用于防御，而不是攻击。贝氏拟态（Batesian mimicry）是指动物通过模仿危险生物来获得保护，免受捕食者攻击；这种模仿常会采用醒目的颜色。19世纪博物学家 Henry Bates 注意到亚马孙（Amazonian）地区有几种蝴蝶外形相同，因而首次提出这种伪装技巧。后来，这种技巧便以他的名字命名。

In Australian waters, the harmless harlequin snake eel sports the same black and white bandings as the highly toxic yellow-lipped sea krait, ensuring no predator will attack. However, the success of Batesian mimicry depends on the ratio of mimics to originals. If a predator encounters too many that are edible, it will just assume none of the animals with those markings are dangerous, explains Martyn Robinson, an educational naturalist with the Australian Museum.

在澳大利亚水域，无害的花斑蛇鳗（harlequin snake eel）具有与剧毒黄唇海环蛇（yellow-lipped sea krait）相同的黑白环纹，从而确保不会有捕食者发动攻击。然而，贝氏拟态能否成功，取决于模仿者与原型之间的比例。澳大利亚博物馆（Australian Museum）的教育博物学家 Martyn Robinson 解释说，如果捕食者遇到太多可以食用的模仿者，就会直接认定带有这类斑纹的动物都不危险。

FAn imitation of a more dangerous creature needn’t be exact, just enough to make potential predators hesitate. The hawk moth caterpillar has markings resembling a snake’s eyes on its abdomen. When confronted, the caterpillar pulls its head in and the ‘eyes’ flash open. Whether the potential predator thinks it has seen a snake, or is simply startled, is unclear, but the outcome is that the caterpillar lives to see another day.

对更危险生物的模仿不必完全一致，只要足以让潜在捕食者犹豫即可。天蛾幼虫（hawk moth caterpillar）的腹部有酷似蛇眼的斑纹。遇到威胁时，它会缩回头部，“眼睛”图案随即突然显露。潜在捕食者究竟以为自己看见了蛇，还是仅仅受到惊吓，目前尚不清楚；但结果是这只幼虫得以继续活下去。

GIn Queensland’s tablelands, the chameleon gecko has another way of making predators hesitate. Its body is brown, but its tail is banded in black and white. Robinson explains that if attacked, the chameleon gecko will drop off its tail, which will wriggle around on the ground. Many lizards do this, but in the case of the chameleon gecko the tail bones actually rub against one another, so it squeaks. ‘The predator is, of course, thoroughly absorbed by this black-and-white-striped, wriggling, squeaking thing on the ground, and the gecko can sneak away,’ Robinson says. It’s a one-time-only trick: the regrown tail is brown, the same as the gecko’s body.

在昆士兰高原地区，变色龙壁虎（chameleon gecko）还有另一种让捕食者迟疑的办法。它的身体呈棕色，尾巴却带有黑白环纹。Robinson 解释说，变色龙壁虎遇袭时会自行断尾，断下的尾巴会在地上不停扭动。许多蜥蜴都会这样做，但变色龙壁虎尾部的骨骼还会相互摩擦，发出吱吱声。Robinson 说：“捕食者当然会完全被地上这个带黑白条纹、不断扭动又吱吱作响的东西吸引，壁虎便能悄悄逃走。”这一招只能使用一次：重新长出的尾巴是棕色的，与壁虎身体相同。

Such precise disguise and elaborate trickery illustrate the limitless possibilities of nature. But, as Robinson points out, only the most successful illusionists are here to tell the tale.

如此精确的伪装和精巧的骗术，展现了大自然无穷的可能性。不过，正如 Robinson 所指出的，只有最成功的伪装者才能幸存下来。
