How do plants talk to each other

植物如何相互交流

Research shows that plants communicate and interact with each other in surprisingly subtle and sophisticated ways.

研究表明，植物彼此沟通和互动的方式细微而复杂，出人意料。

AIn 1983, plant scientists Jack Schultz and Ian Baldwin reported that the leaves of young maple trees increased their defense systems when exposed to maples that had been damaged by plant-eating (herbivorous) insects. The injured trees, they suggested, were alerting neighbors to the presence of a predator by releasing chemical signals into the air. But the plant research community did not accept this. The results were difficult to replicate, critics pointed out. Many also questioned how it could be evolutionarily stable if it benefited neighboring plants but not the plant releasing the signal. By the late 1980s, most ecologists felt that Schultz and Baldwin’s ideas had been discredited.

1983年，植物科学家 Jack Schultz 和 Ian Baldwin 报告称，幼枫树（maple trees）接触到遭植食性昆虫破坏的枫树后，叶片会增强防御系统。他们推测，受损的树木会向空气中释放化学信号，提醒邻近树木有取食者出现。然而，植物研究界并不接受这一观点。批评者指出，实验结果难以重复。许多人还质疑，如果这一行为只让邻近植物受益，却不给释放信号的植物带来好处，它如何能在进化中稳定存在。到20世纪80年代末，大多数生态学家都认为 Schultz 和 Baldwin 的观点已站不住脚。

BA decade later, however, a number of more carefully designed experiments began to yield convincing indications to the contrary. In 2000, evolutionary ecologist Richard Karban showed that wild tobacco plants became resistant to herbivores when grown in close proximity to sagebrush plants whose leaves had been damaged by cutting. This change appeared to be in response to chemicals – known as volatile organic compounds or VOCs – released by the sagebrush plants. Other researchers soon reported similar VOC-induced defense responses in several other plants, including lima bean, broad bean, barley, and corn. And in 2006, Karban showed that VOCs released by damaged sagebrush induce herbivore resistance in plants growing at distances of up to 60 cm, well within the range of sagebrush neighbors in nature.

然而十年后，一批设计更严谨的实验开始得出令人信服的相反证据。2000年，进化生态学家 Richard Karban 发现，野生烟草（wild tobacco）若生长在叶片被剪伤的蒿属植物（sagebrush）附近，就会产生抵御植食动物的能力。这一变化似乎是由蒿属植物释放的化学物质引起的，这类物质称为挥发性有机化合物（volatile organic compounds，VOCs）。其他研究人员很快也在多种植物中发现了由 VOCs 诱发的类似防御反应，包括利马豆（lima bean）、蚕豆（broad bean）、大麦（barley）和玉米（corn）。2006年，Karban 进一步证明，受损蒿属植物释放的挥发性有机化合物，能使最远60厘米外的植物产生抗植食动物能力；在自然环境中，这完全处于邻近蒿属植物之间通常的距离范围内。

CBut the question still remains: ‘Why should a plant waste valuable resources on a function which has no obvious advantage for it?’ One hypothesis is that external communication channels are merely an extension of within-plant signaling. In sagebrush, lima bean, and poplar, VOCs released from damaged parts of a plant induce resistance in intact sections of the same plant, suggesting that each individual plant uses the signals to coordinate its own physiological responses to protect itself. Karban agrees, saying: ‘The interplant signaling we see may be a result of plants co-opting that process.’ Alternatively, VOC-based signaling between plants may have been favored because it enhances the ‘extended fitness’ of the sender by aiding related plants of the same species: a strategy known as kin selection.

但问题依然存在：“植物为什么要把宝贵资源浪费在一种对自身没有明显好处的功能上？”一种假说认为，对外交流渠道不过是植物内部信号传递的延伸。在蒿属植物、利马豆和杨树（poplar）中，植物受损部位释放的 VOCs，会使同一株植物未受损的部位产生抗性。这提示，每株植物会利用这些信号协调自身的生理反应，从而保护自己。Karban 也认同这一点。他说：“我们所看到的植物间信号传递，可能正是植物借用了这套机制的结果。”另一种可能是，基于 VOCs 的植物间信号之所以在进化中受到青睐，是因为它能帮助同种的亲缘植物，从而提高信号释放者的“扩展适合度”；这种策略称为亲缘选择（kin selection）。

DOver the past few years, a team led by Ariel Novoplansky of Ben-Gurion University of the Negev in Israel seems to have found proof that distress signals can be passed through the plants’ roots. They planted garden pea plants in rows and subjected the first in each row to conditions similar to those experienced in a drought. They then evaluated the response by measuring the microscopic holes on leaves, known as pores, which react when there is a shortage of water. After fifteen minutes, the stressed plant was seen to be closing its pores, followed by all of its neighbors, one by one. Importantly, in a control setup where root contact between neighboring plants was blocked, pores stayed open. Meanwhile, David Johnson’s team at the University of Aberdeen in Scotland have been studying the labyrinths of hair-like fungi that occur around the roots of most plants. These fungi are involved in an important two-way relationship: in exchange for sugars, they provide plants with much-needed phosphorus and nitrogen. Research in which broad bean plants were infested with aphids – small herbivorous insects – revealed that these networks also served as a channel for warning neighboring plants of the infestation.

过去几年，以色列内盖夫本-古里安大学（Ben-Gurion University of the Negev）的 Ariel Novoplansky 领导的团队，似乎找到了植物根部能够传递胁迫信号的证据。他们把豌豆（garden pea）植株种成一排排，并让每排第一株承受类似干旱的环境。随后，研究人员测量叶片上在缺水时会作出反应的微小孔洞，也就是气孔，以评估植株的反应。十五分钟后，受胁迫植株开始关闭气孔，接着邻近植株也一株接一株地关闭气孔。重要的是，在阻断相邻植株根部接触的对照条件下，气孔仍然张开。与此同时，苏格兰阿伯丁大学（University of Aberdeen）的 David Johnson 团队一直在研究大多数植物根部周围迷宫般的丝状真菌网络。这些真菌参与一种重要的双向关系：它们以获得糖分为交换，为植物提供急需的磷和氮。研究人员让蚕豆遭到蚜虫（aphids）这种小型植食性昆虫侵袭，结果发现，这些真菌网络也会成为向邻近植物发出虫害警报的渠道。

EMonica Gagliano of the University of Western Australia believes that plants may even use sounds to alert their neighbors. In one study, she demonstrated that chili plants growing next to fennel plants developed more quickly than seedlings grown with other chili plants. Gagliano and her colleagues suspect the chili plants were compensating for the presence of the fennel, which is known to release chemicals that inhibit the growth of other plants. Remarkably, however, when plant communication pathways – airborne volatiles, root contact, and common fungal networks – were blocked, the results begged for an alternative explanation. ‘We think this other channel of communication might be acoustic,’ says Gagliano. But behavioral ecologist Carel ten Cate of the University of Leiden in the Netherlands points out that taking advantage of such benefits would require sensory mechanisms yet to be described in plants. Despite widespread skepticism, Gagliano has received some encouragement. Richard Karban, for example, is cautiously enthusiastic. ‘Whether or not the explanation she favors is the right one,’ he says, ‘I think that she’s gotten results that, as a field, we need to come to grips with.’

西澳大利亚大学（University of Western Australia）的 Monica Gagliano 认为，植物甚至可能用声音向邻近植物示警。在一项研究中，她证明，生长在茴香（fennel）旁的辣椒（chili）植株，比与其他辣椒一起生长的幼苗发育得更快。Gagliano 和同事推测，辣椒通过加快生长来抵消茴香存在所带来的影响，因为已知茴香会释放抑制其他植物生长的化学物质。但值得注意的是，当植物的各种交流路径——空气传播的挥发物、根部接触和共享真菌网络——都被阻断后，实验结果显然需要另一种解释。Gagliano 说：“我们认为，这另一条交流渠道可能是声音。”不过，荷兰莱顿大学（University of Leiden）的行为生态学家 Carel ten Cate 指出，要利用这种声音带来的好处，植物必须具备某种尚未得到描述的感知机制。尽管外界普遍怀疑，Gagliano 也得到了一些鼓励。例如，Richard Karban 对此持谨慎乐观态度。他说：“无论她所倾向的解释是否正确，我认为她得到的结果，是我们整个领域都必须认真面对的。”

FResearchers believe that knowledge of this phenomenon could eventually be applied to agriculture, and lead to the cultivation of hardier crops. But all agree that there is still much work to be done. ‘Applying our limited knowledge [of plant-communication mechanisms] to agriculture is a big jump,’ says Johnson, ‘but it is definitely on the horizon.’ Nevertheless, one message has emerged loud and clear from those studying this new realm of botanical interaction: despite not possessing eyes, ears, or a nervous system, plants are anything but uncommunicative. ‘When I started my PhD (in the late 1980s), all this stuff was considered very weird,’ recalls Ariel Novoplansky. ‘Today, there is no doubt. We now recognise that plants are capable of some very sophisticated exchanges of information with other plants. This idea is not strange anymore.’

研究人员认为，对这一现象的认识将来可能应用于农业，培育出抗逆性更强的作物。不过，所有人都同意，仍有大量工作要做。Johnson 说：“把我们对植物交流机制的有限认识应用于农业，是一次很大的跨越，但这样的前景肯定已经可以预见。”尽管如此，研究这一植物互动新领域的人已经传达出一个十分明确的信息：植物虽然没有眼睛、耳朵或神经系统，却绝非不会交流。Ariel Novoplansky 回忆道：“20世纪80年代末我开始攻读博士学位时，这一切都被认为非常怪异。如今已经毫无疑问。我们现在认识到，植物能够与其他植物进行一些非常复杂的信息交流。这种想法已经不再奇怪了。”
