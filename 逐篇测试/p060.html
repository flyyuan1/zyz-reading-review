Biophilic Design

亲生物设计

ABiophilic design, a movement related to green architecture, has gained much momentum within the building community in recent years. The premise of biophilic design is to avoid or minimise harmful impacts on the natural environment and, equally importantly, to provide and restore beneficial contacts between people and nature in the built environment.

亲生物设计是一场与绿色建筑相关的运动，近年来在建筑界发展势头强劲。亲生物设计的基本理念，是避免或尽量减少对自然环境的有害影响；同样重要的是，要在人工建成的环境中，促进并恢复人与自然之间有益的接触。

BPeople have a psychologically developed need to commune with nature, but this has frequently been neglected. All too often, architects put creative originality before the needs of the people who must live and work in their futuristic constructions, with scant regard for emotional as well as practical considerations. Biophilic design does not advocate tree houses or cave-dwelling, but it does provide nature-based features that maximise human functioning and health. Though not technically a biophilic design, Fallingwater—the stunning house in rural Pennsylvania designed in the 1930s by Frank Lloyd Wright—arguably speaks to the human soul far more than the box-like blocks of flats designed by his contemporary Le Corbusier.

人类在心理上形成了亲近自然的需求，但这一需求经常受到忽视。建筑师往往把创作上的独创性置于居住和工作在其未来主义建筑中的人们的需求之上，很少顾及情感和实际两方面的考量。亲生物设计并不主张住树屋或洞穴，而是提供以自然为基础的元素，最大限度地改善人的机能和健康。流水别墅（Fallingwater）严格来说并非亲生物设计；这座令人赞叹的住宅位于宾夕法尼亚州（Pennsylvania）乡间，由 Frank Lloyd Wright 于20世纪30年代设计。可以说，与其同时代人 Le Corbusier 设计的盒子般公寓楼相比，它更能触动人的心灵。

CIn the modern world, much of our built environment obstructs the age-old connection between humans and nature. The rate of technological progress far exceeds the rate of psychological evolution, leaving us ill-equipped to cope with our lifestyles, which in turn leads to increased stress. Because biophilia attempts to integrate basic and current needs, it can alleviate the difficulties caused by the brain’s constant efforts to function in a modern environment it has not yet evolved to handle. For example, one crucial element of the natural landscape for human health is sunlight. We are evolutionarily programmed to respond positively to sunny areas over dark or overcast ones and sense that they will foster restoration, improve emotional well-being and promote health.

在现代世界中，大量人工建成的环境阻隔了人与自然之间古老的联系。技术进步的速度远远超过心理进化的速度，使我们缺乏应对自身生活方式的能力，进而造成更大压力。亲生物理念试图把人的基本需求与当下需求结合起来，因此可以缓解大脑在尚未进化到足以适应的现代环境中不断努力运作所带来的困难。例如，自然景观中对人类健康至关重要的一个因素是阳光。进化使我们天生更喜欢阳光充足的地方，而不是阴暗或阴云密布之处；我们会感觉前者有助于恢复身心、改善情绪健康并促进身体健康。

DBecause of its tremendous impact on human psychology, biophilic design plays a vital role in healthcare and its delivery. The current healthcare system contains many flaws, especially in its physical spaces. Hospitals, clinics and offices are high-stress environments for patients, visitors and healthcare professionals alike. One well-known study looked at the impact of nature on patients after surgery. One group had a view of a tree; the others had a window looking onto a brick wall. The first group had shorter hospital stays, received fewer negative comments from nurses, required fewer analgesics and had slightly fewer post-operative complications.

亲生物设计对人的心理有巨大影响，因此在医疗保健及相关服务中发挥着重要作用。当前的医疗体系存在许多缺陷，物理空间方面尤其如此。对患者、访客和医护人员来说，医院、诊所和诊室都是压力很大的环境。一项著名研究考察了自然景观对术后患者的影响。一组患者能看见一棵树，另一组患者的窗外则是一堵砖墙。第一组住院时间更短，护士对他们的负面评价更少，所需止痛药更少，术后并发症也略少。

EAs a consequence of this and other studies, nature and nature-based design have been integrated into the physical design of many hospitals. For example, Dartmouth-Hitchcock Medical Center (DHMC) in New Hampshire boasts an atrium design illuminating the entire facility. Natural elements also permeate the building, including wood, stone and numerous live plants. While DHMC was built to incorporate these qualities, other hospitals have had biophilic features added to existing structures. The application of biophilic concepts to hospital interiors has increased substantially as administrators have witnessed patients’ positive responses to nature.

这项研究和其他研究促使自然元素及以自然为基础的设计融入许多医院的空间设计。例如，新罕布什尔州（New Hampshire）的达特茅斯—希区柯克医疗中心（Dartmouth-Hitchcock Medical Center，DHMC）拥有一个能让整座设施充满光线的中庭。木材、石材和大量活体植物等自然元素也遍布楼内。DHMC 从建造之初就纳入了这些特点，另一些医院则在既有建筑中增添亲生物元素。管理人员亲眼看到患者对自然的积极反应后，亲生物理念在医院室内设计中的应用大幅增加。

FMany existing buildings contain biophilic elements, but only a few have been built with the specific idea of biophilic design in mind. One such building is the Adam Joseph Lewis Center for Environmental Studies at Oberlin College in Ohio. The director of Oberlin’s Environmental Studies Program, David Orr, explained that the building’s goals were “to create not just a place for classes but rather a building that would help to redefine the relationship between humankind and the environment—one that would expand our sense of ecological possibilities”. The Lewis Center is sustainable in a broader sense than in the typical application of the word. It harnesses solar power, utilises both active and passive air systems, and monitors the weather to adapt to conditions. The Centre’s “Living Machine” treats wastewater by combining traditional wastewater technology with wetland ecosystems’ purification processes, producing water that can be used in the toilets and for irrigation. In their design, Orr and his team of architects engineered a healthy and comfortable space for students while ensuring the surrounding environment was undamaged.

许多现有建筑都含有亲生物元素，但在建造时明确以亲生物设计为理念的建筑为数不多。俄亥俄州（Ohio）欧柏林学院（Oberlin College）的 Adam Joseph Lewis 环境研究中心（Adam Joseph Lewis Center for Environmental Studies）就是其中之一。欧柏林环境研究项目（Oberlin’s Environmental Studies Program）主任 David Orr 解释说，这座建筑的目标是“创造的不只是上课的场所，而是一座有助于重新界定人类与环境关系的建筑——一座能够拓展我们对生态可能性认识的建筑”。Lewis 中心所体现的可持续性，比这个词通常所指的范围更广。它利用太阳能，采用主动式和被动式空气调节系统，并监测天气以适应不同条件。中心的“生命机器”（Living Machine）把传统污水处理技术与湿地生态系统的净化过程结合起来处理废水，所得水可用于冲洗厕所和灌溉。Orr 和建筑师团队在设计中为学生打造了健康舒适的空间，同时确保周围环境不受损害。

GAnother example is the University of Guelph-Humber building in Ontario, Canada. It contains a centrally located bio-wall, vertically spanning the building. The wall is covered in dense foliage which not only connects people to nature on the inside of the building, but also functions as a new filtration-system prototype. The wall purifies the air and has the potential to fulfil the building’s fresh-air-intake requirements. These examples prove that the built environment need not interfere with biological human needs to commune with nature, nor with existing ecological systems.

另一个例子是位于加拿大安大略省（Ontario）的圭尔夫—汉伯大学（University of Guelph-Humber）大楼。楼内中央设有一面纵向贯穿建筑的生态墙（bio-wall）。墙面覆盖着茂密植物，不仅让建筑内部的人与自然相连，还充当一种新型过滤系统的原型。这面墙能够净化空气，并有可能满足整栋建筑的新鲜空气进气需求。这些实例证明，人工建成的环境不必妨碍人类亲近自然的生物需求，也不必干扰现有生态系统。

HAncient architects built for their cultures, which were almost always more in touch with the earth than Western society is today. They mimicked nature’s forms, producing magnificent structures that still awe us—though biophilic design is a novel concept, they certainly employed some of its recommendations. Today we can add another layer to this tradition and ensure maximal benefit for our planet and ourselves.

古代建筑师为各自文化建造建筑，而那些文化几乎总是比今天的西方社会更贴近大地。他们模仿自然的形态，造出至今仍令我们惊叹的宏伟建筑。尽管亲生物设计是一个新概念，他们显然已经运用了其中一些建议。今天，我们可以在这一传统的基础上进一步发展，确保地球和我们自己都获得最大益处。
