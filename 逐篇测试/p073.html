Intelligent behaviour in birds

鸟类的智能行为

Many people are aware of the intelligence of chimpanzees and other mammals. However, birds also demonstrate intelligent behaviour.

许多人知道黑猩猩和其他哺乳动物很聪明。不过，鸟类也会展现智能行为。

AFor centuries, many scholars maintained that humans were the only intelligent organisms on Earth. Many traits were considered to be exclusively human examples of acumen – for example, language, tool use, deception, and awareness of self and others. However, exciting new research on a number of animals, particularly birds, has called into question the uniqueness of these traits, forcing us to reconsider this opinion. In 1964, people were amazed when naturalist Jane Goodall first discovered chimpanzees making and using tools. But ornithologists, people who study birds, were not overly surprised. Almost 20 years earlier, a renowned ornithologist had shown that tool use was commonplace in populations of woodpecker finches residing on the Galápagos Islands. These tiny birds routinely used twigs to extract grubs from under bark.

几个世纪以来，许多学者一直坚称，人类是地球上唯一具有智能的生物。语言、使用工具、欺骗，以及认识自己和他人等许多特征，都曾被视为人类才有的才智表现。然而，针对多种动物，尤其是鸟类开展的令人振奋的新研究，对这些特征是否为人类独有提出了质疑，迫使我们重新审视这一看法。1964年，博物学家 Jane Goodall 首次发现黑猩猩会制作并使用工具，令世人大为惊讶。但研究鸟类的鸟类学家并没有特别吃惊。早在近20年前，一位著名鸟类学家就已证明，生活在加拉帕戈斯群岛（Galápagos Islands）的啄木鸟雀（woodpecker finches）种群普遍会使用工具。这些小鸟经常用细枝从树皮下掏出幼虫。

BSince then, the catalogue of tool-using animals has grown. At least three Australian bird species make tools similar to those of the woodpecker finch, and when white-winged choughs come across shellfish, they have been known to use rocks as hammers to crack open the recalcitrant shells. Other birds show a more sophisticated level of insight. For example, black kites have been reported dropping bait into lakes to bring fish to the surface of the water, thereby making them easier to catch. A kite may also pick up a smouldering stick from an area recently burned by a bushfire and drop the stick on a patch of unburned grass. The bird then feasts on the small animals that flee from the subsequent fire.

此后，会使用工具的动物名单不断增加。澳大利亚至少有三种鸟会制作与啄木鸟雀所用工具相似的工具；人们还发现，白翅澳鸦（white-winged choughs）遇到贝类时，会把石头当作锤子，敲开难以破开的外壳。其他鸟类展现出更高层次的洞察力。例如，据报道，黑鸢（black kites）会把诱饵投入湖中，引鱼游到水面，从而更容易捕捉。黑鸢还可能从刚遭林火焚烧的区域捡起一根仍在阴燃的树枝，把它扔到一片尚未燃烧的草地上。随后，它便捕食那些逃离新燃起的火的小动物。

CMost tool-using behaviours are a means of extracting food, which may provide a clue as to how the mental abilities needed for tool use evolved. The predominant explanation is based on the proverb that ‘necessity is the mother of invention’. Essentially, brain tissue is energetically expensive, so animals should have evolved only the intellectual capabilities required to overcome the challenges they face in their environment. Consider a hypothetical duck grazing on a seemingly endless supply of grass. Being particularly intelligent will not help the duck eat more grass. In contrast, other species, such as birds of prey, live in a more challenging environment, where food may be distributed erratically, hidden from view or highly mobile. The food itself may be quite intelligent. So, if there are not enough resources to feed all individuals, then only the smartest in each generation will live and reproduce.

大多数使用工具的行为都是获取食物的手段，这或许能提示我们：使用工具所需的心智能力是如何演化出来的。主流解释以“需要是发明之母”这句谚语为基础。简言之，脑组织耗能很高，因此动物应当只演化出克服自身环境挑战所需的智力。不妨设想一只鸭子，在看似取之不尽的草地上吃草。格外聪明并不能帮助它吃到更多草。相比之下，猛禽等其他物种生活在更具挑战的环境中，那里的食物可能分布不定、藏而不露，或活动性很强，猎物本身也可能相当聪明。因此，如果资源不足以养活所有个体，那么每一代中只有最聪明的个体才能存活并繁殖。

DNew Caledonian crows boast many different tools in their toolkit. They use a hooked tool made by removing all but one of the side branches from a twig. They fashion serrated rakes (using their beaks as scissors) from stiff, leathery pandanus leaves. They also make probes by modifying their own moulted feathers. Each tool is used in slightly different ways to pull grubs from deep within tree trunks. The crows carry their favourite tool from one foraging site to the next. They also store their tools for later reuse in a secure place on their perch. Problem-solving abilities have traditionally been thought to be beyond the reach of animals. Nevertheless, birds are coming up with innovative solutions all the time. Recently, New Caledonian crows were observed moulding a piece of wire, something they had never seen before, into a hook and then using it to retrieve food.

新喀里多尼亚乌鸦（New Caledonian crows）的“工具箱”里有许多不同工具。它们会把细枝上除一根侧枝外的其余侧枝全部去掉，制成带钩工具；会用喙充当剪刀，将坚韧如革的露兜树（pandanus）叶制成锯齿耙；还会改造自己脱落的羽毛，做成探针。每种工具的用法略有不同，都是为了从树干深处掏出幼虫。乌鸦会把最喜欢的工具从一个觅食点带到下一个，还会把工具存放在栖木上的安全位置，留待日后再用。传统上，人们认为动物不具备解决问题的能力。尽管如此，鸟类一直在想出创新办法。最近，人们观察到新喀里多尼亚乌鸦把一段从未见过的金属丝弯成钩子，再用它取出食物。

ELiterally hundreds of such reports have accumulated in back copies of scientific journals. Recently, a team of biologists from McGill University in Canada collated them and compared the frequency and size of innovations with the size of the birds’ forebrain (the brain area responsible for higher-order information processing) relative to the hindbrain. The team uncovered a clear relationship: birds with relatively large forebrains are able to invent fresh solutions to ecological challenges, and to exploit the discoveries and inventions of others, more often than birds with relatively small forebrains.

科学期刊的往期刊物中，已经积累了足足数百篇这类报告。最近，加拿大麦吉尔大学（McGill University）的一支生物学家团队汇总了这些报告，并将创新行为的频率和幅度，与鸟类前脑相对于后脑的大小进行比较；前脑是负责高阶信息处理的脑区。团队发现了明确的关系：与前脑相对较小的鸟相比，前脑相对较大的鸟更常为生态挑战想出新办法，也更常利用其他鸟的发现和发明。

FIntelligence in birds may also arise as a result of selection to overcome the dynamic challenges of communal living. Since this involves competition between group members, to be successful a social animal may need to be able to reflect on its own intentions, as well as those of others. The consequence of being part of a community may be the evolution of a distinctly ‘political’ brain.

鸟类的智能也可能源于自然选择，因为它们需要克服群居生活中不断变化的挑战。群居涉及成员之间的竞争，因此社会性动物若想取得成功，可能既要能思考自己的意图，也要能揣摩其他成员的意图。加入一个群体，可能促使它们演化出一种鲜明的“政治型”大脑。

GWhat better way to exercise a political brain than to be deceitful! Perhaps the best example of deception among birds comes from the white-winged choughs. Choughs are cooperative breeders – that is, they form a communal group consisting of one breeding pair and up to 15 non-breeding ‘helpers’. However, because young choughs have so little enthusiasm for foraging, or gathering food, they are often too hungry to help. And because it is socially unacceptable to be part of a group and provide little help, young choughs often act deceptively. For example, when an adult is watching, a young chough will place some food in the mouth of a hungry chick but not release it. Instead, it waits until the adult departs and then eats the food itself. A chough can also help the group by preening the chicks. Interestingly, it is more likely to preen the chicks if another bird can see it do so. A chough that has been sitting totally still on the nest while the rest of the group is foraging out of sight will comically spring up and frantically start to preen the chicks as soon as some of its group members come into view. It is likely that these young choughs are only motivated to help when others are watching because they are concerned about their social status. Choughs need other choughs to like them, as they cannot breed without them.

还有什么比欺骗更能锻炼“政治型”大脑呢！鸟类欺骗行为的最佳例证或许来自白翅澳鸦。白翅澳鸦实行合作繁殖，也就是说，会组成一个群体，其中包括一对繁殖鸟和最多15只不参与繁殖的“帮手”。然而，幼年白翅澳鸦很不热衷于觅食，也就是搜集食物，常常因为太饿而无力帮忙。身处群体却几乎不出力又不为群体所接受，于是幼年白翅澳鸦常常弄虚作假。例如，有成鸟看着时，幼年白翅澳鸦会把食物放进饥饿雏鸟嘴里，却不松口；它会等成鸟离开，再自己吃掉食物。白翅澳鸦也可以通过替雏鸟理羽来帮助群体。有趣的是，若有另一只鸟能看到，它替雏鸟理羽的可能性就更高。当群体其余成员都在视线之外觅食时，一只始终静坐巢中的白翅澳鸦，只要看见某些同伴出现，就会滑稽地一下子跳起来，疯狂地替雏鸟理羽。这些幼年白翅澳鸦很可能只有在其他鸟注视时才有动力帮忙，因为它们在意自己的社会地位。白翅澳鸦需要得到同伴的喜爱，因为离开同伴，它们就无法繁殖。
