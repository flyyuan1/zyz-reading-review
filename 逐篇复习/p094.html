AInvasive species are among the leading threats to the native wildlife of most countries, with approximately 42 percent of endangered species at risk from them. Invasive species can be any kind of organism – for example, a mammal, amphibian, fish, insect or plant – that is not native to an ecosystem. Often they reproduce and spread with great speed. Contrary to popular belief, a plant or animal classified as an invasive species does not necessarily originate in another country. For example, lake trout are native to the Great Lakes of North America, but are considered to be an invasive species in Yellowstone Lake in Wyoming because they compete with native cutthroat trout for habitat.

入侵物种是威胁大多数国家本地野生生物的主要因素之一，约有42%的濒危物种因此面临风险。入侵物种可以是非某一生态系统原生的任何一种生物，例如哺乳动物、两栖动物、鱼类、昆虫或植物。它们往往繁殖、扩散得非常迅速。与大众看法相反，被归为入侵物种的动植物并不一定来自另一个国家。例如，湖鳟（lake trout）原生于北美五大湖（Great Lakes），但在怀俄明州（Wyoming）的黄石湖（Yellowstone Lake）却被视为入侵物种，因为它们会与本地割喉鳟（cutthroat trout）争夺栖息地。

BWhen a new species is introduced into an ecosystem, native wildlife may struggle to compete with it for food and other resources. Invasive species can change the food web in an ecosystem by destroying or replacing native food sources, while providing little or no food for local wildlife. In extreme cases, the invader may prey on the native species. Above all, invasive species threaten biodiversity in many habitats. For instance, the climbing plant species kudzu (which is native to East Asia) can easily replace a habitat that had a wide range of plants with a monoculture consisting solely of kudzu, as has started to happen in the south-east of the USA.

新物种被引入一个生态系统后，本地野生生物可能难以与其争夺食物和其他资源。入侵物种会破坏或取代本地食物来源，却很少或完全不能为当地野生生物提供食物，从而改变生态系统中的食物网。极端情况下，入侵者还可能捕食本地物种。最重要的是，入侵物种威胁着许多栖息地的生物多样性。例如，葛藤（kudzu）原生于东亚；这种攀缘植物很容易取代原本拥有多种植物的栖息地，使那里变成只有葛藤的单一物种群落，而美国东南部已经开始出现这种情况。

CThe phenomenon is not an exclusively modern one. Humans have always transferred a variety of species from one region to another, but the development of rapid means of transportation has increased the frequency of such introductions. Aquatic organisms can be shipped across the oceans, while insects can easily get into the wooden crates that are transported around the world in this way. In addition, climate change has enabled some invasive plant species to colonise new areas. Other invasive species include pets such as snakes or turtles which are intentionally set free into the wild.

这种现象并非现代社会所独有。人类一直会把各种物种从一个地区带到另一个地区，但快速交通工具的发展增加了这类引入的频率。水生生物可以随船跨越海洋，昆虫也很容易钻进以这种方式运往世界各地的木箱。此外，气候变化使一些入侵植物得以在新地区繁衍。其他入侵物种还包括蛇或龟等宠物，它们被人有意放到野外。

DInvasive species do not all present the same level of threat to native ecosystems and can be classified into three types. The first of these may be introduced species which can maintain themselves in a limited range of habitats without upsetting the ecological equilibrium of the area. Some scientists have even argued that in these cases the introduction enhances the diversity of animal and plant life in that specific area. A second group of invaders present a greater threat because their spread is at the expense of one native species. The North American grey squirrel, for example, was introduced to the UK in 1876 largely because wealthy landowners thought it would be a fashionable and attractive addition to the local wildlife on their estates. It spread widely, leading directly to the dramatic decline in the population of the native red squirrel. However, it would appear that this is the only definite negative impact of the grey squirrel.

入侵物种对本地生态系统造成的威胁程度并不相同，可以分为3类。第一类可能是引入物种，它们能够在有限的几类栖息地中维持种群，而不破坏当地的生态平衡。一些科学家甚至认为，在这种情况下，引入物种会提高特定地区动植物的多样性。第二类入侵者威胁更大，因为它们的扩散以牺牲一种本地物种为代价。例如，北美灰松鼠（North American grey squirrel）于1876年被引入英国，主要原因是富有的土地所有者认为，让庄园里多出这种野生动物既时髦又赏心悦目。灰松鼠广泛扩散，直接导致本地红松鼠（red squirrel）数量急剧下降。不过，看起来这就是灰松鼠唯一可以确定的负面影响。

EThere is a third level of threat in which the dominance of the introduced species has an extremely destructive effect on the entire ecosystem. One of the most damaging examples involved a species of comb jellyfish. Native to estuaries along the western Atlantic coast from the northern United States to the Valdés Peninsula in Argentina, this species was released from a ship into the Black Sea in Eastern Europe in 1982, almost certainly by accident. The Black Sea has levels of industrial waste which are by international standards exceptionally high. Despite this, fishing boats were still able to catch healthy numbers of fish. But when the invading jellyfish underwent a population explosion in the space of just six years, the entire marine ecosystem was transformed, and fish numbers declined dramatically because they were in competition with the jellyfish, preying on exactly the same microscopic creatures. The jellyfish had a more serious impact on the ecosystem than the heavily polluted water.

第三个威胁等级是，引入物种占据主导地位，对整个生态系统产生破坏性极强的影响。最具破坏性的例子之一涉及一种栉水母（comb jellyfish）。这一物种原生于大西洋西岸的河口，分布范围从美国北部延伸到阿根廷瓦尔德斯半岛（Valdés Peninsula）。1982年，它被一艘船排入东欧的黑海（Black Sea），几乎可以肯定是意外所致。按照国际标准，黑海的工业废物水平高得异常。尽管如此，渔船仍能捕到数量可观的鱼。但入侵栉水母的种群仅在6年内便暴增，整个海洋生态系统随之改变，鱼类数量急剧下降，因为鱼类与栉水母争食完全相同的微小生物。栉水母对生态系统的影响，甚至比污染严重的海水更大。

FMore than a century after its introduction outside its native range on the Amazon River in South America, a plant known as a water hyacinth can be found in tropical lakes, streams, and rivers around the world. Its beauty attracted botanists seeking exotic plants for botanical gardens and they imported it to a horticultural exhibition in New Orleans in 1884. Visitors were so impressed they planted it in many locations during the 1880s and 1890s, resulting in the aquatic ecosystems of the south-eastern United States being progressively colonised by vast, floating, dense carpets of water hyacinth. Today it is present around the globe, damaging boat engines and even blocking cooling pipes for power plants, occasionally leading to massive blackouts. The impact of the water hyacinth on native water plants is largely unstudied, as is unfortunately the case for most ecosystems invaded by new plant species.

水葫芦（water hyacinth）原生于南美洲亚马孙河（Amazon River）；自被引入原生范围以外的地区以来，已过去100多年，如今世界各地的热带湖泊、溪流和河流中都能见到这种植物。它的美丽吸引了为植物园搜寻异域植物的植物学家，他们于1884年把它带到新奥尔良（New Orleans）的一场园艺展览。参观者对它印象极深，于是纷纷在19世纪80和90年代把水葫芦种到许多地方。结果，美国东南部的水生生态系统逐渐被大片漂浮、浓密的水葫芦覆盖。如今，它已分布全球，不仅会损坏船只发动机，甚至会堵塞发电厂的冷却管道，有时造成大规模停电。水葫芦对本地水生植物的影响尚缺乏研究；遗憾的是，在大多数受到新植物物种入侵的生态系统中，情况也是如此。

GIn the United States, more than 7,000 introduced species have established themselves, of which at least 15 percent cause ecological damage. As the number of invasive species expands, legislation to deal with this problem is rare or non-existent in the majority of countries. Unfortunately, ordinary people outside the scientific community have a very limited understanding of the threat posed by invasive species, which means that other environmental threats receive considerably more media attention. The introduction of new species can initially seem highly desirable, but the full extent of their impact is consistently underestimated.

美国已有7000多个引入物种建立了稳定种群，其中至少15%会造成生态破坏。入侵物种数量不断增加，但大多数国家很少制定、甚至完全没有制定应对这一问题的法律。遗憾的是，科学界以外的普通人对入侵物种所构成的威胁了解十分有限，这意味着其他环境威胁得到的媒体关注要多得多。引入新物种最初可能显得十分可取，但人们总是低估其影响的全部程度。

HAlthough ultimately measures need to be taken at an international level, limited action is possible by individuals. One way is for people to plant native plants in their gardens rather than species from abroad. It is also useful to learn to identify invasive species, and report any sightings to wildlife organisations. Regularly cleaning clothing, boots, boats, tyres, and any other equipment regularly used outdoors can remove insects and plant parts that may introduce invasive species into new locations.

尽管最终必须在国际层面采取措施，个人仍可以开展一些有限行动。一种办法是在花园中种植本地植物，而不是外国物种。学会识别入侵物种，并把任何目击情况报告给野生动物组织，也很有用。定期清洁衣物、靴子、船只、轮胎及其他经常在户外使用的设备，可以清除可能把入侵物种带到新地点的昆虫和植物碎片。
