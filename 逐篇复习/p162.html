AWhen Simon Baron-Cohen recently published a book, The Essential Difference, in which he claimed that the female brain is predominantly hard-wired for empathy, the male brain is predominantly hard-wired for understanding and building systems, he caught the mood of the moment. His team's experiments on day-old babies, and the finding that the male babies looked for longer at a hanging toy and the female babies longer at a face, have been repeatedly cited in the press since his book's publication. However, Elizabeth Spelke is one expert who is sceptical about whether this experiment deserves such attention. This is one isolated experiment, she says. 'Its findings fly in the face of dozens of studies on similar aspects of cognition carried out on young babies over decades. It is astonishing how much this one study has been cited, when the many studies that show no difference between the sexes, or a difference in the other direction, are ignored.'

Simon Baron-Cohen 最近出版了《本质差异》（The Essential Difference）一书，声称女性大脑天生主要倾向于共情，男性大脑则天生主要倾向于理解和构建系统；这一观点正好迎合了当时的社会情绪。他的团队以出生仅一天的婴儿为对象进行实验，发现男婴注视悬挂玩具的时间更长，女婴注视人脸的时间更长。自该书出版以来，这些实验及其发现不断被媒体引用。然而，专家 Elizabeth Spelke 怀疑这项实验是否值得受到如此关注。她说，这只是一项孤立的实验。“它的发现与几十年来针对幼儿认知相似方面所做的数十项研究背道而驰。令人惊讶的是，这一项研究被引用了这么多次，而那些显示两性没有差异，或显示差异方向相反的众多研究却遭到忽视。”

BOne of Baron-Cohen's other main studies was conducted by collecting and examining responses to questionnaires completed by adults on their own behaviour: the sort of quiz well known to readers of magazines. The answers recorded in such cases will reflect differences formed by cultural assumptions and socialisation: it is hard to see what they tell us about innate differences. Nevertheless, they have become a fashionable reference point, and recently Princess Anne, patron of Women into Science and Engineering, gave a speech in which she quoted Baron-Cohen's research as proof that six out of ten men have a systemising brain. This kind of science has passed into everyday culture, often outrunning the intentions of those who did the original work.

Baron-Cohen 的另一项主要研究，是收集并分析成年人就自身行为填写的问卷答案；这类测验为杂志读者所熟知。在这种情况下，记录下来的答案会反映由文化假设和社会化过程形成的差异，很难看出它们能告诉我们什么有关先天差异的信息。尽管如此，这些研究已经成为时髦的参考依据。最近，“女性投身科学与工程”组织的赞助人 Anne 公主发表演讲，引用 Baron-Cohen 的研究来证明，每10名男性中有6人的大脑偏向系统化思维。这类科学已经进入日常文化，而且其传播常常超出了原研究者的本意。

CSocial expectations play heavily with young men and women, and expectations are in some ways more conventional in Britain today than they were ten or 20 years ago. It is particularly when they have children that women find out how society reinforces traditional expectations of their behaviour. They discover that many pressures - including the work schedule of their male partner and the expectations of his employer that it should be maintained - make it hard to combine work and home without giving their paid work a lower priority than their male colleagues do. In such a context, the claims about males' greater power to systemise and compete only serve to put further pressure on women.

社会期待对年轻男女产生着强大影响，而且从某些方面看，当今英国的社会期待比10年或20年前更加传统。女性尤其是在有了孩子后，才会发现社会如何强化对她们行为的传统期待。她们发现，许多压力使自己很难兼顾工作与家庭，同时又像男性同事那样重视有报酬的工作；这些压力包括男性伴侣的工作安排，以及其雇主要求维持这一安排的期待。在这样的背景下，有关男性更善于系统化思考和竞争的说法，只会给女性增加更多压力。

DThe pressures are equally powerful on men to continue in a traditional lifestyle. Some educationalists argue that among young men the concern that they might appear 'feminine' is higher than ever and may even hold back educational achievement, since studying is often seen as girlish. In later life, the drive to competitiveness in the workplace is mythologised as being tied to high levels of masculinity. Men are also told that high achievers attract more women, even though evidence shows that as women slowly become more equal at work, they begin to care less about the economic status of potential partners.

要求男性延续传统生活方式的压力同样强大。一些教育工作者认为，年轻男性如今比以往任何时候都更担心自己显得“女性化”，这甚至可能阻碍学业成就，因为学习常被视为像女孩子做的事。步入成年后，职场上追求竞争的动力又被神话为与高度的男子气概相联系。男性还被告知，成就越高就越能吸引女性；然而证据显示，随着女性在职场中逐渐变得更加平等，她们开始不那么看重潜在伴侣的经济地位。

EMen are often held back from making the choice to move deeper into domestic life, and in particular childcare, feeling that it lacks status. While the gains in making a social revolution were clear for women - they could gain status and income by moving beyond the home - the gains in the parallel social revolution are harder for men to see, since it will involve them in losing status and income. The idea that men are naturally less empathetic also produces feelings of fatalism about their role. Yet there is much evidence to show that even being empathetic and good at parenting is a learned behaviour.

男性往往会因为觉得家庭生活缺乏地位，而在选择更深入地投入家庭、尤其是照料孩子时受到阻碍。社会革命给女性带来的好处很明确——走出家庭可以让她们获得地位和收入；但男性较难看出与之对应的社会革命能带来什么好处，因为这会让他们失去地位和收入。男性天生较缺乏共情能力这一观念，也会让他们对自己的角色产生宿命感。然而，大量证据表明，即使是善于共情和育儿，也都是后天习得的行为。

FIt is true that, as the linguist Steven Pinker noted, in our culture women respond more readily to their children's cries, but there is nothing to say that this is down to anything other than practice and socialisation. In a culture where most men return to work straight after the birth of their children, it would be extraordinary if they did manage to remain as alert to their needs as women who remain at home. In one test carried out with teenagers, although girls said that they liked children more than boys said they did, when left alone with a crying infant, their responses were no different. Women can testify to the fact that parenting is a tricky, complicated process, not always a natural, easy role.

正如语言学家 Steven Pinker 指出的那样，在我们的文化中，女性确实更容易对孩子的哭声作出反应，但没有证据表明，这一差异还源于练习和社会化之外的其他因素。在一种多数男性会在孩子出生后立即重返工作的文化中，如果他们仍能像留在家中的女性一样敏锐地察觉孩子的需要，那才会令人惊讶。一项以青少年为对象的测试发现，虽然女孩自称比男孩更喜欢孩子，但当他们被单独留下照顾一名哭泣的婴儿时，男女的反应并无不同。女性可以证明，育儿是一个棘手而复杂的过程，并不总是一种自然、轻松的角色。

GHowever, while the definite trend of a decade ago is no longer evident, the movement towards equality has not completely stalled. A more equal sharing of childcare and even domestic labour is still desired by many men as well as women. Nearly three-quarters of young British men say that if it were financially possible they would like to stay at home to look after their families. And three-quarters of young men tell pollsters that the amount of time fathers are allowed to take off work after a birth - paternity leave - is too short in Britain. The government is therefore proposing more equal policies on maternity/paternity leave, knowing that the pressure is still forwards rather than backwards. And remember that in Britain, young men in full-time work spend about an hour a day looking after their children. That may sound paltry, but not compared to the 15 minutes they spent 30 years ago: if the time went on increasing fourfold every 30 years, equal partnership would become a reality for their grandchildren.

不过，尽管10年前那种明确趋势已不再明显，走向平等的进程并未完全停滞。许多男性和女性仍希望更平等地分担育儿乃至家务。近四分之三的英国年轻男性表示，如果经济条件允许，他们愿意留在家中照料家人。另有四分之三的年轻男性向民意调查人员表示，在英国，孩子出生后父亲获准休假的时间——即陪产假——太短。因此，政府正提议制定更为平等的产假和陪产假政策，因为它知道推动变化的压力依然指向前进，而不是倒退。还要记得，在英国，全职工作的年轻男性每天约花1小时照看孩子。这听起来或许少得可怜，但与30年前的15分钟相比并非如此：如果照看孩子的时间每30年都继续增加到原来的4倍，那么到了他们的孙辈，平等的伴侣关系就会成为现实。
