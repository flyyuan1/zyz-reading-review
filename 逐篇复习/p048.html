Izon is a New Zealand company specialising in nanoscience – the study of atoms, molecules and other very small pieces of matter.

Izon是一家专门从事纳米科学的新西兰公司；纳米科学研究原子、分子和其他极微小的物质单元。

The new US headquarters of Izon are situated in Cambridge, Massachusetts, one of the most important global centres of scientific research and technology. Half the other offices in the building Izon shares have scientific names like Stemgent, or the word ‘pharmaceutical’ in the name. In the past three years, Izon has expanded to 17 times its previous size. It’s done so selling only one product that it’s been developing since 2004, the qNano. This is an adjustable tool for the measurement and analysis of tiny particles in scientific research, which comes with a $27,000 price tag. Izon’s success results from the fact that the qNano machine provides precise measurements, while similar machines of the same price only offer averaging techniques. In effect, researchers using the qNano are able to gather information about very small things, and it has many possible applications from drug delivery analysis through to environmental research.

Izon 新设立的美国总部位于马萨诸塞州（Massachusetts）剑桥市（Cambridge），这里是全球最重要的科研与技术中心之一。在 Izon 所用大楼的其他办公室中，有一半公司的名称像 Stemgent 一样带有科学色彩，或含有 pharmaceutical（制药）一词。过去3年里，Izon的规模扩大到原来的17倍。它只销售一种产品就实现了这样的增长，那就是从2004年起一直开发的qNano。qNano 是一种可调节工具，用于在科学研究中测量和分析微小颗粒，售价$27,000。Izon之所以成功，是因为qNano能够提供精确测量，而价格相同的同类机器只能使用求平均值的技术。实际上，研究人员可以用 qNano 收集极微小物体的信息；它拥有许多潜在用途，从药物递送分析到环境研究都包括在内。

Kristoffer Bolen is Izon’s US head of sales. Alongside his full-time role with the company, he’s just finished the fifth semester of six for his High-Tech Master of Business Administration at Northeastern University, which will be his second master’s degree, alongside a Master of Science. These soon-to-be twin master’s degrees provide Bolen with the perfect combination of skills for his job, which depends on him being able to manage the sales process and also comprehend in depth a very complicated piece of technology. When a customer buys a qNano device, an Izon representative will fly anywhere in America to train them how to use the instrument. The representative then checks in with the client on an ongoing basis to be completely informed about how each qNano is being used, and then liaises with technicians back in New Zealand.

Kristoffer Bolen 是Izon的美国销售主管。他在公司全职工作之余，刚完成东北大学（Northeastern University）高科技工商管理硕士6个学期中的第5个学期。加上已有的理学硕士学位，这将是他的第二个硕士学位。这两个即将同时拥有的硕士学位，使 Bolen 具备了工作所需的理想技能组合：他既要能管理销售流程，又要深入理解一项非常复杂的技术。客户购买qNano设备后，Izon代表会飞往美国任何地方，培训客户使用仪器。此后，代表还会持续联系客户，全面了解每台qNano的使用方式，再与新西兰的技术人员沟通。

The Izon story started 8 years ago in New Zealand. Hans van der Voorn was aimless, having sold his own company, and was looking for something more taxing to set his mind to. Van der Voorn was introduced to three scientists with an idea that involved simply a small plastic membrane with a tiny adjustable hole to measure a range of very small particles. Initially he put up an investment of $1.5 million to move the process forward to the development of a prototype, and this led to the founding of Izon and the production of the qNano. Van der Voorn believes that the worlds of science and business sometimes don’t understand each other very well. However, he thinks that engineering can be a bridge between the two, and his long experience in that field has been crucial to the company’s success. He soon realised that Izon was going to need people on the ground overseas if it was going to grow, so the US office was opened. For now Izon trades in the US as a foreign company, so avoids the high level of compliance that comes with being an American-registered company. But he sees that one day soon this will change, as Izon’s current position disallows it from entering into partnerships locally.

Izon的故事始于8年前的新西兰。Hans van der Voorn 卖掉自己的公司后一度没有目标，正想找一件更具挑战性的事情投入精力。有人把3名科学家介绍给 Van der Voorn；他们的构想很简单，就是用一片带有可调微孔的小塑料膜测量一系列极微小颗粒。他最初投入150万，推动项目进入原型开发阶段，Izon 公司由此创立，qNano 也随之问世。Van der Voorn 认为，科学界和商界有时并不能很好地相互理解。不过，他觉得工程学可以在二者之间架起桥梁，而自己在这一领域的长期经验对公司成功至关重要。他很快意识到，Izon要发展，就需要员工常驻海外，因此开设了美国办事处。目前，Izon在美国以外国公司身份经营，从而避开了注册为美国公司所伴随的大量合规要求。但他认为这种情况很快会改变，因为Izon目前的身份不允许它在当地建立合作伙伴关系。

Marketing the qNano is complicated. Izon needs scientists both to buy and advertise the product. The science world may seem like a big playing field, but it’s actually a small group. As a result, the critical part of the company’s marketing is articles that clients publish describing work they have performed with the qNano. So for Izon the sale isn’t enough in itself; it also has a stake in how customers are using the qNano. That’s where Yaniv Gaynor comes in. As development and training manager, he’s involved throughout the sales process and manages most of the customer’s post-sale experience. The sales process is new for Gaynor, who shifted cities and switched from a role as teacher at the University of Minnesota to his first private sector role with Izon. ‘It’s tricky, it’s challenging, and it’s not in my comfort zone,’ he concedes. But nine out of ten times, if there’s an unhappy customer, he’s able to turn their experience around. And there’s another part to this, because sitting with customers as they learn about the qNano, Gaynor gets to see how they are using the product. ‘People ask very good questions, and these are great triggers for future development,’ he says.

qNano的营销工作很复杂。Izon既需要科学家购买产品，也需要他们宣传产品。科学界看似范围很大，实际上圈子很小。因此，公司营销最关键的一环，是客户发表文章，介绍自己使用qNano开展的工作。对Izon而言，仅仅完成销售还不够；客户如何使用qNano也与公司利益相关。这正是 Yaniv Gaynor 发挥作用的地方。作为开发与培训经理，他参与整个销售流程，并负责客户售后体验中的大部分工作。销售对 Gaynor 而言是一个新领域；他搬到另一座城市，辞去明尼苏达大学（University of Minnesota）教师一职，加入 Izon，第一次进入私营部门工作。“这很棘手，也很有挑战性，不是我熟悉的领域，”他承认。不过，遇到不满意的客户时，十次中有九次，他都能扭转局面，让客户的体验由差转好。此外还有另一层收获：陪伴客户了解qNano时，Gaynor 能看到他们如何使用产品。“人们会提出非常好的问题，而这些问题为今后的开发提供了极好的启发，”他说。

‘Our business is selling research tools,’ van der Voorn says. But he knows that the application range of this tool is wide. For instance, Izon is engaged in a project with Harvard University, looking for a particular type of particle of blood that is a symptom of the medical condition of thrombosis. If in the future they can identify this particle, they can design a simple test for it. This would make it easier for doctors to diagnose thrombosis. Another planned development is eventually to shift the US base to more spacious offices on the other side of town. With larger headquarters, the company will be able to bring clients into the Izon building for training with its product, rather than travelling to them, which will hopefully result in an even closer relationship between the company and its customers.

“我们的业务是销售研究工具，”van der Voorn 说。但他知道，这种工具的应用范围非常广。例如，Izon 正在与哈佛大学（Harvard University）合作开展一个项目，寻找血液中一种特定颗粒；这种颗粒是血栓形成（thrombosis）的征兆。如果将来能够识别这种颗粒，他们就可以设计一种简单的检测方法，使医生更容易诊断血栓形成。另一项计划是最终把美国总部搬到城镇另一边更宽敞的办公室。总部扩大后，公司便能把客户请到Izon大楼接受产品培训，而不必派人前往客户所在地；公司希望，这会使自己与客户的关系更加紧密。
