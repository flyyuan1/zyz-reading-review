Why it is necessary to explain the meaning of the word celebrity

为什么有必要解释“名人”一词的含义

AIt seems that our current society cannot get enough information about the daily lives of celebrities. But how did celebrities become such an important force in our culture? While people have always shared a certain obsession with the fantastic and the famous, the notion of celebrity, as well as the types of people termed ‘celebrities’, has evolved greatly throughout the ages. The word ‘celebrity’ has its roots in the language of the ancient Roman civilization. The word we now know to mean ‘a condition of being famous’ or ‘a famous person’ is derived from the Latin word ‘celeber’, meaning ‘frequented or populous’.

当今社会似乎永远看不够名人日常生活的信息。但名人是如何在我们的文化中变得如此重要的？尽管人们一直都对奇异事物和著名人物抱有某种迷恋，但“名人”这一概念，以及被称为名人的人物类型，都在各个时代发生了很大变化。“celebrity”一词源自古罗马文明的语言。如今这个表示“成名状态”或“知名人物”的词，来自拉丁语“celeber”，意思是“人来人往的或人口众多的”。

BThe celebrities of the ancient world were the powerful and awesome deities of Greece and Rome, and the citizens of these civilizations believed in a vast number of immortals who had a direct impact on their lives. It was, therefore, important to know about these figures’ personal lives. This need to know led to the creation of myths, which personalized the gods and involved them in ancient celebrity scandals that thrilled and excited the common people.

古代世界的名人，是希腊和罗马那些强大而令人敬畏的神灵。这两个文明的居民相信，众多不朽的神灵会直接影响他们的生活。因此，了解这些神灵的私生活十分重要。这种求知需要促成了神话的产生；神话把众神人格化，让他们卷入古代的名人丑闻，给普通民众带来刺激和兴奋。

The advantages of celebrity status in the ancient world

CDuring ancient times, amateur and professional athletes also began to make an impact on the celebrity culture. Victors in the ancient Olympic Games were treated as heroes and were often elevated to god-like status. In the ancient Roman civilization, gladiators – the equivalent of today’s professional athletes – were also revered by the common people for their heroics and seemingly superhuman strength.

在古代，业余和职业运动员也开始影响名人文化。古代奥林匹克运动会（Olympic Games）的获胜者被视为英雄，地位经常被抬高到近似神明的程度。在古罗马文明中，角斗士相当于今天的职业运动员；普通民众同样崇敬他们，因为他们英勇无畏，似乎拥有超人的力量。

艰难处境带来的结果

DAs Europe moved into the Dark Ages (the years spanning approximately 400–1300 AD) and a time when athletics and the arts were largely forgotten, monarchs and rulers continued to maintain celebrity status, while religious figures took on newfound fame. The miraculous lives and fascinating deaths of spiritual figures lent excitement to the lives of common people when there was often little else to be excited about, as they faced war, disease and food shortages.

欧洲进入黑暗时代（Dark Ages）后，也就是大约公元400至1300年期间，体育和艺术大多被遗忘，君主和统治者却继续保持着名人地位，宗教人物则获得了新的声望。当时普通民众面对战争、疾病和粮食短缺，往往几乎没有其他令人振奋的事情；宗教人物充满奇迹的一生和引人入胜的死亡，便为他们的生活增添了刺激。

Q18 · 正确标题错误

Creativity having greater value

创造力受到更高重视

你的标题

Admiration for physical achievement

对体能成就的崇拜

EDuring the period 1300–1600 AD, or the Renaissance period as it is known, interest in ruling figures faded. As Europe emerged from its long neglect of the arts, there was greater appreciation for portraits, statues and stone carvings. This period of appreciation for the arts lent a sense of celebrity to artists who were noted for their works and their personal achievements. It was a time when artists began to surpass political and religious individuals for supreme celebrity – a trend that would continue into later centuries.

在公元1300至1600年间，也就是人们所说的文艺复兴时期（Renaissance period），人们对统治者的兴趣减退了。随着欧洲摆脱长期忽视艺术的状态，人们更加欣赏肖像画、雕像和石雕。这一崇尚艺术的时期，使那些以作品和个人成就闻名的艺术家也拥有了名人光环。艺术家在这一时期开始超越政治和宗教人物，成为最受瞩目的名人，而这一趋势在后来几个世纪中继续延续。

Q19 · 正确标题错误

Literacy and widespread celebrity

读写能力的普及与名望的广泛传播

你的标题

The impact of broadcasting on concepts of celebrity

广播电视对名人观念的影响

FWhile the figures of the ancient and early modern civilizations were able to achieve moderate and sometimes lasting celebrity within particular cultures, the global reach of their fame was limited. It was not until the 1700s, when technological advances made publishing commercially viable, that the extent of a person’s fame could spread further. The increase in the availability of the written word was accompanied by a huge rise in the number of common people who could read, allowing a mass audience to find out about celebrities for the first time. Suddenly, the lives of authors, politicians, war heroes, and other celebrities could now be read about in newspapers around the world. These gave ordinary people the opportunity to become intimately knowledgeable about the figures they most admired.

古代和近代早期文明中的人物，虽然能在特定文化中获得一定程度、乃至有时长久不衰的声名，但他们的名气在全球传播的范围有限。直到18世纪，技术进步使商业出版成为可行之事，个人声名才得以传播得更远。文字资料越来越容易取得，与此同时，普通民众中能够阅读的人数也大幅增加，使大众第一次可以了解名人。作家、政治家、战争英雄及其他名人的生活，突然间都能通过世界各地的报纸为人所知。普通人因而有机会深入了解自己最崇拜的人物。

Q20 · 正确标题错误

The impact of broadcasting on concepts of celebrity

广播电视对名人观念的影响

你的标题

Attitudes of celebrities towards the media

名人对媒体的态度

GIn the modern era, particularly as radio and film took off in the 1900s, things really began to change. First, radio began to make its way into the average home in the 1920s and 1930s. Professional athletes also began to be regarded as stars, as their games and exploits could be broadcast over the air for an entirely new audience. Then, the rise of television in the 1950s only cemented the premier level of celebrity that film stars, athletes and television actors were beginning to share. This also meant a huge increase in the individual salaries of these celebrities. Even a few animals gained fame through children’s TV shows. The emergence of reality television shows in the late 1990s allowed all kinds of people with little ability to enjoy a short burst of fame on the television screen. All you needed, it seemed, was an attractive appearance. Today, reality television programmers make it possible to be famous not for doing anything in particular, but simply for being, with the audience deciding whether someone deserves to become a celebrity or not. As the meaning of celebrity continues to evolve and redefine itself in a quickly changing world, there is no telling who will become of interest next.

进入现代，尤其是在20世纪广播和电影兴起后，情况才真正开始改变。首先，20世纪20和30年代，收音机开始进入普通家庭。职业运动员也开始被视为明星，因为他们的比赛和出色表现可以通过无线电播送给全新的受众。随后，20世纪50年代电视的兴起，进一步巩固了电影明星、运动员和电视演员开始共同拥有的顶级名人地位。这也意味着这些名人的个人收入大幅增加。甚至还有少数动物凭借儿童电视节目成名。20世纪90年代末真人秀出现后，各种才能不多的人也能在电视屏幕上短暂走红。当时看来，只需要一副吸引人的外表就够了。如今，真人秀节目制作者让人们可以不因任何具体作为而出名，仅仅因为其存在本身就能成名，而某人是否值得成为名人，则由观众决定。在这个瞬息万变的世界里，“名人”的含义仍在不断演变和重新界定，谁会成为下一个关注对象，无人能够预料。
