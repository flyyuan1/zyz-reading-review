The rise, fall, and rise of one of North America’s rarest mammals

北美洲最稀有哺乳动物之一的兴起、衰落与复兴

AThe black-footed ferret (Mustela nigripes) is a small carnivorous mammal, similar in length to a domestic cat, and is the only ferret species native to North America. For at least 100,000 years they lived in an area that extended almost uninterrupted from southern Canada to northern Mexico. But numbers fell dramatically throughout the 19th and 20th centuries, to such an extent that the black-footed ferret was declared ‘possibly extinct’. Then, almost miraculously, it returned from the dead.

黑足鼬（black-footed ferret，学名 Mustela nigripes）是一种小型食肉哺乳动物，体长与家猫相近，也是北美洲唯一原生的雪貂类动物（ferret）。在至少10万年的漫长时期内，它们曾生活在一片从加拿大南部延伸到墨西哥北部、几乎连成一体的区域。然而，19和20世纪期间，黑足鼬数量急剧下降，以至于被宣布“可能已经灭绝”。随后，它几乎奇迹般地死而复生。

BWithin the genus Mustela, the ferret belongs to the subgenus Putorius, of which there are only three extant species: the European polecat; the Siberian polecat; and the black-footed ferret. The European polecat lives in open forests and meadows, and is thought to be the ancestor of the domestic ferret. The Siberian polecat looks more similar to the black-footed ferret, and leads a similar life on open grasslands and semi-desert regions across Russia, China and Siberia.

在鼬属（Mustela）中，黑足鼬属于艾鼬亚属（Putorius）；这个亚属现存物种只有三种：欧洲艾鼬（European polecat）、西伯利亚艾鼬（Siberian polecat）和黑足鼬。欧洲艾鼬生活在开阔森林和草地中，被认为是家养雪貂的祖先。西伯利亚艾鼬在外形上与黑足鼬更为相似，生活方式也相近，栖息于俄罗斯、中国和西伯利亚各地的开阔草原与半荒漠地区。

CFerrets probably evolved in Europe between three and four million years ago, and dispersed into North America across an ice-age land bridge at least two million years ago. They then advanced south-eastward to the Great Plains through ice-free passageways. The black-footed ferret’s history in North America is inextricably linked to the creature which became almost its sole prey — the prairie dog. These small rodents are a type of ground squirrel which live in networks of underground tunnels, known as burrows. The black-footed ferret’s short legs and long, slim body make it superbly adapted for life in the burrow systems of its prey.

这些鼬类很可能在300万至400万年前于欧洲演化形成，并在至少200万年前经由冰期陆桥扩散到北美洲。随后，它们穿过无冰通道向东南迁移，抵达大平原（Great Plains）。黑足鼬在北美洲的历史，与一种几乎成为其唯一猎物的动物密不可分，那就是草原犬鼠（prairie dog）。这些小型啮齿动物属于地松鼠，生活在称为洞穴的地下隧道网络中。黑足鼬腿短、身体细长，非常适合在猎物的洞穴系统中生活。

DSpending virtually all their time underground, black-footed ferrets have always been elusive. Ferrets are never mentioned in accounts of the early explorers or pioneers who crossed the Great Plains by wagon train during the early to mid-1800s. They were, however, occasionally listed in records kept by fur companies in the upper Missouri River basin during that period. Black-footed ferrets were not officially recognized by scientists until 1851, when a description and drawing of one featured in a book by the renowned naturalist John James Audubon and Reverend John Bachman. For over two decades, no other specimens were seen, and scientists even began to question their existence. Then, in 1874, a Dr Elliott Coues issued a request for specimens through the popular magazine The American Sportsman, and was soon rewarded. With these he was able to add to Audubon’s description, and confirm the existence of the black-footed ferret.

黑足鼬几乎一直生活在地下，向来难以寻觅。19世纪早期至中期，早期探险者或拓荒者乘篷车队穿越大平原时留下的记述，从未提到过这种动物。不过，在当时密苏里河（Missouri River）上游流域毛皮公司保存的记录中，偶尔会列出黑足鼬。直到1851年，科学家才正式确认这种动物；著名博物学家 John James Audubon 与 John Bachman 牧师合著的一本书中，收录了一只黑足鼬的描述和图画。此后二十多年，再没有人见到其他标本，科学家甚至开始怀疑这种动物是否存在。1874年，一位名叫 Elliott Coues 的博士通过大众杂志《The American Sportsman》征集标本，很快便有所收获。借助这些标本，他得以补充 Audubon 的描述，并证实黑足鼬确实存在。

EIn the decades that followed, European settlement across North America changed the landscape dramatically, with vast areas being transformed into crop and grazing land. As agriculture and ranching expanded, the black-footed ferret’s main prey, the prairie dog, became a target for extermination, and was eventually eradicated from 98% of its original range. The result was devastating for black-footed ferret populations throughout the continent. Even today, the prairie dog is still trapped and poisoned, because ranchers believe that it competes with livestock for grass — a notion that belies recent research showing that prairie dogs and cattle feed on different plants.

随后数十年，欧洲移民在北美洲各地定居，极大改变了当地景观，大片土地被改造成农田和牧场。随着农业和畜牧业扩张，黑足鼬的主要猎物草原犬鼠成为根除目标，最终从原分布范围的98%中消失。整个北美洲的黑足鼬种群因此遭受毁灭性打击。即使今天，人们仍在捕捉和毒杀草原犬鼠，因为牧场主认为它会与牲畜争食牧草；然而，近期研究表明，草原犬鼠与牛所吃的植物并不相同，这一结果与上述观念相矛盾。

FAs late as the 1950s, black-footed ferrets were still thought to occur in low densities throughout most of their historic range. However, by the 1960s, the only known population was a small colony in south-western South Dakota. In 1967, the US Fish and Wildlife Service classified the black-footed ferret as an endangered species, and in 1974 the South Dakota colony disappeared. Finally, in January 1979, the last ferret in a captive-breeding programme died — making the black-footed ferret the first mammal to be declared extinct in the Northern Hemisphere in the 20th century.

迟至20世纪50年代，人们仍认为黑足鼬以较低密度分布在其大部分历史栖息范围内。然而，到20世纪60年代，唯一已知的种群只剩南达科他州（South Dakota）西南部的一个小群落。1967年，美国鱼类及野生动物管理局（US Fish and Wildlife Service）把黑足鼬列为濒危物种；1974年，南达科他州的群落消失。最终，1979年1月，圈养繁殖计划中的最后一只黑足鼬死亡，黑足鼬由此成为20世纪北半球第一种被宣布灭绝的哺乳动物。

GBut that was not the end of the story. In 1981, a farm dog called Shep carried an odd-looking creature to its owner’s ranch house in Meeteetse, Wyoming. The rancher put the carcass in his fridge and alerted state officials. The dog had found a black-footed ferret — which led to efforts again being made to save the species. An intensive search in the region led to the discovery of a 130-strong population. This wild colony thrived for a while, but disaster struck again and the number of animals was drastically reduced by the disease distemper — probably contracted from domestic dogs. In 1985, in a final bid to save the species, the remaining 18 individuals in the colony were trapped for a new captive-breeding programme. This project was successful, and there are now more than 2,000 black-footed ferrets in captivity across the US and Canada.

但故事并未就此结束。1981年，一只名叫 Shep 的农场犬把一只外形奇特的动物叼到其主人位于怀俄明州（Wyoming）米蒂茨（Meeteetse）的牧场住宅。牧场主把尸体放进冰箱，并通知州政府官员。这只狗发现的是一只黑足鼬，拯救该物种的行动因此再次展开。研究人员在当地进行全面搜寻，发现了一个由130只黑足鼬组成的种群。这个野生群落一度繁盛，但灾难再度降临：犬瘟热（distemper）使其数量锐减，而疾病很可能是从家犬传染来的。1985年，为挽救该物种作最后的努力，人们捕捉了群落中剩余的18只黑足鼬，开展新的圈养繁殖计划。项目获得成功，如今美国和加拿大圈养的黑足鼬已超过2,000只。

HA reintroduction programme began in 1991. Before they can be reintroduced into their natural habitat, captive ferrets have to learn to hunt and escape from predators. They are first held in pens on the prairie, where they are fed prairie dog meat. Once the ferrets associate these animals with food, keepers unleash live prey into the pens, and they soon realise that they must hunt or go hungry. Then, to get the ferrets used to living underground, they have to be tempted into the holes with food.

野外放归计划始于1991年。圈养黑足鼬在回归自然栖息地之前，必须学会捕猎和逃避捕食者。它们先被关在草原上的围栏中，以草原犬鼠肉为食。黑足鼬把这种动物与食物联系起来后，饲养员会把活猎物放进围栏，它们很快便明白，自己要么捕猎，要么挨饿。随后，为让黑足鼬习惯地下生活，饲养员还必须用食物把它们诱入洞中。

IReintroduction programmes are now underway across central USA, but there is one location that is key. The Janos area on the US-Mexico border is the obvious place to reactivate the continent’s wild black-footed ferret population, being one of the most important remaining grassland ecosystems in the Americas, and home to an abundance of prairie dogs. At the last report, the future of the black-footed ferret finally looks promising.

如今，美国中部各地都在实施野外放归计划，但有一个地点至关重要。美国与墨西哥边境的 Janos 地区，显然是重建北美洲野生黑足鼬种群的理想地点；这里是美洲现存最重要的草原生态系统之一，也生活着大量草原犬鼠。据最近一次报告，黑足鼬的未来终于显得充满希望。
