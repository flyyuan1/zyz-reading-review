你的标题

Real multi-tasking is nearly impossible

真正的多任务处理几乎不可能

ADo you think you're a master of multi-tasking? Think again. Unless you are one of the three percent of super-taskers in the population, research shows that your brain isn't capable of paying close attention to more than one complex task at a time. Researchers who study attention say that effective multi-tasking is beyond most of us. Psychiatrist Edward M Hallowell even describes multi-tasking as 'a mythical activity in which people believe they can perform two or more tasks simultaneously as effectively as they can perform one'.

你认为自己是多任务处理高手？再想一想。研究表明，除非你属于人口中那3%的“超级多任务者”，否则你的大脑无法同时密切关注一项以上的复杂任务。研究注意力的学者说，有效的多任务处理超出了我们大多数人的能力。精神科医生 Edward M Hallowell 甚至把多任务处理描述为“一种虚构的活动；人们相信自己可以同时完成两项或更多任务，而且效率与只做一项任务时一样”。

Q15 · 正确标题错误

A distinction between situations when people can and can’t multi-task

区分人们能与不能进行多任务处理的情形

你的标题

The effects of multi-tasking on neurological structure

多任务处理对神经结构的影响

BIt is true that you can check your email while eating your lunch, or listen to music while walking. But innate activities like walking, chewing, and breathing do not require you to pay attention, whereas activities such as reading, tapping out a text message, or driving a car do require attention. Why is paying attention to two things at once difficult? 'The brain can perform simultaneous tasks, but attention has capacity limitations,' says Associate Professor Paul E Dux, a cognitive neuroscientist at the University of Queensland in Australia. When you do only one thing at a time, you're better at that task than when you're doing multiple things concurrently. Take the classic multi-tasking scenario of talking on a mobile phone while driving - an ill-advised activity that many people believe they have mastered. When David Strayer, Professor of Psychology at the University of Utah in the US, and his team observed 56,000 drivers as they approached an intersection, the majority of drivers who were talking on their phone failed to stop in accordance with traffic laws. And it did not matter if the driver was using a handheld or hands-free device. Even with both eyes on the road and both hands on the wheel, drivers' performance was impaired. Strayer's research shows that performance deteriorates drastically when attention is split between tasks: more mistakes are made and it takes longer to complete each activity.

的确，你可以一边吃午饭一边查邮件，或一边走路一边听音乐。但走路、咀嚼和呼吸等先天活动不需要你投入注意力，阅读、输入短信或开车等活动则需要注意。为什么同时注意两件事很难？澳大利亚昆士兰大学（University of Queensland）认知神经科学家 Paul E Dux 副教授说：“大脑可以同时执行任务，但注意力的容量有限。”一次只做一件事时，你完成这项任务的表现，会比同时做多件事时更好。 以一边开车一边打手机这个典型的多任务场景为例。许多人以为自己已经掌握这种做法，但它并不明智。美国犹他大学（University of Utah）心理学教授 David Strayer 及其团队观察56,000名驶近十字路口的司机时发现，正在打电话的大多数司机都没有依照交通法规停车。司机使用手持设备还是免提设备并无差别。即使双眼都看着道路、双手都放在方向盘上，驾驶表现仍会受损。Strayer 的研究显示，注意力分散到不同任务上时，表现会急剧下降：错误更多，每项活动的完成时间也更长。

Q16 · 正确标题正确

The neurological reasons for struggling to manage more things

难以同时处理更多事务的神经学原因

你的标题

The neurological reasons for struggling to manage more things

难以同时处理更多事务的神经学原因

CThe prefrontal cortex is the brain region responsible for choosing what to pay attention to, and for coordinating inputs from other brain areas. By scanning the prefrontal cortex of people while they multi-tasked, scientists at the French Institute of Health and Medical Research in Paris (INSERM) found that when people focused on a single thing, the right and left sides of the prefrontal cortex work together. But when people attempt to perform two things at once, the sides work independently. Neuroscientist Etienne Koechlin says his study demonstrates that while the brain can switch back and forth between two tasks, we might be in great trouble when we try to juggle more than two tasks, simply because we have only two frontal lobes.'

前额叶皮层是负责选择注意对象，并协调其他脑区输入的大脑区域。位于巴黎的法国国家健康与医学研究院（French Institute of Health and Medical Research，INSERM）的科学家扫描人们进行多任务处理时的前额叶皮层，发现专注于一件事时，前额叶皮层左右两侧会协同工作；但人们试图同时做两件事时，两侧会各自独立工作。神经科学家 Etienne Koechlin 说，他的研究证明，大脑虽然能在两项任务之间来回切换，但尝试同时应付两项以上的任务时，我们可能会陷入大麻烦，原因很简单：我们只有两个额叶。

Q17 · 正确标题正确

Multi-tasking and gender

多任务处理与性别

你的标题

Multi-tasking and gender

多任务处理与性别

DTo the question of whether there is a difference between the sexes, Koechlin's imaging studies uncovered no differences in the ability to switch between tasks in the prefrontal cortices of men or women. But other researchers studying real life scenarios such as finding lost keys, believe there might be some truth to the claim that women are superior multi-taskers. Women have a much better strategy for finding the keys, whereas men tend to jump to it and be far less organised and thorough. 'It's as if they don't stop to reflect and plan for a moment,' says Professor Keith Laws from the University of Hertfordshire in England. But while the ability to develop strategies for coping with the numerous tasks in everyday life could give women an advantage, 'nobody can juggle two, never mind three, "complex" tasks at the same time.'

至于男女之间是否存在差异，Koechlin 的成像研究没有发现男性和女性前额叶皮层在任务切换能力方面有任何不同。但另一些研究人员通过寻找遗失钥匙等现实场景开展研究，认为女性是更优秀的多任务处理者这一说法或许确有几分道理。女性寻找钥匙的策略好得多，男性则往往贸然行动，远没有那么有条理、那么周全。英国赫特福德大学（University of Hertfordshire）的 Keith Laws 教授说：“他们就像根本不会停下来思考片刻，先作计划。”不过，为应对日常生活中的众多任务而制定策略的能力，虽可能让女性占据优势，“却没有任何人能同时应付两项‘复杂’任务，更不用说三项了”。

Q18 · 正确标题正确

Professions in which super-taskers are likely to be found

可能出现超级多任务者的职业

你的标题

Professions in which super-taskers are likely to be found

可能出现超级多任务者的职业

EHowever, David Strayer's research uncovered that some rare people possess extraordinary multi-tasking ability. These so-called 'super-taskers' exhibit different patterns of brain activity when multi-tasking compared to ordinary people: they show less activity in the prefrontal cortex during multi-tasking, suggesting their brains are functioning with a high level of efficiency. Strayer thinks that pilots of high-performance aircraft, high-end chefs who can cook several meals at the same time to perfection, and elite doctors in hospital emergency rooms might all be more likely to be super-taskers. 'All other things being equal, we suspect that super-taskers will rise to a top position in any occupation that places a high demand on juggling various tasks that demand attention at the same time.'

不过，David Strayer 的研究发现，少数人拥有非凡的多任务处理能力。与普通人相比，这些所谓的“超级多任务者”在处理多项任务时，表现出不同的大脑活动模式：他们的前额叶皮层活动更少，说明其大脑以很高的效率运作。Strayer 认为，高性能飞机的飞行员、能够同时把数道菜做到尽善尽美的顶级厨师，以及医院急诊室里的精英医生，都更有可能是超级多任务者。“在其他条件都相同的情况下，我们推测，在任何高度要求同时应付多项需要注意力的任务的职业中，超级多任务者都会升到顶尖位置。”

Q19 · 正确标题正确

The ability to multi-task is determined by people’s genes

多任务处理能力由人的基因决定

你的标题

The ability to multi-task is determined by people’s genes

多任务处理能力由人的基因决定

FThe ability to multi-task probably comes down to the DNA you inherit from your parents to a large extent, says Strayer. 'You are either born with the neural structure that allows you to overcome the usual multi-tasking challenges, or you aren't. Super-taskers' brains are doing something we can't do.' All in all, these findings may have very real consequences on our lives.

Strayer 说，多任务处理能力很可能在很大程度上取决于从父母那里继承的 DNA。“你要么天生具有可以克服常见多任务处理难题的神经结构，要么就没有。超级多任务者的大脑能做到我们做不到的事。”总的来说，这些发现可能会给我们的生活带来非常切实的影响。
