Katelin Butler, returning to her home city of Hobart, reflects on how a new museum has brought about change.

Katelin Butler 回到故乡霍巴特（Hobart），思考一座新博物馆如何带来改变。

As an eighteen-year-old living in the small Australian island state of Tasmania, moving to the ‘mainland’ could not happen soon enough. Now, ten years later, I look forward to going home. This is probably because I’ve grown up, but also perhaps because the cultural landscape of Tasmania has matured. The latest addition to Tasmania’s cultural scene is David Walsh’s Museum of Old and New Art (MONA), a museum that has established Hobart, the capital city of Tasmania, on the global art circuit.

18岁时，我住在澳大利亚的岛州塔斯马尼亚（Tasmania），只盼着尽快搬到“大陆”。如今十年过去，我却期待回家。这大概是因为我长大了，也可能是因为塔斯马尼亚的文化面貌已经成熟。塔斯马尼亚文化领域最新增添的成员，是 David Walsh 创办的古今艺术博物馆（MONA）。这座博物馆让塔斯马尼亚首府霍巴特进入了全球艺术版图。

This building is an example of how art and architecture can have an instant impact on the social and cultural reputation of a place. Andrew Bain of The Sydney Morning Herald wrote, ‘In a virtual blink, Hobart’s cultural landscape has been transformed, with art, wine, fine food and stylish accommodation becoming integral features of the city … If Hobart’s makeover has an origin, it’s the opening of MONA – the Museum of Old and New Art.’ MONA is said to have been solely instrumental in the reinvention of Hobart as a cultural hub.

这座建筑表明，艺术和建筑能够立即影响一个地方的社会与文化声誉。《悉尼先驱晨报》（The Sydney Morning Herald）的 Andrew Bain 写道：“仿佛就在眨眼之间，霍巴特的文化面貌彻底改变；艺术、葡萄酒、美食和时尚住宿都成了这座城市不可或缺的特色……如果霍巴特的改造有一个起点，那就是 MONA——古今艺术博物馆——的开放。”据说，霍巴特之所以能够重塑为文化中心，完全是 MONA 的功劳。

MONA made the national press and international blogs before it opened. After that, Tourism Industry Council of Tasmania’s chief executive, Luke Martin, claimed that MONA was proving so popular it was underpinning the tourism industry in Tasmania. However, MONA’s effect on Hobart has broader ramifications for Tasmania’s tourism industry. As reported in Hobart’s local paper, The Mercury, there is concern about how long people are staying in Tasmania. Rather than spending a week in the state and hiring a car to experience the beauty of the Tasmanian landscape, people are making short stays in Hobart only to visit the gallery. The next challenge for the state is how to entice holiday-makers to stay longer, and to venture further afield. Tasmania is more than a one-hit wonder.

MONA 尚未开放便登上全国报纸和国际博客。开放后，塔斯马尼亚旅游业委员会（Tourism Industry Council of Tasmania）首席执行官 Luke Martin 声称，MONA 正大受欢迎，已经成为塔斯马尼亚旅游业的支柱。然而，MONA 给霍巴特带来的变化，对塔斯马尼亚旅游业也有更广泛的影响。正如霍巴特当地报纸《水星报》（The Mercury）报道的那样，人们担心游客在塔斯马尼亚停留的时间。游客没有在全州游览一周并租车欣赏塔斯马尼亚风景之美，而是仅为了参观这座美术馆，才在霍巴特短暂停留。该州接下来的挑战，是如何吸引度假者停留更久，并前往更远的地区。塔斯马尼亚不只有这一处亮点。

According to Martin, possibilities for packaging up MONA with visits to other galleries around Tasmania are being investigated. Although not directly related to these investigations, the 1891 Queen Victoria Museum and Art Gallery at Royal Park in Launceston is currently being restored to its original condition; there are also plans to extensively redevelop the Tasmanian Museum and Art Gallery in Hobart. In addition to this, there are up to twenty other existing quality galleries in the state. So there is more to see than just MONA.

Martin 表示，人们正在研究把 MONA 与参观塔斯马尼亚各地其他美术馆组合成套装行程的可能性。虽然并非与这些研究直接相关，朗塞斯顿（Launceston）皇家公园（Royal Park）内建于1891年的维多利亚女王博物馆和美术馆（Queen Victoria Museum and Art Gallery），目前正恢复原貌；霍巴特的塔斯马尼亚博物馆和美术馆（Tasmanian Museum and Art Gallery）也有大规模改建计划。此外，全州还有多达二十家现有的优质美术馆。因此，值得参观的并不只有 MONA。

Although large, the building is rather nondescript, sitting heavily on the edge of the Derwent River. MONA’s ‘wow factor’ comes from the notoriety of David Walsh, the museum’s millionaire founder, and his choice of unusual artworks. The architecture has more depth of meaning and does exactly as it should: it supports the vision of the museum, essentially the vision of Walsh. It’s a new building in the context of a maturing city. The dark, moody labyrinth of gallery spaces could be likened to the mind of the eccentric founder himself. Each artwork appears to be integrated into the dark materiality of the architecture – the interiors are far from light, bright and neutral, as might be seen in a more traditional gallery. The dim lighting and rough, rocky surfaces are a reminder that visitors are three levels underground.

这座建筑虽然很大，却相当不起眼，沉甸甸地坐落在德文特河（Derwent River）边。MONA 令人惊叹之处，来自其百万富翁创办人 David Walsh 惹人议论的名声，以及他对非同寻常艺术品的选择。建筑本身有更深层的含义，也恰如其分地发挥作用：它支撑着博物馆的愿景，而这本质上就是 Walsh 的愿景。在一座逐渐成熟的城市中，这是一栋新建筑。阴暗而氛围阴郁的迷宫式展览空间，可以比作这位古怪创办人本人的内心。每件艺术品似乎都融入建筑材料的幽暗质感之中；这里的室内空间绝非传统美术馆中常见的明亮、通透和中性。昏暗灯光与粗糙岩石表面不断提醒参观者：他们正位于地下三层。

The warped, mirrored surface at the museum’s entry could be seen as the start of a journey of reflecting on one’s existence. Much of the art explores the human condition – there are uninhibited artistic descriptions of human relationships, death, and even the digestion process. Going to a gallery with such confronting artworks with your parents is an interesting experience; I was pleasantly surprised by my parents’ openness to the exhibitions. My father, who normally whisks through a gallery while my mother likes to take her time, happily spent almost five hours at MONA; the urge to linger is a common feeling here. This is because the gallery engages all of the senses, and visitors often pause to experience the effect each artwork has on them. This raises the question of who is actually visiting MONA – and it’s definitely not just the normal gallery-goers. People from all kinds of socioeconomic backgrounds are curious about what lies within the subterranean maze of MONA. Conservative minds are being opened, which is a good thing anywhere.

博物馆入口处变形的镜面，可以被视为一段反思自身存在之旅的起点。许多艺术作品都在探索人的生存状态，毫不掩饰地以艺术形式描绘人际关系、死亡，甚至消化过程。和父母一起去美术馆看这样富有冲击力的作品，是一种有趣的体验；父母对展览的开放态度令我惊喜。父亲平时总会匆匆走过美术馆，母亲却喜欢慢慢观看；这次父亲很愉快地在 MONA 待了近五小时。来到这里，人们普遍会想多停留一会儿。这是因为美术馆能调动全部感官，参观者往往会停下来，感受每件艺术品对自己产生的影响。这也引出了一个问题：究竟是谁在参观 MONA？答案肯定不只是平时常去美术馆的人。来自各种社会经济背景的人，都好奇 MONA 地下迷宫里藏着什么。思想保守的人也在逐渐敞开心扉，这无论在哪里都是好事。

Walsh’s antiquities collection is superb, but he knew that old coins would compete for attention against new works that are more challenging in scale and subject matter, hence the device of setting antiquities among twentieth- and twenty-first-century works. For me, one of the intriguing outcomes of this strategy was an intensified feeling that the makers of the coins and antiquities were the great contemporary artists of their time.

Walsh 收藏的古物极其出色，但他知道，古钱币必须与规模和题材都更具挑战性的新作品争夺注意力，因此采取了把古物放在20世纪和21世纪作品之间的办法。对我来说，这一策略带来的一个耐人寻味的结果，是让我更强烈地感到：制作这些钱币和古物的人，正是他们那个时代伟大的当代艺术家。

The so-called Bilbao Effect has worked its magic on Hobart because, just as Frank Gehry’s Guggenheim Museum has transformed the city of Bilbao in Spain, MONA has been a magnet for tourists in Hobart. However, the architectural ‘wow’ factor of the Guggenheim was the most famous component of that city’s transformation, whereas Fender Katsalidis’s MONA does not have the same spectacular impact as the shimmering metal surfaces of Gehry’s building. Walsh himself abhors descriptions of MONA as the Bilbao of the south, saying that Fender Katsalidis solved a problem when he designed MONA, in executing a project of this scale in an underground location. Walsh contrasts this with what he describes as the “architectural self-indulgence of Gehry when designing the Guggenheim in Bilbao.” Call it the Bilbao Effect, but this phenomenon has been around for thousands of years – good public architecture forms the centrepiece of a city. This is probably true, but like the Guggenheim, MONA has initiated something very important for Tasmania, and for Hobart in particular. Now the question is how Tasmania will build on David Walsh’s input into its cultural setting.

所谓“毕尔巴鄂效应”（Bilbao Effect）已经在霍巴特显现魔力：正如 Frank Gehry 设计的古根海姆博物馆（Guggenheim Museum）改变了西班牙毕尔巴鄂（Bilbao）市，MONA 也成了霍巴特吸引游客的磁石。不过，古根海姆博物馆在建筑上令人惊叹的效果，是毕尔巴鄂转型中最著名的部分；Fender Katsalidis 设计的 MONA 却没有 Gehry 那座建筑闪亮金属表面所带来的同等壮观冲击。Walsh 本人非常厌恶把 MONA 称为“南方的毕尔巴鄂”。他说，Fender Katsalidis 设计 MONA 时，是在解决一个难题：如何在地下完成如此规模的项目。他把这一点与自己所谓“Gehry 设计毕尔巴鄂古根海姆博物馆时在建筑上的自我放纵”相对照。姑且把它称为毕尔巴鄂效应，但这种现象已经存在数千年——优秀的公共建筑会成为一座城市的核心。这种说法大概没错；但与古根海姆博物馆一样，MONA 已经为塔斯马尼亚、尤其是霍巴特开启了非常重要的新局面。现在的问题是，塔斯马尼亚将如何在 David Walsh 为当地文化环境作出贡献的基础上继续发展。
