A review of Frank M Snowden’s masterly history of a country’s fight to eradicate a deadly disease

评述 Frank M Snowden 的一部杰出史著，书中讲述了一个国家为根除致命疾病而展开的斗争。

AThe word ‘malaria’ means ‘bad air’ in Italian, and this terrible disease marked the life of the people of that country for thousands of years. Yet by 1962, Italy was officially declared malaria-free, and it has remained so ever since. Frank Snowden’s study of this successful endeavour is a remarkable piece of historical work. Original, crystal clear, analytical and passionate, Snowden takes us to areas historians have rarely visited before.

“malaria”在意大利语中意为“污浊空气”；数千年来，这种可怕疾病一直深深影响着意大利人民的生活。然而，到1962年，意大利被正式宣布为无疟疾国家，此后也一直保持这一状态。Frank Snowden 对这项成功事业的研究，是一部非凡的历史著作。作品见解独到、清晰透彻，兼具分析深度与热情；Snowden 带领我们走进历史学家过去很少涉足的领域。

BEverybody now knows that malaria is carried by mosquitoes. But in the 19th century most experts subscribed to the theory of ‘miasma’ or ‘poisoning of the air’. Others made a link between swamps, water and malaria, but did not make the further leap towards insects. The consequence of these theories was that little was done to combat the disease before the end of that century. The situation was so serious that from a total population of 25m Italians, 11m were ‘permanently at risk’. In warm, damp, malarial zones, the life expectancy of land workers was a terrifying 22.5 years. The economic impact of the disease was immense. Epidemics were blamed on people who originated from the hotter parts of Italy, given the widespread belief that malaria was hereditary.

如今人人都知道，疟疾由蚊子传播。但在19世纪，大多数专家都认同“瘴气”（miasma）理论，也就是认为空气中的毒气会致病。还有一些人把沼泽、水和疟疾联系起来，却没有进一步想到昆虫。受这些理论影响，在19世纪末以前，人们几乎没有采取措施对抗疟疾。局势严重到，在意大利2500万总人口中，有1100万人“长期处于风险之中”。在温暖、潮湿且疟疾流行的地区，农业劳动者的预期寿命低得惊人，只有22.5岁。这种疾病对经济造成的影响十分巨大。由于人们普遍认为疟疾会遗传，疫情暴发还会被归咎于来自意大利较炎热地区的人。

COne of the first breakthroughs in the war against malaria came in 1898 when the zoologist Giovanni Battista Grassi demonstrated that the micro-organisms causing the disease were carried in the digestive tract of the mosquito. By releasing mosquitoes into rooms to drink the blood of healthy human volunteers, Grassi was able to make the direct link between the insects and the disease. Definitive proof of this theory was obtained after an extraordinary series of experiments in Italy, where healthy people were introduced into malarial zones but kept free of mosquito bites – and remained well. The recently formed Italian state at last had the necessary information to begin tackling the disease.

抗击疟疾的首批突破之一出现在1898年。当时，动物学家 Giovanni Battista Grassi 证明，致病微生物存在于蚊子的消化道中。Grassi 把蚊子放进房间，让它们吸食健康人类志愿者的血，从而直接建立起蚊虫与疾病之间的联系。随后，意大利开展了一系列非同寻常的实验，最终确证了这一理论：健康人进入疟疾流行区，但始终避免被蚊子叮咬，结果没有患病。新近成立的意大利国家终于获得了开始治理这种疾病所需的信息。

DA complicated approach was adopted, which made use of quinine – a drug obtained from tree bark which had long been used to combat fever, but was now seen as a crucial part of the war on malaria. Italy introduced a quinine law and a quinine tax in 1904, and the drug was administered to large numbers of rural workers. Despite its often terrible side effects, the drug was successful in limiting the spread of the disease, and in breaking cycles of infection. In addition, Italy set up rural health centres and invested heavily in education programmes. Malaria, as Snowden shows, was not just a medical problem, but a social and regional issue, and could only be defeated through multi-layered strategies.

意大利采用了一套复杂的办法，其中使用了奎宁（quinine）。奎宁是一种从树皮中提取的药物，长期以来一直用于退烧，如今则被视为抗击疟疾的关键一环。1904年，意大利制定了奎宁法并开征奎宁税，还为大批农村劳动者用药。尽管奎宁常会产生严重副作用，它仍成功限制了疾病传播，并打破感染循环。此外，意大利还建立了乡村卫生中心，并大量投资教育项目。正如 Snowden 所示，疟疾不仅是医学问题，也是社会和地区问题，只有采取多层次策略才能将其击败。

EIt was originally decided to give quinine to all those in vulnerable regions – even healthy people. However, peasants were often suspicious of medicine being forced upon them, and doctors were frequently met with hostility and stubborn refusal to accept the treatment offered. But despite the refusals, the strategy as a whole was hugely successful. Deaths from malaria fell by some 80% in the first decade of the 20th century and some areas escaped altogether from the scourge of the disease.

最初的决定是，给易受感染地区的所有人服用奎宁，即使健康者也不例外。然而，农民往往对这种强加给他们的药物心存疑虑，常常敌视医生，并执意拒绝接受治疗。不过，尽管有人拒绝，整体策略仍取得了巨大成功。20世纪最初10年间，疟疾死亡人数下降了约80%，有些地区甚至彻底摆脱了这种疾病的祸害。

FThe 1915-18 war delayed the campaign, as funds were diverted to the battlefields. In the 1920s and 1930s the draining of the damp, unhealthy marshlands around Rome had a certain impact on the spread of malaria. However, as war swept through the drained lands in the 1940s, the disease returned with a vengeance. Yet the country’s leading malariologist Alberto Missiroli refused to order the distribution of quinine, so allowing the epidemic to spread unchecked. According to Snowden, he did this in order to create the ideal conditions for a new strategy, supported by the US Rockefeller Foundation – a massive experiment involving the extermination of mosquitoes with the chemical DDT. It is estimated that more than a third of the inhabitants in the affected area contracted malaria and countless thousands died.

1915至1918年的战争拖延了抗疟行动，因为资金被转用于战场。20世纪20和30年代，人们排干罗马周边潮湿、有害健康的沼泽地，这在一定程度上影响了疟疾传播。然而，20世纪40年代，战争席卷这些排干的土地，疟疾又猛烈卷土重来。然而，该国最杰出的疟疾专家 Alberto Missiroli 拒绝下令发放奎宁，任由疫情不受控制地蔓延。Snowden 称，他这样做是为了给一项新策略创造理想条件。这项策略得到美国洛克菲勒基金会（Rockefeller Foundation）支持，是一次利用化学品 DDT 消灭蚊虫的大规模实验。据估计，受影响地区超过三分之一的居民染上疟疾，死亡人数以千计，难以计数。

GWith the end of the war in 1945, the US government and the Rockefeller Foundation were free to experiment. DDT was sprayed from the air, and 3m Italians had their bodies covered with the chemical. The effects were immense, and by 1962 malaria was more or less gone from the whole country. One of the final victims to die of the disease in Italy was the popular cyclist, Fausto Coppi. He had contracted malaria in Africa in 1960, and the failure of doctors in Italy to spot the disease was a sign of the times. A few decades earlier, they would have immediately noticed the telltale signs; it was later claimed that a small dose of quinine would have saved his life.

1945年战争结束后，美国政府和洛克菲勒基金会可以放手开展实验。人们从空中喷洒 DDT，还有300万意大利人全身被涂上这种化学品。效果十分显著，到1962年，疟疾已在意大利全国基本消失。在意大利死于这种疾病的最后几名患者中，有一位是广受喜爱的自行车运动员 Fausto Coppi。他于1960年在非洲感染疟疾，意大利医生未能发现这种疾病，正反映了时代已经改变。若在几十年前，医生会立即注意到那些明显征兆；后来有人称，只需一小剂奎宁便能挽救他的生命。

HAs there are still more than 1m deaths every year from malaria worldwide, Snowden’s book also has contemporary relevance. This is a disease that affects every level of the societies where it is rampant. The economic miracle of the 1950s and 1960s which made Italy into a modern industrial nation would not have been possible without the eradication of malaria. Moreover, this book convincingly argues that the disease was ‘an integral part of the big picture of modern Italian history’. This magnificent study, beautifully written and impeccably documented, deserves an audience beyond specialists in history, or in Italy.

如今，全球每年仍有100多万人死于疟疾，因此 Snowden 的著作也具有现实意义。在疟疾肆虐的社会中，这种疾病会影响每一个层面。20世纪50和60年代的经济奇迹使意大利成为现代工业国家；如果没有根除疟疾，这一切便不可能实现。此外，本书令人信服地论证，疟疾是“现代意大利历史宏大图景中不可分割的一部分”。这部研究写作优美、文献翔实，堪称杰作；它值得历史或意大利研究专业人士以外的读者阅读。
