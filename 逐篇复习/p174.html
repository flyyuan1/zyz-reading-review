evidence from studies of rats and birds

来自大鼠与鸟类研究的证据

AThe best known model of animal learning behaviour is probably the so-called classical model associated with the Russian scientist Pavlov. The key features of this model are stimulus, outcome and repetition. Pavlov demonstrated that animals could be trained to associate a sensory stimulus with a particular outcome, even where no natural association exists. In one famous experiment, dogs learned to associate the sound of a bell with the expectation of food. Subsequent models, such as the ‘operant conditioning’ model, also involved observing animals in isolation. Recently, however, behavioural research has focused on how animals are equipped to learn in social settings. And interest in the concept of role modelling, already a popular notion for guiding child development, has spread to other species.

最为人熟知的动物学习行为模型，可能是与俄罗斯科学家 Pavlov 联系在一起的所谓经典模型。这一模型的关键要素是刺激、结果和重复。Pavlov 证明，通过训练，可以使动物把某种感官刺激与特定结果联系起来，即使两者本来并无自然联系。在一个著名实验中，狗学会了把铃声与即将获得食物的预期联系起来。后来的模型，例如“操作性条件作用”模型，也是在动物被单独隔离的情况下对它们进行观察。不过，近年来的行为研究已将重点转向：动物具备怎样的能力，能够在社会环境中学习。以榜样示范来引导儿童发展的观念早已流行，如今，人们对这一概念的兴趣也延伸到了其他物种。

BHeyes and Galef (1996) have compiled some very interesting and accessible papers on social learning in mammals. One of these papers starts with an account of a school field trip to Israel. In a pine forest there, many pine cones were discovered which had been stripped to the central core. So an investigation was started which had no weighty theoretical intent, but was directed initially at finding out what were eating the nutritious pine seeds, and how they managed to get them out of the cones. The culprit proved to be the versatile and athletic black rat. The forest-dwelling rat used a standard technique, biting off each cone scale in sequence, from base to tip, following the spiral growth pattern of the cone.

Heyes 和 Galef（1996年）汇编了一些研究哺乳动物社会学习的论文，内容十分有趣，也易于理解。其中一篇论文以一次学校赴以色列（Israel）的实地考察经历开篇。人们在那里的一片松林中发现了许多被剥得只剩中心芯轴的松果。于是，一项调查开始了；它并没有宏大的理论目标，起初只是想弄清楚，是什么动物在吃这些营养丰富的松子，以及它们如何把松子从松果中取出来。结果发现，干这件事的是适应能力强、行动敏捷的黑鼠（black rat）。这些生活在森林里的黑鼠采用一套固定方法，沿松果螺旋生长的方向，从底部到顶端依次咬掉每片鳞片。

CThe investigation was then broadened, with the aim of determining how the rat acquires such a skill. Urban black rats were found to lack the skill, and were unable to learn it even when they were housed with experienced cone strippers, such as those found in Israel. However, infants of urban mothers who were fostered to forest mothers acquired the skill, whereas infants of forest mothers fostered by urban mothers did not. Clearly, the skill is not inherited, but has to be taught.

随后，调查范围扩大了，目的是弄清黑鼠如何获得这种技能。研究发现，城市黑鼠不会这项技能，即使把它们与熟练剥松果的黑鼠——例如以色列发现的那些黑鼠——放在一起饲养，它们也学不会。然而，城市母鼠生下的幼鼠交由森林母鼠抚养后，学会了这项技能；森林母鼠生下的幼鼠交由城市母鼠抚养后，却没有学会。显然，这项技能并非遗传而来，而是必须通过传授才能掌握。

DFurther elegant experiments showed that naive adult urban rats can develop the skill if they are provided with cones from which the first complete spiral of scales has been removed, rather as people can deduce how to use a new photocopier once they have been shown how to switch it on. In the case of forest rats, mothers supply their infants with cones which they have partially stripped, thereby providing the key for acquiring the complete stripping skill.

后续一些设计巧妙的实验表明，如果给没有相关经验的成年城市黑鼠提供已经剥去第一整圈螺旋鳞片的松果，它们就能掌握这项技能；这很像别人演示了新复印机如何开机后，人们便能推断出该怎样使用它。森林母鼠会把自己剥开一部分的松果交给幼鼠，从而提供掌握完整剥松果技能的关键线索。

EWe might conclude that this is a good example of adaptive learning, but how economical is it? The researchers went on to determine this by measuring the oxygen uptake of a rat stripping a cone. The rat was placed in a metabolic chamber so that the amount of energy used in the cone-stripping operation could be calculated. This was then compared with the energy benefit of the pine seeds, as measured by a machine known as a calorimeter. The rat's energy expenditure proved to be less than 10% of the energetic value of the cone – an acceptable profit margin.

我们也许会认为，这是适应性学习的一个好例子，但它在能量上究竟划不划算呢？研究人员接着测量黑鼠剥松果时的耗氧量，以此回答这个问题。他们把黑鼠放进代谢室，这样就能计算剥松果这一操作消耗的能量。再将这一能耗与松子所提供的能量收益相比较；后者用一种叫作量热计的仪器测量。结果表明，黑鼠消耗的能量不到松果所含能量的10%，这样的收益可以接受。

FA paper by Bednekoff and Balda (1996) provides another example of the adaptiveness of social learning, this time in birds. It concerns the seed caching behaviour of two birds: Clark's Nutcracker (Nucifraga columbiana) and the Mexican Jay (Aphelocoma ultramarina). The former is a specialist, caching 30,000 or so seeds in scattered locations, from where it recovers them over the winter months. The Mexican Jay also caches food, but is not nearly as dependent on this as the Nutcracker. The two species also differ in their social structure, the Nutcracker being rather solitary, while the Jay forages in social groups.

Bednekoff 和 Balda（1996年）的一篇论文提供了另一个社会学习适应性的例子，这次研究的对象是鸟类。论文讨论两种鸟储藏种子的行为：Clark's Nutcracker（Nucifraga columbiana）和 Mexican Jay（Aphelocoma ultramarina）。前者擅长储食，会在分散的地点藏下大约30,000颗种子，再在冬季的几个月里取回。Mexican Jay 也会储藏食物，但它对此的依赖远不及 Nutcracker。这两种鸟的社会结构也不同：Nutcracker 比较独居，而 Jay 则成群觅食。

GAn experiment was conducted to discover not just whether a bird can remember where it hid a seed itself, but also if it can remember where it saw another bird hide a seed. The design of the experiment is slightly comical, with a cacher bird wandering about a room with lots of holes in the floor and hiding food in some of them, while watched by an observer bird perched in a cage. Two days later, cachers and observers are tested for their discovery rate against an estimated random performance. In the role of cacher, not only the Nutcracker but also the less specialised Jay performed above the rate of chance. More surprisingly, however, Jay observers were as successful as Jay cachers, whereas Nutcracker observers did no better than chance. So it seems that, whereas the Nutcracker is highly adapted for remembering where it hid its own seed, the gregarious Mexican Jay is more adept than the Nutcracker at remembering, and so exploiting, the caches of other birds.

研究人员开展了一项实验，不仅想了解鸟能否记住自己把种子藏在何处，也想知道它是否能记住自己看到另一只鸟藏种子的地点。实验设计略带几分滑稽：一只负责藏食的鸟在地板上有许多洞的房间里走动，把食物藏进其中一些洞里；与此同时，一只停在笼中的鸟在旁观察。两天后，研究人员测试藏食鸟与观察鸟找到种子的比率，并将结果与估算的随机发现水平相比较。在担任藏食者时，不仅 Nutcracker，就连储食能力没那么专门化的 Jay，表现也超过了随机水平。然而，更令人惊讶的是，Jay 观察者与 Jay 藏食者一样成功，而 Nutcracker 观察者的表现却并不优于随机水平。由此看来，Nutcracker 非常适于记住自己藏种子的位置，而喜群居的 Mexican Jay 则比 Nutcracker 更善于记住其他鸟的藏食地点，并加以利用。
