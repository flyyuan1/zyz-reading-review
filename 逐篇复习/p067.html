Satellite technology is helping farmers boost crop yields

卫星技术正在帮助农民提高作物产量

AFor farmers, working out the optimal amount of seed, fertiliser, pesticide and water to scatter on a field can make, or break, the subsequent harvest. Regular laboratory analyses of soil and plant samples from various parts of the field can help – but such expertise is costly, and often unavailable. However, a new and cheaper method of doing this analysis is now on offer. Precise prescriptions for growing crops can be obtained quickly, and less expensively, by calculating the amount of electromagnetic radiation reflected from agricultural land. The data is collected by orbiting satellites.

对农民来说，算出一块田地应播撒多少种子、肥料、农药和水才最合适，可能决定随后收成的成败。定期对田地不同区域的土壤和植物样本进行实验室分析会有所帮助，但这类专业服务费用高昂，而且往往无法获得。不过，现在已经有一种新的、更便宜的分析方法。通过计算农田反射的电磁辐射量，可以迅速以较低成本获得种植作物的精确方案，而相关数据由轨道卫星收集。

BExamining the wavelength of the radiation that is reflected can reveal, with surprising precision, the properties of the soil, the quantity of crop being grown, and the levels in those crops of chlorophyll*, various minerals, moisture and other indicators of their quality. If recent and forecast weather data is added to the mix, detailed maps can be produced indicating exactly how, where and when crops should be grown. The service usually costs less than US$15 per hectare for a handful of readings a year, and can increase yields by as much as 10%.

分析反射辐射的波长，可以以惊人的精度揭示土壤性质、作物生长量，以及作物中叶绿素、各种矿物质和水分的含量，还有其他反映作物品质的指标。如果再加入近期天气和天气预报数据，就能制作详细地图，准确说明应当如何、在哪里以及何时种植作物。这项服务通常每年提供几次读数，每公顷收费不到15美元，并能把产量提高多达10%。

CSuch precision farming using satellite-based intelligence is a relatively new technique. Even so, it is catching on quickly. Five times a year, for example, a French cereal-growers’ co-operative called Sevépi purchases satellite data and makes it available to its members in the form of maps of their fields, divided into three or four colour-coded zones per hectare. For each zone, one of about 50 fertiliser formulas is recommended. On top of this, if the wheat in the field has already grown quite high early in the season, and heavy showers are expected, an appropriate dose of growth regulator is recommended for each zone. (Long, fragile stems break more easily in downpours.) Then, farm vehicles equipped with global-positioning system locators automatically mix and apply the prescribed dose to each area.

这种利用卫星信息的精准农业是一项较新的技术。即便如此，它仍在迅速普及。例如，一家名为 Sevépi 的法国谷物种植者合作社每年购买五次卫星数据，再以田地图的形式提供给成员；图上每公顷被划分为三到四个用颜色标示的区域。每个区域都会得到一种推荐的肥料配方，选自大约50种配方。此外，如果田里的小麦在生长季初已经长得很高，并预计会有强降雨，每个区域还会得到适量生长调节剂的使用建议。（细长脆弱的茎秆在暴雨中更容易折断。）随后，配备全球定位系统定位器的农用车辆会自动混合规定剂量，并施用于各个区域。

DFrance is the pioneer in this sort of surveillance. More farmland is analysed by satellite there than in any other country, according to Infoterra (a subsidiary of EADS Astrium), the firm that is France’s largest provider of such information, supplying data to companies such as Sevépi. Moreover, Henri Douche, head of Infoterra’s agriculture sales in Toulouse, reckons the amount of monitored farmland will increase as weather patterns change and farmers can no longer rely on the past as a guide to the future. When confounded by the yield variations that these new weather patterns will bring, even farmers who are afraid of new technology will sign up, he says.

法国是这类监测的先驱。法国最大的此类信息供应商 Infoterra 是 EADS Astrium 的一家子公司，也向 Sevépi 等公司提供数据。据该公司称，法国接受卫星分析的农田面积超过其他任何国家。此外，Infoterra 驻图卢兹（Toulouse）的农业销售负责人 Henri Douche 估计，随着天气模式改变，农民再也不能以过去指导未来，受监测的农田面积还会增加。他说，当新天气模式带来的产量波动使农民无所适从时，即使害怕新技术的农民也会登记使用这项服务。

EInexpensive data on the productivity of land is advantageous to governments, too. Areas where fertilisers and pesticides are being applied excessively can be pinpointed, studied and regulated by environmental and land-use agencies. Guy Lafond, an agronomist with Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada, a government agency, says the satellite data it purchases is proving useful for the study of fields with declining productivity in the province of Saskatchewan. Overkill with nitrate fertilisers (which are also a source of greenhouse gases) appears partly responsible. And according to RapidEye, a German satellite operator, insurance companies are also studying satellite data with a view to selling insurance policies to governments of famine-prone countries that might be threatened by crop failure.

廉价的土地生产力数据对政府也有好处。环境和土地使用机构可以准确找出肥料和农药使用过量的地区，对其进行研究和监管。加拿大农业及农业食品部（Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada）是一个政府机构，该机构的农学家 Guy Lafond 表示，该机构购买的卫星数据正显示出其作用，有助于研究萨斯喀彻温省（Saskatchewan）生产力下降的农田。过量使用硝酸盐肥料似乎要负部分责任，而这类肥料也是温室气体的来源。据德国卫星运营商 RapidEye 称，保险公司也在研究卫星数据，希望向可能受作物歉收威胁的饥荒高风险国家政府出售保险。

FIn March, RapidEye began selling data that helps forecast harvests. ‘Too often, farmers limit productivity by managing fields uniformly,’ says Fredrick Jung-Rothenhaüsler, head of product development at the firm’s headquarters in Brandenburg an der Havel. ‘Our satellites are the first commercial satellites to include the Red-Edge band of the light spectrum, which is sensitive to changes in chlorophyll content. More research will be necessary to realise the full potential of the Red-Edge band. However, this band can assist in monitoring vegetation health, improving species separation and also help in measuring protein and nitrogen content in biomass.’ The company’s data, which comes from both Europe and the Americas, breaks field productivity down into patches just five metres square.

3月，RapidEye 开始销售有助于预测收成的数据。Fredrick Jung-Rothenhaüsler 是该公司位于哈弗尔河畔勃兰登堡（Brandenburg an der Havel）的总部产品开发负责人，他说：“农民经常对整片田地采用完全相同的管理方式，因而限制了生产力。我们的卫星是首批把光谱红边波段（Red-Edge band）纳入其中的商业卫星；该波段能敏锐反映叶绿素含量变化。要充分发挥红边波段的潜力，还需要开展更多研究。不过，这一波段可以帮助监测植被健康、提高物种区分能力，也能协助测量生物质中的蛋白质和氮含量。”该公司的数据来自欧洲和美洲，能把田地生产力细分到仅五米见方的地块。

GThe advantages that satellite technology provides in terms of precision farming do not have to be restricted to rich countries. In Africa, where many soils have become badly depleted of nutrients, better fertiliser management would greatly improve the situation. As a consequence, the charitable trust World Agroforestry Centre, in the city of Nairobi, in Kenya, has begun to build up a catalogue of the radiation patterns derived from around 100,000 samples of African soils. The information is the key to understanding the potential of these soils to be more agriculturally productive. Once passed on to the International Centre for Tropical Agriculture, based in Colombia, South America, it is intended that the information be used to build a database called the ‘Digital Soil Map’. When ready, this will provide farmers with free forecasts, developed with regularly updated satellite imagery, across farmland in a number of countries in Africa. This is information which will almost certainly assist in improving crop yields. For a hunger-ravaged continent, that is good news indeed.

卫星技术在精准农业方面提供的优势不必只限于富裕国家。在非洲，许多土壤的养分已经严重耗竭，更好地管理肥料将大幅改善情况。因此，位于肯尼亚内罗毕的慈善信托机构世界农用林业中心（World Agroforestry Centre），已经开始建立一份辐射模式目录；这些模式来自约10万个非洲土壤样本。相关信息是了解这些土壤提高农业生产力潜力的关键。信息传给位于南美洲哥伦比亚的国际热带农业中心（International Centre for Tropical Agriculture）后，计划用来建立一个名为“数字土壤地图”（Digital Soil Map）的数据库。数据库完成后，将结合定期更新的卫星图像，为非洲多个国家的农民免费提供农田预测信息。这些信息几乎肯定有助于提高作物产量。对于一片饱受饥饿折磨的大陆来说，这的确是好消息。

* chlorophyll: the green substance in plants that allows them to absorb sunlight

* chlorophyll：叶绿素，植物中使其能够吸收阳光的绿色物质
