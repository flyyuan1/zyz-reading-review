H 段 科学界的责任 认真衡量并如实呈现风险

AWe run risks. Most of the time we ignore them; sometimes we stop and decide how to deal with them. That’s life. Risks arise in sports, in business, in personal relationships; they are present in the laboratory, in political calculations, everywhere. Acting individually, we often prefer to gain some benefit by accepting some danger. Acting collectively, we make similar trade-offs.

我们都要冒风险。大多数时候，我们忽略风险；有时则会停下来，决定如何应对。这就是生活。体育运动、商业和人际关系中都会出现风险；实验室里有风险，政治考量中有风险，风险无处不在。以个人身份行动时，我们常愿意承受一些危险来换取某些好处；集体行动时，我们也会作出类似的权衡。

BWhen society must assess risks, and those risks can be clarified, at least in part, by science, technology or mathematics, the responsibility of the research community is to be clear and complete. But that is not always so easy. Furthermore, too few people pay full attention to the best evidence, much less modify their behaviors as a result. Even when the evidence of high risk is clear – about smoking, for instance – many will deny the risk, or dispute it, or accept that it exists and still go ahead. As a former cigarette smoker, I understand denial.

当社会必须评估风险，而科学、技术或数学至少能澄清其中一部分时，研究界有责任作出清楚而完整的说明。但这并不总是那么容易。此外，充分关注最佳证据的人太少，愿意因此改变行为的人就更少了。即使高风险的证据十分明确——例如吸烟的风险——许多人仍会否认或质疑风险，或者承认它存在却照做不误。我以前也吸烟，因此理解这种否认心理。

CPerhaps more important than the basic facts of risk are the varied perceptions of it. Hearing the prediction of 60% chance of rain, or recognizing the minuscule likelihood of becoming ill from a flu shot, most people understand the right responses: take your umbrella and get your flu shot. But some, fearfully but irrationally, go to the extreme and stay at home and avoid all contact with other people. Irrational decisions occasionally affect us all. When it comes to risks the best odds do not always influence people to make the correct decision.

人们对风险的不同看法，或许比风险的基本事实更为重要。听到降雨概率为60%的预报，或认识到因接种流感疫苗而生病的可能性微乎其微，大多数人都明白正确的应对方式：带上雨伞，接种流感疫苗。但有些人出于恐惧，会作出不理性的极端反应，留在家中并避免与任何人接触。我们每个人偶尔都会作出不理性的决定。面对风险时，即使概率最有利，也不一定能促使人们作出正确决定。

DIn her book, The Universe and the Teacup, the science writer K. C. Cole reminds us that the risks of losing something and gaining something appear to be calculated in our minds according to quite different scales. People will risk a lot to prevent a loss, she notes; some people will rush into a burning building to save a pet. But they will usually risk very little if the only perceived outcome is a possible gain; many people won’t even risk pursuing an exciting job, if the current job, though unfulfilling, remains comfortable. That psychological mechanism, a desire to maintain the status quo, helps explain the high hurdles that any rational risk assessment must confront.

科学作家 K. C. Cole 在《宇宙与茶杯》（The Universe and the Teacup）一书中提醒我们，失去某物的风险与得到某物的机会，似乎会在我们头脑中按照截然不同的尺度来计算。她指出，人们愿意冒很大风险来避免损失；有些人会冲进着火的建筑营救宠物。但如果他们认为唯一可能的结果只是获得某种好处，通常就只愿承担很小风险。即使现有工作不能令人满足，只要还算舒适，许多人甚至不愿冒险追求一份令人兴奋的新工作。这种希望维持现状的心理机制，有助于解释任何理性风险评估都必须跨越的重重障碍。

EOrganizations face the same hurdles whenever they must cope with major shifts in policy. In his classic examination of the character of high-tech industry, Only the Paranoid Survive, Andrew S. Grove gives a perceptive account of a crisis that took place during his tenure as Chairman of the Intel Corporation. The business environment for Intel and its competitors was transformed by a flood of low-cost memory chips from Japan, and Intel made the difficult decision to abandon its chip-making business. Such a period of change in the outside world is filled with danger, Grove notes, precisely because it is so easy for executives to try to disregard it or misinterpret the mounting evidence showing the profound risks of not making change at the very core. The distinction Cole cites is instructive here: one reason management and staff may not act is that they tend to focus on the potential gains of risking change (which they characterize as small), rather than on the catastrophic losses that risking change could prevent. The period becomes critical for a company, according to Grove, and deadly when unattended to.

每当机构必须应对重大政策转变时，也会面临同样的障碍。Andrew S. Grove 在考察高科技产业特征的经典著作《只有偏执狂才能生存》（Only the Paranoid Survive）中，深刻讲述了他担任英特尔公司（Intel Corporation）董事长期间发生的一场危机。来自日本的低价存储芯片大量涌入，彻底改变了 Intel 及其竞争者的商业环境，Intel 因而作出艰难决定，放弃芯片制造业务。Grove 指出，外部世界发生这种变化的时期充满危险，原因恰恰是高管很容易试图忽视或误读不断增加的证据；这些证据显示，不从核心作出改变会带来深重风险。Cole 提到的区别在这里很有启发性：管理层和员工可能不采取行动，一个原因是他们往往关注冒险变革可能带来的收益，并把收益说得很小，却不去关注冒险变革可以防止的灾难性损失。Grove 认为，这个时期对企业至关重要；若置之不理，则会致命。

FLikewise, at the national level, and even the global one, institutions tend to be more willing to take a risk if it means avoiding a loss, than they are if it might confer a gain. Almost everyone, for instance, sees the signs of ecological decay. Reid J. Lifset’s article, ‘Full Accounting’, describes newly developed techniques for clarifying which critical decision would have the highest pay-offs for the environment. Once these ‘leverage points’ as he calls them, are located, many environmental organizations will be willing to risk the resources needed to prevent further deterioration.

同样，在国家乃至全球层面，如果承担风险意味着避免损失，机构往往比风险可能带来收益时更愿意冒险。例如，几乎人人都看到了生态恶化的迹象。Reid J. Lifset 的文章《全面核算》（Full Accounting）介绍了新近开发的技术，用来明确哪项关键决策能给环境带来最高回报。他把这些关键着力点称为“杠杆点”（leverage points）；一旦找到它们，许多环境组织就会愿意冒险投入防止环境进一步恶化所需的资源。

GMore complicated are decisions by nations to invest broadly in educational research. Some governmental leaders are oblivious to the risks posed by social under-investment, because the urgent needs of the present take precedence over actions that would give rise to even more substantial gains in the future. Their attitude is that today’s risks must be manageable, and that tomorrow’s gains are at the very least difficult to measure. Better consideration and decision making for what lies ahead is essential.

更复杂的是各国是否决定广泛投资教育研究。一些政府领导人没有意识到社会投资不足带来的风险，因为眼前的迫切需求优先于那些会在未来产生更大收益的行动。他们认为，眼前的风险肯定是可控的，而未来的收益至少很难衡量。对未来作出更周全的考量和决策至关重要。

HNevertheless, I confess to being an optimist. My glass is generally half full – though that may not help me handle risk. Whether one’s temperament is optimistic or pessimistic, everyone in our scientific and engineering community has a social responsibility: measure with care and portray with honesty the level of risk involved in any action or decision.

尽管如此，我承认自己是个乐观主义者。在我看来，杯子通常是半满的——不过，这也许无助于我应对风险。无论一个人的性情是乐观还是悲观，我们科学与工程界的每个人都负有一项社会责任：认真衡量任何行动或决定涉及的风险程度，并如实呈现。
