Q14 · 正确标题未作答

How the number of decision-makers affects the decision

决策者数量如何影响决策

你的标题

未作答

AIt has long been held that decisions made collectively by large groups of people are more likely to turn out to be accurate than decisions made by individuals. The idea goes back to the ‘jury theorem’ of Nicolas de Condorcet, an 18th-century French philosopher who was one of the first to apply mathematics to the social sciences. Condorcet’s theory describes collective decisions, outlining how democratic decisions tend to outperform dictatorial ones. If, for example, each member of a jury has only partial information, the majority decision is more likely to be correct than a decision arrived at by a single juror. Moreover, the probability of a correct decision increases with the size of the jury.

长期以来，人们一直认为，由一大群人共同作出的决定，比个人作出的决定更有可能是准确的。这一观点可追溯至18世纪法国哲学家 Nicolas de Condorcet 提出的“陪审团定理”（jury theorem）；他是最早将数学应用于社会科学的人之一。Condorcet 的理论描述了集体决策，并阐明民主决策往往优于独裁决策。例如，如果陪审团的每名成员都只掌握部分信息，那么多数人的决定比单个陪审员作出的决定更可能正确。此外，正确决策的概率会随着陪审团规模的扩大而提高。

Q15 · 正确标题未作答

Signals used by certain insects to indicate a discovery

某些昆虫用来表明有所发现的信号

你的标题

未作答

BNow it is becoming clear that group decisions are also extremely valuable for the success of social animals, such as ants, bees, birds and dolphins. Bees make collective decisions, and they do it rather well, according to Christian List of the London School of Economics, who has studied group decision-making in humans and animals. Researchers led by Dr List looked at colonies once the original colony reached a certain size. The queen goes off with about two-thirds of the worker bees to live in a new home or nest, leaving a daughter queen in the old nest with the remaining workers. Among the bees that depart are some that have searched for and found some new nest sites, and reported back using a characteristic body movement known as a ‘waggle dance’ to indicate to the other bees the suitable places they have located. The longer the dance, the better the site. After a while, other bees start to visit the sites signalled by their companions to see for themselves and, on their return, also perform more waggle dances. The process eventually leads to a consensus on the best site and the breakaway swarm migrates. The decision is remarkably reliable, with the bees choosing the best site even when there are only small differences between alternative sites.

如今越来越清楚的是，群体决策对蚂蚁、蜜蜂、鸟类和海豚等社会性动物能否成功生存也极为重要。伦敦政治经济学院（London School of Economics）的 Christian List 研究过人类和动物的群体决策；据他说，蜜蜂会共同作出决定，而且做得相当好。List 博士带领研究人员观察了蜂群达到一定规模后的情况。蜂王会带着约三分之二的工蜂离开，前往新居或新巢，并把一只子代蜂王和其余工蜂留在旧巢。离开的蜜蜂中，有些已经搜寻并找到数个新巢址；它们回来后会用一种称为“摇摆舞”（waggle dance）的特有身体动作报告发现，以此向其他蜜蜂指示合适的地点。舞跳得越久，说明地点越好。过一阵子，其他蜜蜂开始亲自前往同伴所指示的地点察看，回来后也会跳更多摇摆舞。这个过程最终使蜂群就最佳地点达成共识，分出的蜂群随后迁往那里。这种决策非常可靠，即使备选地点之间只有细微差别，蜜蜂也能选出最佳地点。

CBut exactly how do bees reach such a robust consensus? To find out, Dr List and his colleagues used a computer-generated model of the decision-making process. By experimenting with it, they found that, when bees in the model were very good at finding nesting sites but did not share their information, this dramatically slowed down the migration, leaving the swarm homeless and vulnerable. Conversely, bees in the model blindly followed the waggle dances of others without first checking. The researchers concluded that the ability of bees to identify successfully and quickly the best site depends on both the bees’ interdependence in communicating the whereabouts of the best site, and their independence in confirming this information for themselves.

但蜜蜂究竟如何达成如此稳固的共识？为了找出答案，List 博士及其同事使用了一个由计算机生成的决策过程模型。通过对模型进行试验，他们发现，当模型中的蜜蜂很善于寻找巢址，却不共享信息时，迁徙速度会大幅减慢，使蜂群无家可归且容易受到伤害。相反，在另一种情况下，模型中的蜜蜂没有先行核查，就盲目跟随其他蜜蜂的摇摆舞。研究人员得出结论：蜜蜂能否成功而迅速地找出最佳地点，既取决于它们相互依靠、交流最佳地点的位置，也取决于它们独立地亲自核实这一信息。

The effect of man-made imitations on insects

人工仿制物对昆虫的影响

DAnother situation in which collective decisions are taken occurs when animals are either isolated from crucial sources of information or dominated by other members of the group. Jose Halloy of the Free University of Brussels in Belgium used robotic cockroaches to subvert the behaviour of living cockroaches and control their decision-making process. In his experiment, the artificial bugs were introduced to the live ones and soon became sufficiently socially integrated that they were perceived by the real cockroaches as equals. By manipulating the robots, which were in the minority, Halloy was able to persuade the living cockroaches to choose an inappropriate shelter—even one which they had rejected before being infiltrated by the robots.

动物无法接触关键信息来源，或受群体中其他成员支配时，也会出现集体决策的情况。比利时布鲁塞尔自由大学（Free University of Brussels）的 Jose Halloy 利用机器蟑螂改变活蟑螂原有的行为，并控制它们的决策过程。在他的实验中，机器虫被放入活蟑螂之中，很快便充分融入其社会，以至于真蟑螂把它们视为同类。Halloy 通过操纵处于少数的机器蟑螂，成功说服活蟑螂选择不合适的藏身处，甚至选择它们在机器蟑螂混入之前曾拒绝过的地方。

EThe way insects put into effect collective decisions can be complex and as important as the decisions themselves. At the University of Bristol, in the UK, Nigel Franks and his colleagues studied how a species of ant establishes a new nest. Franks and his associates reported how the insects reduce the problems associated with making a necessarily swift choice. If the ants’ existing nest becomes suddenly threatened, the insects choose certain ants to act as scouts to find a new nest. How quickly they accomplish the transfer to a new home depends not only on how soon the best available site is found, but also on how quickly the migration there can be achieved.

昆虫落实集体决定的方式可能很复杂，而且与决定本身同样重要。在英国布里斯托大学（University of Bristol），Nigel Franks 及其同事研究了某种蚂蚁如何建立新巢。Franks 及其同事说明了这些昆虫如何减少必须迅速作出选择所带来的问题。如果蚂蚁现有的巢穴突然受到威胁，它们会选出一些蚂蚁担任侦察员，去寻找新巢。它们能多快完成搬迁，不仅取决于多快找到可选地点中最好的一个，也取决于多快能够迁到那里。

Q19 · 正确标题未作答

The need to instruct additional insect guides

指导更多昆虫充当引路者的必要性

你的标题

未作答

FOnce the suitable new nest is identified, the chosen ants begin to lead others, which have made it to the new site or which may simply be in the vicinity, back to the original threatened nest. In this way, those ants which are familiar with the route can help transport, for example, the queen and young ants to the new site, and simultaneously show the way to those ants which have been left behind to guard the old nest. In this way, moving processes are accomplished faster and more efficiently. Thus the dynamics of collective decision-making are closely related to the efficient implementation of those decisions. How this might apply to choices that humans make is, as yet, unclear. But it does suggest, even for humans, the importance of recruiting dynamic leaders to a cause, because the most important thing about collective decision-making, as shown by these insect experiments, is to get others to follow.

一旦确定合适的新巢，获选的蚂蚁就开始把已经到达新地点或恰好在附近的其他蚂蚁带回受到威胁的原巢。这样一来，熟悉路线的蚂蚁便能协助把蚁后和幼蚁等运往新址，同时也为留下守卫旧巢的蚂蚁指路。迁巢过程因而完成得更快、更高效。因此，集体决策的运作方式与这些决定能否得到高效执行密切相关。这一点如何适用于人类作出的选择，目前尚不清楚。但它确实提示，即使对人类而言，为一项事业招募行动力强的领导者也很重要，因为这些昆虫实验表明，集体决策最重要的一点，就是让其他成员跟随。
