Q27 · 正确标题未作答

A new approach to sense-of-place research

地方感研究的新方法

你的标题

未作答

AHuman beings naturally become attached to places they visit or inhabit and these emotional attachments have become increasingly important in research on recreation sites and activities. Research into this phenomenon is called ‘sense-of-place research’. This research has employed a variety of approaches to gauge people’s feelings toward a place, including surveys and personal interviews, but so far has not used photo-based methods. However, Visitor Employed Photography (VEP), used to capture visitor perceptions of landscape and recreational quality, represents a potential innovation in sense-of-place research.

人类会自然地依恋自己到访或居住的地方；在休闲场所与活动研究中，这种情感依恋变得越来越重要。对这一现象的研究称为“地方感研究”（sense-of-place research）。研究人员使用过多种方法衡量人们对某个地方的感受，包括问卷调查和个人访谈，但到目前为止尚未使用以照片为基础的方法。访客自主摄影法（Visitor Employed Photography，VEP）用于捕捉访客对景观和休闲品质的认识，可能为地方感研究带来一种创新。

Q28 · 正确标题未作答

Defining the significance of places

界定地方的意义

你的标题

未作答

BA ‘place’ is a setting that we give meaning to based on the personal experiences, relationships and feelings we associate with it. A crucial distinction that needs to be made is between the more subjective concept of attachment and the symbolic ‘meanings’ or labels we use to describe the type of place a setting represents. Is, for example, a multiple-use forest area a wilderness? A playground? A workplace? Symbolic meanings are important in that they form the basis of our attachment to a place: we attribute meaning to our settings, and in turn become attached to the meanings (Stedman, 2003). All settings can have multiple meanings depending on how we encounter them. Some researchers suggest that, because meaning emerges through individual experience, for example ‘my camping trip’, place meanings are completely individualistic: a given setting such as a park will contain as many different meanings as there are people using the setting (Meinig, 1979). Others, however, (e.g. Greider & Garkovich, 1994) assert that meanings are based on social categories and therefore are shared by others within these categories. For example, farmers share certain meanings for a plot of land that are distinct from those of real-estate developers or hunters.

“地方”是一个环境；我们根据与之相连的个人经历、人际关系和感受，赋予这个环境意义。这里必须作出一个关键区分：一边是较为主观的依恋概念，另一边是我们用来描述某个环境所代表地方类型的象征性“意义”或标签。例如，一片具有多种用途的森林地区究竟是荒野、游乐场，还是工作场所？象征性意义十分重要，因为它们构成我们依恋某个地方的基础：我们赋予周围环境意义，继而又依恋这些意义（Stedman，2003）。 所有环境都可能具有多重意义，具体取决于我们如何接触它们。一些研究人员认为，由于意义产生于个人经历，例如“我的露营之旅”，地方意义完全因人而异：公园等特定环境所包含的不同意义，会与使用这个环境的人数一样多（Meinig，1979）。但另一些研究人员，例如 Greider 和 Garkovich（1994），则主张意义建立在社会类别之上，因此会由同一类别中的人共同拥有。例如，农民对一块土地所共有的某些意义，不同于房地产开发商或猎人赋予它的意义。

Q29 · 正确标题未作答

The relevance of time to the sense of belonging to a place

时间与地方归属感的关系

你的标题

未作答

CClearly, place attachment is built through familiarity with a place over a period of months or even years. Relph (1976) describes sense-of-place attachment as the steady accumulation of events within a setting; this creates ‘home places’. According to this view, those who have participated fully in the life of the home or community, or have accumulated a series of everyday events in a setting, will have the strongest attachment to it. Extended residence in a place tends to make us feel toward it almost as a living thing, affecting our emotions in the same way as a family would (Ryden, 1993). However, Tuan (1977) notes that a sense of place may also develop quite rapidly in ‘chosen places’, where dramatic landscapes and intense experiences can lead to an immediate attachment. Indeed, many settings, especially those that attract visitors, may simultaneously exist as home places and chosen places.

显然，对地方的依恋，是在数月甚至数年间逐渐熟悉某个地方而形成的。Relph（1976）把地方感中的依恋描述为某个环境中各种事件的不断积累，这会形成“家园之地”（home places）。按照这种观点，那些充分参与家园或社区生活，或在某个环境中积累了一系列日常事件的人，会对那里产生最强的依恋。长期居住在一个地方，往往会让我们几乎把它当作有生命的事物来感受；它对我们情绪的影响，就像家人一样（Ryden，1993）。不过，Tuan（1977）指出，在“自选之地”（chosen places），地方感也可能很快形成；令人震撼的景观和强烈的经历可以让人立即产生依恋。 事实上，许多地方，尤其是吸引访客的地方，可能同时既是家园之地，也是自选之地。

Q30 · 正确标题未作答

The advantages of photography in sense-of-place research

摄影在地方感研究中的优势

你的标题

未作答

DClearly we are dealing with a complex phenomenon and photo-based research methods may help us to understand it better. In VEP, tourists are asked to take photographs. This technique has primarily been used to assess the perceptions of visitors to parks and recreation places. Haywood (1990) describes several benefits of VEP. Photography is an enjoyable, familiar activity to tourists which helps to sharpen observation and identify specific locations that are important. It can give clearer ideas on elements that are liked or disliked and also facilitates comparisons between places.

显然，我们面对的是一种复杂现象，而基于照片的研究方法或许能帮助我们更好地理解它。在访客自主摄影法（VEP）中，游客会被要求拍照。这种技术主要用于评估游客对公园和休闲场所的认识。Haywood（1990）介绍了 VEP 的几项益处。摄影是游客熟悉且乐于参与的活动，能帮助他们更敏锐地观察，并找出重要的具体地点。它可以更清楚地显示人们喜欢或不喜欢哪些要素，也便于比较不同地方。

Q31 · 正确标题未作答

Important considerations when using VEP

使用 VEP 时的重要注意事项

你的标题

未作答

EHowever, several methodological issues need to be taken into account. First, who should take the photographs? VEP research typically involves visitors or tourists but, when applied to questions of attachment to a community, this approach has potential pitfalls. Chenoweth (1984) notes that research subjects may take photos that represent only a part of their entire recreational experience. This tendency probably relates to unfamiliarity with the setting. For example, when researchers assign the task of photographing a travel route with which respondents are not familiar, participants may save too many pictures and then use them all up at the end of their visit, even if there is no suitable material. Markwell (1997) noted an opposite tendency in his study of pictures taken on a nature tour: beginnings of excursions were over-represented, due perhaps to the initial novelty of the trip. Furthermore, Haywood (1990) suggests that compressing the photo-taking period into a single day (as he did in his work) may result in an over-representation of tourist icons rather than ordinary places. In contrast, Yamashita (2002), when focusing on local residents’ perceptions of the qualities of the water around them, noted that residents may have more difficulty expressing visual appeal than visitors, precisely because they are insiders and less conscious of aesthetic qualities. When addressing complex attachment to landscape, we would expect, however, that familiarity ought to increase the validity of the items selected to represent sources of attachment. We also expect that photographs taken by local residents will represent a wider range of phenomena than pictures taken by transient visitors.

不过，还需要考虑几个方法问题。首先，照片应由谁来拍？VEP 研究通常由访客或游客参与，但把这种方法用于社区依恋问题时，可能会遇到一些问题。Chenoweth（1984）指出，研究对象拍摄的照片可能只代表其全部休闲体验的一部分。这种倾向或许与他们不熟悉环境有关。例如，研究人员让受访者拍摄一条自己不熟悉的旅行路线时，参与者可能把过多的拍摄次数留到后面，等到游览结束时才全部拍完，即使当时已没有合适的内容可拍。 Markwell（1997）在研究自然之旅中拍摄的照片时，发现了相反倾向：出游开始阶段的照片过多，或许是因为旅程初期仍有新鲜感。此外，Haywood（1990）认为，把拍照时间压缩到一天之内，就像他在研究中所做的那样，可能导致旅游地标而非普通地点在照片中过度出现。 相比之下，Yamashita（2002）研究当地居民如何看待周围水域的品质时发现，居民可能比访客更难表达景观在视觉上的吸引力，恰恰因为他们长期置身其中，反而较少留意这些审美特征。不过，在探究人们对景观的复杂依恋时，我们仍可以预期：对地方越熟悉，选出的拍摄对象就越能准确体现依恋的来源。 我们还预计，当地居民拍摄的照片所代表的现象，会比短暂停留的访客所拍照片更为广泛。

Q32 · 正确标题未作答

Making sense of photographic studies

解读摄影研究

你的标题

未作答

FBut how should the photographs be interpreted? Goin (2001) notes that with every photo taken ‘a fiction is created … but presents to the uninformed an overwhelming conviction of fact’ (p. 363). By implication, what photos appear to be and what they really represent may be very different things, and some follow-up helps to uncover the intended meanings of the participant. Yamashita (2002) notes the utility of asking respondents to provide descriptions of each photo in a notebook or diary. These elaborations are helpful, but in cases of complex phenomena, an interview may help participants clarify their intentions (Markwell, 1997).

但是，照片应当如何解释？Goin（2001）指出，每拍下一张照片，“就创造了一个虚构之物……但它会让不了解情况的人深信那是事实”（第363页）。这意味着，照片看起来是什么与它实际代表什么，可能是截然不同的两回事；进行一些后续了解，有助于揭示参与者想要表达的意义。Yamashita（2002）指出，让受访者在笔记本或日记中为每张照片写下说明，是一种有用的做法。这些补充解释很有帮助，但面对复杂现象时，访谈或许更能帮助参与者说明自己的意图（Markwell，1997）。
