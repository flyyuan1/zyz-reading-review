Socotra Island

索科特拉岛

Situated in the Arabian Sea off the Arabian Peninsula is the Socotra archipelago, a remnant of an ancient landmass that became separated from Africa and Arabia 18 million years ago.

索科特拉群岛（Socotra archipelago）位于阿拉伯半岛外的阿拉伯海，是一片古老陆块的残余；这片陆块在1,800万年前与非洲和阿拉伯分离。

When scientists began the first substantial exploration of the islands 30 years ago, they found a fascinating collection of unusual plants and animals - a unique combination of 'relict' populations (organisms that have become extinct elsewhere) and species that have evolved to cope with the island's harsh conditions. In fact, almost a third of its plant species and up to 80 percent of its terrestrial animals are found nowhere else.

30年前，科学家首次开始对这些岛屿进行大规模考察，发现了一批引人入胜的奇特动植物：这里既有“孑遗”种群（在其他地方已经灭绝的生物），也有为适应岛上严酷条件而演化出的物种，两者形成了独特的组合。事实上，岛上近三分之一的植物物种和多达80%的陆生动物，在世界其他地方都找不到。

Life in the arid conditions is harsh for the people living there too. Isolated from the mainland by hundreds of kilometres, and with monsoons that rage for several months every year, the islanders were historically self-sufficient - relying only on what they themselves could produce to survive. There are floods and droughts, and many islanders lack adequate water resources and nutrition. Medical care and education are limited.

对当地居民来说，在这种干旱环境中生活同样艰苦。岛屿与大陆相隔数百公里，而且每年都有持续数月的猛烈季风，因此岛民过去一直自给自足，只能依靠自己生产的物资生存。这里既有洪水，也有干旱；许多岛民缺少充足的水资源和营养，医疗和教育也很有限。

However, the main island, Socotra, is experiencing significant change. A rapid development programme has been instituted that will have far-reaching impacts on the island's people and the environment for decades to come. In the past, the only way to get to Socotra was by travelling for several days on a boat, but irregular flights began touching down on dirt landing strips during the late 1960s. By 1999, a runway had been constructed on Socotra that was capable of taking larger aircraft all year round, and since 2000 the capital, Hadibo, has seen huge expansion. New schools have been built, and a modern terminal was added to the airport, through which an increasing number of tourists are beginning to pass. And there are plans to construct new harbour facilities that will enable fishermen to sell their catch more easily, and also make it simpler for local people to have food, and bricks and timber for construction projects brought to the island.

然而，主岛索科特拉岛正在经历重大变化。一项快速发展计划已经启动，未来几十年都将对岛上居民和环境产生深远影响。过去，前往索科特拉的唯一方式是乘船航行数日；到20世纪60年代后期，才有班次不固定的航班开始在土质跑道上降落。到1999年，岛上已经建成了一条全年都能供较大的飞机起降的跑道；自2000年以来，首府哈迪布（Hadibo）大规模扩张。新学校已经落成，机场也增设了现代化航站楼，开始有越来越多游客经此出入。此外，岛上还计划新建港口设施，让渔民更容易出售渔获，也让居民更方便地把食品以及建筑工程所需的砖块和木材运到岛上。

However, although these improvements are significant, it’s the continuing development of Socotra’s road system that is having the greatest impact on the islanders. Until 1991, a few dirt tracks, navigable only by four-wheel drive, gave access to part of the island. Otherwise it was only possible to reach most areas on foot, or with a donkey. By 2001, however, there was an extensive network of unpaved car tracks, and the following year a tarmac road, the first of its kind on Socotra, connecting the airport to Hadibo, was completed. Now, a road traverses the Diksam plateau and continues on to the south coast, making essential services more accessible to a large number of villagers.

不过，尽管这些改善十分显著，对岛民影响最大的还是索科特拉公路系统的持续发展。直到1991年，岛上只有几条必须驾驶四轮驱动车才能通行的土路，只能到达部分地区。除此之外，前往大多数地区只能步行或骑驴。不过，到2001年，岛上已有广泛的未铺装车道网络；次年，索科特拉第一条柏油路完工，把机场与哈迪布连接起来。如今，一条公路横穿 Diksam 高原并继续通往南部海岸，使许多村民更容易获得基本服务。

There is no doubt that the development of Socotra’s infrastructure has brought many benefits to the island's inhabitants, but there is growing concern that the speed with which it is taking place is so rapid that it will be difficult for local people to maintain certain traditions. There is also the fear that the developments will threaten the island's unique ecosystems. Building new roads and harbours may lead to the introduction of alien species to Socotra Island (as has already happened with the Indian house crow), damage to the sea's biodiversity through the increased collection and sale of coral, and tourist developments on an inappropriate and unsustainable scale.

毫无疑问，索科特拉的基础设施建设给岛民带来了许多好处；但人们越来越担心，建设推进得如此迅速，当地居民将很难维持某些传统。人们也担心，这些开发会威胁岛上独特的生态系统。修建新道路和港口可能把外来物种带入索科特拉岛（印度家鸦已经是一个先例）；珊瑚采集和销售增加，可能破坏海洋生物多样性；旅游开发也可能达到不恰当且不可持续的规模。

Problems were also caused by increasing access to sensitive areas. For example, cutting the road along the coast caused considerable damage to the stunning scenery. However, some steps were then taken to limit the damage caused by further development. For example, biodiversity project officers were consulted in the planning stages of the road that crosses the centre of Socotra, so it avoids some of the most scenic areas and sites that contain plants only found in a limited number of locations. It is an area which is also home to the greatest concentrations of dragon's blood trees and supports many endemic animals.

进入生态敏感地区变得更容易，也造成了问题。例如，沿海岸开辟公路，严重破坏了当地壮丽的景色。不过，此后人们采取了一些措施，限制进一步开发带来的破坏。例如，在规划横穿索科特拉中部的公路时，建设方征询了生物多样性项目人员的意见，因此公路避开了一些风景最优美的地区，以及生长着少数地点才有的植物的区域。这一带还集中分布着数量最多的龙血树，并为许多当地特有动物提供栖息地。

An agreement has now been reached about all future construction of roads. To protect the island's ecologically sensitive areas, any proposed road will be subject to a report assessing its environmental impact prior to construction. The challenge has been to bring the benefits of development to Socotra without damaging the environment, which supports the people's way of life and will form the basis of future development on the island.

如今，各方已经就未来所有公路建设达成协议。为了保护岛上的生态敏感地区，任何拟建公路都必须在施工前接受环境影响评估并形成报告。面临的挑战，是在不破坏环境的前提下让索科特拉享受发展带来的好处；当地环境既支撑着居民的生活方式，也将成为岛屿未来发展的基础。
