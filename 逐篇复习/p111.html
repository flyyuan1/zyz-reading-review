The latest in a conflicting series of studies suggests calorie restriction could potentially slow ageing in humans

一系列结论相互冲突的研究又有了最新结果：限制热量摄入或许能延缓人类衰老。

ACalorie restriction, or ‘semi-starvation’ as some refer to it, has been proven to extend lifespan in many living organisms from yeast to mice, but the picture for primates, including humans, is not so clear. Research published by a team at the University of Wisconsin in the United States shows that rhesus monkeys also live longer on a calorie-restricted diet. But those findings disagree with research by the National Institute on Aging (NIA) in Maryland, also in the United States. Rozalyn Anderson, of the Wisconsin team, says the research is not intended as a recommendation of calorie restriction. ‘I find the idea monumentally unattractive!’ she says. ‘We study it because it is so effective at delaying ageing and the onset of age-related disease. It’s a way to tease out what it is that creates increased disease vulnerability as a function of age.’

已有研究证明，限制热量摄入，即有些人所说的“半饥饿”，能够延长从酵母到小鼠等许多生物的寿命，但对于包括人类在内的灵长类动物，情况并没有这么清楚。美国威斯康星大学（University of Wisconsin）的一支团队发表的研究显示，采用限制热量饮食的猕猴（rhesus monkeys）也会活得更久。但这些发现与同在美国、位于马里兰州（Maryland）的美国国家老龄研究所（National Institute on Aging，NIA）的研究结果不一致。威斯康星团队的 Rozalyn Anderson 说，这项研究并非意在建议人们限制热量摄入。她说：“我觉得这种做法一点也不吸引人！我们研究它，是因为它在延缓衰老和年龄相关疾病的出现方面非常有效。借助这种方法，我们可以弄清楚究竟是什么原因使人随着年龄增长而更容易患病。”

BBoth groups started long-term trials on rhesus monkeys in the late 1980s to determine whether calorie restriction would extend the lifespan of primates. In mice, many experiments had come to the same finding: feed them a diet with 30% fewer calories and see a lifespan extension of 40%. The monkey trials were set up in a similar way: researchers took the calorie content of a standard monkey diet, cut it by 30% (while continuing to supply all essential nutrients) and monitored whether those monkeys lived longer, healthier lives than those on the standard diet.

20世纪80年代末，两个研究组都开始对猕猴进行长期试验，以确定限制热量摄入能否延长灵长类动物的寿命。在小鼠实验中，许多试验都得出了相同发现：喂食热量减少30%的饮食，寿命便会延长40%。猕猴试验的设计方式与此类似：研究人员以标准猕猴饮食的热量为基准，将其削减30%，同时继续供应所有必需营养物质，并观察这些猕猴是否比采用标准饮食的猕猴活得更久、更健康。

CIn 2009, with the monkeys approaching old age, preliminary findings of the two trials started coming in. For the Wisconsin monkeys, calorie restriction seemed to be working. Compared to well-fed control animals, the lean monkeys were living longer and suffering less from the diseases of ageing: diabetes, heart disease and brain diseases. However, in 2012 the NIA results emerged with a dramatically different conclusion: their monkeys were not living any longer than the controls, although they were healthier.

到2009年，随着猕猴日渐衰老，两项试验开始产生初步结果。对威斯康星的猕猴来说，限制热量摄入似乎奏效了。与食物充足的对照组动物相比，这些精瘦的猕猴寿命更长，也较少罹患与衰老有关的疾病，如糖尿病、心脏病和脑部疾病。然而，2012年公布的 NIA 结果却得出了截然不同的结论：他们的猕猴虽然更健康，但寿命并不比对照组长。

DThe Wisconsin group’s latest results confirm that their calorie-restricted monkeys are living longer than the controls. They also offer a possible explanation of why the two groups’ findings don’t agree, and that lies in the treatment of the control group. The Wisconsin study began with monkeys in early adulthood. Initially, all the monkeys were allowed to eat as much as they liked. A few months into the trial, the monkeys were placed into one of two groups: the controls (who continued to be fed as much as they wished) and the calorie-restricted monkeys (who were given an individualised diet of 30% less than they were previously eating).

威斯康星研究组的最新结果证实，他们限制热量摄入的猕猴比对照组活得更久。他们还为两组研究结果为何不一致提供了一个可能解释，原因就在于对照组所接受的处理。威斯康星的研究从刚进入成年期的猕猴开始。最初，所有猕猴都可以随意进食。试验开始几个月后，猕猴被分入两组中的一组：对照组继续想吃多少就喂多少，而限制热量组得到的是个性化饮食，热量比它们此前的摄入量少30%。

EThe NIA study differed in two ways. First, the control group of monkeys were not allowed to eat as much as they wished. They were given a diet considered to represent a normal calorie count, while the calorie-restricted monkeys were fed 30% less than that. Second, whereas the Wisconsin monkeys were given highly processed food high in sucrose, making it easy to standardise, the NIA diet was based on whole grains, fish oils, and was very low in sugar. These different settings for the normal control diet may provide an explanation of why the two groups showed different results.

NIA 的研究有两处不同。第一，对照组猕猴不能随意进食。研究人员给它们喂食被视为正常热量水平的饮食，而限制热量组猕猴的饮食热量比这一水平低30%。第二，威斯康星的猕猴吃的是蔗糖含量高、便于统一标准的高度加工食品，NIA 的饮食则以全谷物和鱼油为基础，而且糖含量很低。正常对照饮食的这些不同设置，或许可以解释两组研究为何得出了不同结果。

FThe Wisconsin group may in effect have studied the effects of ‘overeating’. Their control animals weighed up to 10% more than average for their age and gender. Compared to them, the calorie-restricted animals not only suffered fewer diseases, they lived longer. Julie Mattison, head of the NIA study, observes that an overweight person who goes to a fast food restaurant every day will obviously benefit if their calories are cut back by 30%.

威斯康星研究组实际上研究的可能是“过量进食”的影响。他们的对照组动物体重最高比同年龄、同性别动物的平均值高10%。相比之下，限制热量组的动物不仅患病较少，寿命也更长。NIA 研究负责人 Julie Mattison 指出，一个超重的人如果每天都去快餐店吃饭，那么将其热量摄入削减30%，显然会令其受益。

GThe NIA study fed their control monkeys what they considered a ‘standard’ caloric intake and saw that longevity for monkeys that were fed 30% less was the same. But in Anderson’s view, the NIA control monkeys were not fed enough; the NIA was calorie restricting both groups of animals. Their older female control monkeys, for example, weighed nearly 20% less than the national average. Indeed, Anderson points out that several of the NIA control monkeys have lived past the age of 40, far exceeding the 27-year average lifespan for captive rhesus monkeys. ‘That’s the maximum lifespan ever detected for the species, so the idea that their intervention is doing nothing is really at odds with the data,’ says Anderson.

NIA 给对照组猕猴喂食他们认为属于“标准”热量摄入水平的饮食，并发现饮食热量低30%的猕猴寿命与其相同。但 Anderson 认为，NIA 的对照组猕猴进食量不足；NIA 其实同时限制了两组动物的热量摄入。例如，他们对照组中的年长雌性猕猴体重比全国平均值低近20%。Anderson 还指出，NIA 对照组有几只猕猴确实活过了40岁，远远超过圈养猕猴27年的平均寿命。Anderson 说：“这是迄今在该物种中观察到的最长寿命，所以说他们的干预毫无作用，实在与数据相抵触。”

HHowever, neither group had the right notion of a standard diet according to Leonie Heilbronn, who researches calorie restriction and healthy ageing at the University of Adelaide in Australia. Heilbronn points out that the NIA controls were a very healthy set of monkeys and leaner than a control monkey should be. On the other hand, she notes, the Wisconsin controls were a little bigger than they had to be. On balance, Heilbronn agrees with the Wisconsin researcher’s argument. ‘I think these studies suggest calorie restriction definitely will increase lifespan,’ she says.

不过，在澳大利亚阿德莱德大学（University of Adelaide）研究热量限制与健康衰老的 Leonie Heilbronn 认为，两组研究对标准饮食的理解都不正确。Heilbronn 指出，NIA 的对照组是一批非常健康的猕猴，而且比对照组猕猴应有的体形更瘦。另一方面，她也指出，威斯康星对照组猕猴又稍微偏胖了一些。综合来看，Heilbronn 赞同威斯康星研究人员的论点。她说：“我认为，这些研究表明限制热量摄入肯定会延长寿命。”

IBoth studies agree that cutting calories is beneficial to health — in both cases the calorie-restricted monkeys had fewer diseases related to ageing. As to the question of whether calorie restriction extends lifespan in primates, Anderson and Mattison are currently working together to compare the two studies’ raw data. They plan to co-publish a joint analysis. The current data conflict will ultimately provide deeper insights into ageing, Anderson predicts. ‘The fact the two studies were set up differently, asking the same question in different ways — I think we will gain maximally from that.’

两项研究都认同，减少热量摄入有益健康；在两项研究中，限制热量组的猕猴罹患衰老相关疾病的数量都较少。至于限制热量摄入能否延长灵长类动物寿命，Anderson 和 Mattison 目前正共同对两项研究的原始数据进行比较。她们计划共同发表一份联合分析。Anderson 预测，目前数据之间的冲突最终会让人们对衰老获得更深入的认识。“两项研究的设置不同，以不同方式探究同一个问题——我认为我们能最大限度地从中获益。”
