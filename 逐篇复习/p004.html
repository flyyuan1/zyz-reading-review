What is it and where does it come from?

它是什么，又来自哪里？

In the ancient world, the waxy grey substance we now refer to as ambergris was highly prized for its medicinal properties, and was widely used as a spice, which was believed to be an aphrodisiac when added to food or wine. Ambergris itself is pleasantly aromatic, especially when warmed, and it was also highly valued as a fixing agent in the making of perfume, since it enabled a scent to retain its fragrance for much longer than might otherwise have been possible. Most ambergris was found in the form of lumps floating on the surface of the sea, or washed up on the shores of tropical and temperate oceans. At one time, ambergris was worth its weight in gold, but there was much confusion about its origins.

在古代，人们十分珍视如今称为龙涎香（ambergris）的灰色蜡状物质，认为它具有药用价值。它还被广泛用作香料；人们相信，把它加入食物或酒中可以催情。龙涎香本身带有宜人的香气，加热后尤其如此。它在制作香水时也被视为珍贵的定香剂，因为它能使香味比原本保持得久得多。人们发现的龙涎香大多呈块状，漂浮在海面上，或被冲上热带和温带海域的岸边。龙涎香一度价值堪比同等重量的黄金，但当时人们对它的来源仍十分困惑。

Ambergris was known to the Arabs as ‘ambar’ and was originally called amber in the West in the Middle Ages. This eventually led to further confusion in the popular mind between ambergris and true amber, the mineral known to mineralogists as succinite, which is actually fossilised tree resin, and generally yellow in colour. Both substances were rare and costly, and both were associated with the sea, largely because for Europeans the most common source of amber was the shores of the Baltic. In Chapter 92 of Moby Dick, the American writer Herman Melville pours scorn on those who believed the two substances to be the same: ‘Though the word ambergris is but the French compound for grey amber, yet the two substances are quite distinct. For amber, though at times found on the sea-coast, is also dug up in some far-inland soils, whereas ambergris is never found except upon the sea. Besides, amber is a hard, transparent, brittle, odourless substance, used for mouth-pieces to pipes, for beads and ornaments; but ambergris is soft, waxy, and so highly fragrant that it is largely used in perfumery.’

阿拉伯人称龙涎香为“ambar”，中世纪的西方最初则称它为“amber”。这最终使普通人更加混淆龙涎香和真正的琥珀。真正的琥珀是矿物学家所称的 succinite，实际上是树脂形成的化石，通常呈黄色。这两种物质都稀有而昂贵，也都与海洋有关，主要是因为对欧洲人而言，最常见的琥珀产地是波罗的海沿岸。美国作家 Herman Melville 在《白鲸》（Moby Dick）第92章中，对那些认为二者是同一种物质的人大加嘲讽：“虽然 ambergris 这个词不过是法语中‘灰色琥珀’的合成词，但这两种物质截然不同。琥珀有时会在海岸发现，却也能从远离海洋的内陆土壤中挖出；而龙涎香除海上之外，从未在别处发现。此外，琥珀坚硬、透明、易碎而且无味，可用来制作烟斗嘴、珠子和饰品；龙涎香则柔软、呈蜡状，香气极浓，因此大量用于香水制造。”

Moby Dick was published in 1851, by which time the mystery of the origins of ambergris had been resolved by the scientific community. In 1783, the botanist Joseph Banks, who had accompanied Captain James Cook on his voyages of discovery in the Pacific, presented a paper to the Royal Society of London by the German physician Dr Franz Xavier Schwediawer in which it was conclusively proved that ambergris came from sperm whales. In this, he was confirming an observation made in the 13ᵗʰ century by the great Venetian traveller Marco Polo who, while on the island of Socotra in the Indian Ocean, had witnessed a sperm whale vomiting up ambergris. But whereas Marco Polo imagined that the whale had swallowed the lump in the depths of the sea, Schwediawer showed that the origin of the material was inside the whale itself.

《白鲸》出版于1851年；到那时，科学界已经解开龙涎香来源之谜。1783年，曾随 James Cook 船长在太平洋进行探索航行的植物学家 Joseph Banks，向伦敦皇家学会（Royal Society of London）提交了一篇由德国医生 Franz Xavier Schwediawer 博士撰写的论文。论文确凿证明龙涎香来自抹香鲸（sperm whales）。他由此证实了13世纪威尼斯著名旅行家马可·波罗（Marco Polo）的一项观察：马可·波罗在印度洋索科特拉岛（Socotra）时，曾亲眼看见一头抹香鲸吐出龙涎香。不过，马可·波罗以为鲸是在深海中吞下了那团物质，Schwediawer 则表明，它其实产生于鲸的体内。

The sperm whale is the largest of the odontocetes, or toothed whales. Males can grow up to 20 metres in length. Melville described the sperm whale as ‘the king of whales’, and his novel Moby Dick is based on the pursuit of one such creature. Sperm whales are renowned for their ability to dive to great depths, possibly as far as 3 000 metres below the surface, and for remaining underwater for periods of two hours or more in pursuit of their favourite prey, the giant squid.

抹香鲸是齿鲸类中体型最大的物种，雄性体长可达20米。Melville 称抹香鲸为“鲸中之王”，他的小说《白鲸》正是围绕追捕这样一头鲸展开的。抹香鲸以深潜能力著称，可能潜至海面以下3,000米，并能在水下停留2小时或更久，追捕它们最喜爱的猎物——大王乌贼（giant squid）。

It is from the problems the whales have in digesting the beaks of such creatures that ambergris has its origins. The beak is sharp and irritates the whale’s lower intestine, which responds by producing a black, foul-smelling liquid. It is not clear to scientists whether this secretion should be considered a normal response by the whale’s digestive system or a pathological one, but from time to time large quantities of the liquid are vomited up by the whale. Once outside the whale’s body and exposed to air, the substance hardens, acquiring the waxy, greyish and pleasantly aromatic characteristics of ambergris. Often the beaks of squid are still found embedded in lumps of ambergris, some of which can weigh several hundred kilograms. Melville took some delight in contrasting the origins of ambergris with the high value placed upon it by refined society: ‘Who would think, then, that such fine ladies and gentlemen should regale themselves with an essence found in the inglorious bowels of a sick whale!’

龙涎香的形成，源于抹香鲸难以消化这类生物的喙。乌贼的喙十分锋利，会刺激鲸的下段肠道；肠道随即产生一种黑色、恶臭的液体。科学家尚不清楚，这种分泌究竟应视为鲸消化系统的正常反应，还是一种病理反应；但鲸会不时吐出大量这种液体。物质离开鲸体并接触空气后会变硬，逐渐具有龙涎香那种蜡质、灰白且气味宜人的特征。人们常能在龙涎香块中发现仍嵌在里面的乌贼喙，有些香块重达数百千克。Melville 颇为得意地把龙涎香的来源与上流社会赋予它的高价值作了对比：“那么，谁能想到，这些优雅的淑女绅士竟会享用一种取自病鲸那不光彩的肠腹之中的香精！”

Sperm whales were ruthlessly pursued by commercial whalers in the 19ᵗʰ and 20ᵗʰ centuries. In 1963-64 alone, almost 30 000 individuals were killed, and only the imposition of a ban on the hunting of sperm whales in 1984 saved the species from extinction. Ambergris was by far the most valuable product to be extracted during the processing of the whales’ carcasses, and over 90 per cent of the annual worldwide total was acquired in this way, as a by-product of commercial whaling. However, even before the ban on hunting sperm whales was imposed, the 1972 Marine Mammal Protection Act had prohibited trade in ambergris. Just as petroleum and plastic products were replacing other natural products of whaling, so ambergris was supplanted in the making of perfume by other materials, some natural and some synthetic in origin. Nevertheless, it is possible that, as sperm-whale populations recover to their former numbers in the wild, so the sight of lumps of ambergris washed ashore along the tide-line will once again become a familiar one to beach-combers the world over.

19世纪和20世纪，商业捕鲸者对抹香鲸进行了残酷捕杀。仅1963至1964年，就有近30,000头抹香鲸被杀；直到1984年实施抹香鲸禁捕令，才使这一物种免于灭绝。在处理鲸尸时提取的产品中，龙涎香价值远高于其他产品；全球每年获得的龙涎香有90%以上都以这种方式取得，是商业捕鲸的副产品。然而，早在抹香鲸禁捕令实施之前，1972年的《海洋哺乳动物保护法》（Marine Mammal Protection Act）就已禁止龙涎香贸易。正如石油和塑料制品逐渐取代捕鲸业的其他天然产品一样，在香水制造中，龙涎香也被其他材料取代，其中既有天然材料，也有合成材料。尽管如此，随着野生抹香鲸种群恢复到以往的数量，世界各地在海滩上寻觅物品的人，或许会再次经常见到一块块龙涎香被冲上岸边潮线的景象。
