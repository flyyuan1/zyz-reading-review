Nicholas Wade examines leaf-cutter ants and their amazing agriculture

Nicholas Wade 考察切叶蚁及其惊人的农业。

ALeaf-cutting ants and their fungus ‘farm’ are a marvel of nature and perhaps the best-known example of symbiosis — the mutual dependence of two species. Ants cultivate a mushroom-like fungus in ‘farms’. Both the ants and their so-called ‘agriculture’ have been extensively studied over the years, but recent research has uncovered intriguing new findings. Ants invented agriculture 50 million years before people did, and the leaf-cutters, members of the large attine ant family, practise the most sophisticated example of it. They grow their fungus in underground chambers that can reach the size of a football. A single leaf-cutter nest may contain a thousand such chambers, embedded in an underground metropolis up to 18 feet deep, and support a society of more than a million ants.

切叶蚁（leaf-cutting ants）和它们的真菌“农场”堪称自然奇观，或许也是共生关系最著名的例子。共生是两个物种相互依存的关系。蚂蚁在“农场”里培育一种类似蘑菇的真菌。多年来，蚂蚁及其所谓的“农业”一直受到广泛研究，而近期研究又揭示了一些耐人寻味的新发现。 蚂蚁比人类早5000万年发明农业；切叶蚁属于庞大的切叶蚁族（attine ants），实行着其中最精细复杂的农业形式。它们在地下洞室中培育真菌，每个洞室最大可达到一个足球那么大。单个切叶蚁巢穴可能有1000个这样的洞室，分布在深达18英尺的地下都市中，并供养一个拥有100多万只蚂蚁的社会。

BThese ant communities are the dominant plant-eaters of the Neotropics, the region comprising South and Central America, Mexico and the Caribbean. Biologists believe 15 percent of the leaf production of tropical forests disappears down the nests of leaf-cutter ants. In the nest, the leaves are shredded and added to the fungus, which digests the leaves and is in turn eaten by the ants. The attine ants’ achievement is remarkable because it allows them to consume, courtesy of their mushroom’s digestive powers, the harvest of tropical forests whose leaves are laden with poisonous chemicals.

这些蚂蚁群落是新热带区（Neotropics）占主导地位的植食动物。新热带区包括南美洲、中美洲、墨西哥和加勒比地区。生物学家认为，热带森林所产出的叶片中，有15%被切叶蚁搬进巢穴。叶片在巢内被撕碎并加入真菌；真菌消化叶片，随后又被蚂蚁吃掉。切叶蚁族的这项成就非同寻常，因为借助蘑菇的消化能力，它们得以享用热带森林的收成，而那些叶片中含有大量有毒化学物质。

CThere are more than 200 known species of the attine ant tribe, divided into 12 groups, or genera. The leaf-cutters use fresh vegetation; the other groups, known as the lower attines because their nests are smaller and their techniques more primitive, feed their gardens with similar leaves that have fallen on the ground and insects that lie on the forest floor. Lower attine ants are all a similar size. However, leaf-cutter worker ants come in made-to-fit sizes — large ants to saw off leaves, medium ones to shred them, and miniature workers to seed them with fungus and clean off alien growths.

已知切叶蚁族有200多个物种，分成12个类群，也就是12个属。切叶蚁使用新鲜植物；其他类群的巢穴较小、技术也更原始，因此被称为低等切叶蚁族（lower attines），它们用落在地面的类似叶片和林地地表的昆虫来喂养自己的真菌园。低等切叶蚁族的蚂蚁体形都很相近。然而，切叶蚁工蚁的体形各自适合不同任务：大型蚂蚁负责锯断叶片，中型蚂蚁负责撕碎叶片，微型工蚁则在叶片上接种真菌，并清除外来生长物。

DIn 1994, biologists from the United States Department of Agriculture analysed the DNA of ant fungi. They found that the leaf-cutters’ fungus was descended from a single pure strain, propagated for at least 23 million years. However, the fungi grown by lower attine ants fell into four different groups, as if the ants had domesticated wild fungi at least four times in evolutionary history. What could be driving these two patterns of fungus gardening — the pure clone cultivation of the leaf-cutters and the multiple varieties of the lower attines?

1994年，美国农业部（United States Department of Agriculture）的生物学家分析了蚂蚁所培育真菌的 DNA。他们发现，切叶蚁的真菌来自一个纯正的单一菌株，至少已经繁殖了2300万年。然而，低等切叶蚁族培育的真菌分属4个不同类群，仿佛蚂蚁在演化史上至少4次驯化过野生真菌。究竟是什么推动了这两种真菌种植模式：切叶蚁培育纯克隆菌株，而低等切叶蚁族培育多个品种？

EThe answer has been suggested by Cameron Currie of the University of Toronto. The pure strain of fungus grown by the leaf-cutters, it seemed to him, resembled the single crops grown by humans to the exclusion of all others, such as potato growing. These ‘monocultures’, which lack the genetic diversity to respond to changing environmental threats, are particularly vulnerable to parasites — organisms that live and feed on their host, often causing harm. Currie felt there had to be a parasite in the ant-fungus system. But a century of ant research had provided no evidence for his idea. Textbooks describe how leaf-cutter ants scrupulously weed their gardens of all foreign organisms. “People kept telling me the ants keep their gardens free of parasites,” said Currie. Nevertheless, after three years of sifting through attine ant gardens, Currie discovered several alien organisms, particularly a family of parasitic moulds called ‘Escovopsis’.

多伦多大学（University of Toronto）的 Cameron Currie 提出了一个可能的答案。在他看来，切叶蚁培育的纯菌株，很像人类排除其他作物、只种植一种作物的做法，例如只种马铃薯。这些“单一种植”（monocultures）的作物缺乏应对不断变化的环境威胁所需的遗传多样性，因此特别容易受到寄生生物侵害。寄生生物依附宿主生活并从中获取营养，往往会对宿主造成伤害。Currie 认为，蚂蚁与真菌的体系中一定存在寄生生物。然而，长达一个世纪的蚂蚁研究没有提供任何证据支持他的想法。教科书描述，切叶蚁会一丝不苟地除掉真菌园中的所有外来生物。Currie 说：“人们一直告诉我，蚂蚁会让真菌园远离寄生生物。”尽管如此，在连续3年筛查切叶蚁族真菌园后，Currie 发现了几种外来生物，尤其是一类名为 Escovopsis 的寄生霉菌。

FEscovopsis is a deadly disease that can devastate a fungus garden in a couple of days. It blooms like a white cloud which envelops the whole garden. Other ants won’t go near it, and the ants associated with the garden just starve to death. Evidently, the ants usually manage to keep Escovopsis and other parasites under control. Nevertheless, with any lapse in control Escovopsis will quickly burst forth. Although new leaf-cutter gardens start off free of Escovopsis, within two years some 60 percent become infected.

Escovopsis 引起的致命病害可在两天左右摧毁一座真菌园。它像白云一样迅速繁殖，笼罩整座真菌园。其他蚂蚁不会靠近，依赖这座真菌园的蚂蚁则只能饿死。显然，蚂蚁通常能够控制 Escovopsis 和其他寄生生物。不过，只要控制稍有松懈，Escovopsis 便会迅速暴发。新的切叶蚁真菌园起初不含 Escovopsis，但两年内约有60%会受到感染。

GThe discovery of Escovopsis’s role brings a new level of understanding to the evolution of the attine ants. In the last decade, evolutionary biologists have been increasingly aware of the role of parasites as driving forces in evolution. With Currie’s work, there is now a possible reason for the different varieties of fungus in the lower attine mushroom gardens — to stay one step ahead of the relentless Escovopsis. Interestingly, the leaf-cutters had, in general, fewer alien moulds in their gardens than the lower attines, yet more Escovopsis infections. Clearly, the price they pay for cultivating a pure variety of fungus is a higher risk from Escovopsis.

发现 Escovopsis 的作用，使人们对切叶蚁族的演化有了更深一层的认识。过去10年间，演化生物学家越来越意识到，寄生生物是推动演化的力量。Currie 的研究如今为低等切叶蚁族真菌园为何拥有不同真菌品种提供了一个可能解释：这样可以在应对不断侵袭的 Escovopsis 时保持优势。有趣的是，与低等切叶蚁族相比，切叶蚁的真菌园里通常有更少的外来霉菌，Escovopsis 感染却更多。显然，它们培育单一纯菌种所付出的代价，是面临更高的 Escovopsis 感染风险。

HSo how do attine ants keep this parasite under control? People have known for a hundred years that ants have a whitish growth on their body surface. It was thought to be wax but, after examining it under a microscope, Currie discovered a specialised patch on the ants’ bodies that harbours a particular kind of bacterium, one well known to the pharmaceutical industry and the source of half the antibiotics used in medicine. This bacterium is a potent poisoner of Escovopsis, inhibiting its growth and suppressing spore formation. Astoundingly, the leaf-cutter ants are accomplishing feats beyond the power of humans: they are growing a monocultural crop year after year without disaster, and they are using an antibiotic apparently so wisely that, unlike people, they are not provoking antibiotic resistance in the target disease-producing organism.

那么，切叶蚁族如何控制这种寄生生物？一百年来，人们一直知道蚂蚁体表有一层泛白的生长物。人们原以为那是蜡，但 Currie 用显微镜检查后发现，蚂蚁身体上有一块特殊区域，栖息着某种细菌。这类细菌为制药行业所熟知，医学使用的抗生素有一半来自它。这种细菌能有效毒杀 Escovopsis，抑制其生长，并抑制孢子形成。 令人震惊的是，切叶蚁完成了人类无力做到的壮举：它们年复一年地种植单一作物，却不会遭遇灾难；它们使用抗生素的方式看来也十分明智，不像人类那样，会促使目标致病生物产生抗生素耐药性。
