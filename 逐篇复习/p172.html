Is the drawing of a young Milanese girl really by famous artist Leonardo da Vinci?

这幅描绘米兰少女的画，真是著名艺术家 Leonardo da Vinci 的作品吗？

On January 30, 1998, an art auction was held in New York city. One drawing was of a pretty girl, but it attracted little attention. The catalogue listed the work as a German drawing done in ink and with coloured chalk, dating back to the early 19th century, with styling borrowed from the much earlier Renaissance period. A New York dealer, Kate Ganz, purchased the picture for $21,850. The price had barely changed almost ten years later when a Canadian collector, Peter Silverman, bought it. The drawing might actually date from the Renaissance, he thought. Ganz herself had mentioned Leonardo da Vinci as an influence on the artist. Silverman wondered whether it was the work of the great Leonardo himself, but discovery of a Da Vinci is truly rare. At the time of Silverman’s purchase, it had been more than 75 years since the last authentication of one of the artist’s paintings. It had never been recorded that Da Vinci ever made a major work like this, and neither were there any known copies. If this image was an authentic Leonardo, where had it been hiding for 500 years?

1998年1月30日，纽约市举行了一场艺术品拍卖会。其中一幅画描绘的是一位漂亮女孩，却没引起多少关注。拍卖图录将它列为一幅以墨水和彩色粉笔绘制的德国画作，创作于19世纪初，风格则借鉴了早得多的文艺复兴时期。纽约画商 Kate Ganz 以$21,850买下了这幅画。将近十年后，加拿大收藏家 Peter Silverman 买下它时，价格几乎没变。他想，这幅画其实可能出自文艺复兴时期。Ganz 本人曾提到，画家受过 Leonardo da Vinci 的影响。Silverman 想知道它会不会就是伟大的 Leonardo 本人的作品，但发现一幅 Da Vinci 真迹，实在是极其罕见的事。Silverman 购买这幅画时，距上一次有画作被鉴定为这位艺术家的真迹，已经过去了超过75年。从未有记录表明 Da Vinci 创作过这样一幅重要作品，也没有任何已知的摹本。如果这幅画是 Leonardo 的真迹，那么这500年来，它究竟藏在何处？

Silverman emailed a digital image of the drawing to Martin Kemp, professor of art history at Oxford University and renowned Leonardo scholar. Kemp immediately flew to Zurich, where Silverman kept the drawing in a bank vault. Kemp was excited and began his own investigation. He was aided by Pascal Cotte of Lumière Technology in Paris, who used high resolution multispectral scans which allowed Kemp to study the drawing’s different layers. The more Kemp looked, the more he saw what he considered evidence of Leonardo’s hand: the beautiful tone of the colours, the precise lines, and the fact that the shaded areas showed distinctive strokes just like Leonardo’s. The subject’s expression was self-confident but thoughtful. She appeared to be someone growing up too fast, and conveyed Leonardo’s belief that a portrait should reveal ‘motion of the mind’.

Silverman 把这幅画的电子图像通过邮件发给了 Martin Kemp；他是牛津大学（Oxford University）的艺术史教授，也是一位著名的 Leonardo 研究学者。Kemp 立即飞往苏黎世（Zurich），Silverman 将画作存放在当地一家银行的保险库里。Kemp 十分兴奋，开始了自己的调查。巴黎 Lumière Technology 公司的 Pascal Cotte 协助了他；Cotte 使用高分辨率多光谱扫描，使 Kemp 得以研究画作的不同层次。Kemp 看得越仔细，就越能发现一些被他视为画作出自 Leonardo 之手的迹象：优美的色调、精确的线条，以及阴影区域中与 Leonardo 的笔法如出一辙的独特笔触。画中人物的神情自信，却又若有所思。她看起来像是过早长大的孩子，也传达了 Leonardo 的信念：肖像应当展现“心灵的活动”。

Kemp also needed proof that the portrait had been made during Leonardo's lifetime (1452-1519) and that its historical details fitted the artist's biography. Material analysis strongly suggested that the artist had used calfskin, quite normal for the time, and this was then carbon-dated. Its origin was placed somewhere between 1440 and 1650. Then costume research revealed that the girl belonged specifically to the Milanese court of the 1490s, with its fashion for elaborately bound hair. Leonardo lived in Milan during this time, accepting commissions for portraits of people in the royal court. Stitch marks on the edge of the portrait suggested that it came from a book, possibly the sort, common at the time, that would commemorate a royal marriage.

Kemp 还需要证据，证明这幅肖像创作于 Leonardo 在世期间（1452—1519年），而且画中的历史细节与这位艺术家的生平相符。材料分析有力地支持了画家使用小牛皮的推断，这在当时相当常见；随后，研究人员对它进行了碳测年。测年结果将它的年代范围确定在1440年至1650年之间。接着，服饰研究表明，画中女孩正是1490年代的米兰宫廷中人，当时流行精心束起的发型。Leonardo 那时住在米兰，接受委托，为宫廷人物绘制肖像。肖像边缘的缝线痕迹提示，它原本来自一本书，可能是当时常见的那种纪念王室婚礼的书。

Kemp's detective work led him to a name, Bianca Sforza. A daughter of the Duke of Milan, she was married in 1496 to Galeazzo Sanseverino, commander of the Milanese troops and a patron of Leonardo's. Tragically, she died a few months later. In 2010 Kemp and Cotte published their findings in a book. Several prominent Leonardo scholars agreed, others were sceptical. The possibility of a high-quality forgery was raised. Doubt centred around the drawing’s sudden, almost miraculous appearance. Where had it come from?

Kemp 的追查引出了一个名字：Bianca Sforza。她是米兰公爵的女儿，于1496年嫁给了米兰军队指挥官、也是 Leonardo 赞助人的 Galeazzo Sanseverino。不幸的是，几个月后她就去世了。2010年，Kemp 和 Cotte 将研究结果结集出版。几位著名的 Leonardo 研究学者表示赞同，另一些人则持怀疑态度。有人提出，这可能是一件高水平的赝品。疑虑集中在这幅画突如其来、近乎奇迹般的现身上。它究竟从何而来？

Kemp didn’t know. Then, quite unexpectedly, a message came from David Wright, professor of art history at the University of South Florida. Having followed the very public dispute, Wright suggested to Kemp, whom he had never met, that his answer might lie in the National Library of Poland in Warsaw, inside a book called the Sforziad. Wright, an expert on Renaissance iconography, described it as a commemorative volume for the marriage of Bianca Sforza, a suitable occasion for a Leonardo portrait.

Kemp 不知道。这时，南佛罗里达大学（University of South Florida）的艺术史教授 David Wright 意外地发来了一条消息。Wright 一直关注着这场公开争论；他向素未谋面的 Kemp 提出，答案也许藏在华沙（Warsaw）的波兰国家图书馆（National Library of Poland）里，藏在一本名为《Sforziad》的书中。Wright 是文艺复兴图像学专家；据他介绍，这是一部纪念 Bianca Sforza 婚礼的书，而这样的场合正适合请 Leonardo 绘制一幅肖像。

Kemp and Cotte travelled to Warsaw. Cotte’s macrophotography revealed that a page had been removed from the exact place in the Sforziad where a portrait would have been added. The moment arrived when they inserted a copy of Bianca’s portrait into the open book. It fitted perfectly. For Kemp, this was the proof. It was a single portrait by Leonardo that had gone into a book and then onto a shelf.

Kemp 和 Cotte 前往华沙。Cotte 的微距摄影显示，《Sforziad》中恰好在本应放入肖像的位置，有一页被取走了。随后，他们将 Bianca 肖像的复制件放进了摊开的书中。尺寸完全吻合。在 Kemp 看来，这就是证据。这是一幅 Leonardo 的单幅肖像作品，先被装进书中，随后随书上了书架。

According to Wright, the volume reached Poland in the early 1500s, when a member of the Sforza family married a Polish royal. The page was removed, possibly at the time of the book’s rebinding in the 17th or 18th century. The trail grows faint here. What is known is that at some point it was acquired by an Italian man, a specialist in art restoration, and that his widow put it up for sale at auction.

据 Wright 所说，16世纪初，Sforza 家族的一位成员与波兰王室成员结婚时，这本书传到了波兰。那一页可能是在17或18世纪重新装订这本书时被取走的。线索到这里就变得模糊了。已知的是，这幅画后来曾由一位意大利男子获得；他是一位艺术品修复专家，他的遗孀将画作送上了拍卖会。

These are amazing times for Leonardo admirers. Recently, London’s National Gallery exhibited Leonardo’s painting of Jesus Christ holding a globe, a work lost for centuries. In Florence, researchers looking for Leonardo’s Battle of Anghiari, last seen in the mid-1500s, are using technology to find out if the painting might be hidden behind a wall in the Palazzo Vecchio, but the issue has not yet been resolved.

对 Leonardo 的仰慕者来说，这是一个令人惊喜的时期。不久前，伦敦国家美术馆（National Gallery）展出了 Leonardo 描绘 Jesus Christ 手持球体的一幅画作，这件作品此前失踪了数百年。在佛罗伦萨（Florence），研究人员正借助技术寻找 Leonardo 的《Battle of Anghiari》；这幅画最后一次被人看见是在16世纪中叶，他们想查明它是否藏在旧宫（Palazzo Vecchio）的一堵墙后，但这个问题至今尚未解决。

Authenticating a centuries-old artwork is seldom an objective process. Ego, personal taste, and fear of legal cases all contribute to the judgement. To reach wider consensus, Kemp sent his latest findings to a number of leading specialists, almost all of whom declined to comment. Agreement ‘will take time’ concedes Kemp, ‘but I have clear confidence in the research we’ve done. One thing is sure, should the day come when Bianca Sforza’s face hangs in a museum as a true Leonardo, everyone will stare.’

鉴定一件已有数百年历史的艺术品，很少是一个客观的过程。自我意识、个人品味以及对法律诉讼的担忧，都会影响判断。为争取更广泛的共识，Kemp 将最新发现寄给了一批顶尖专家，但其中几乎所有人都拒绝置评。Kemp 承认，达成共识“还需要时间”，但他说：“我对我们所做的研究有充分的信心。有一点是确定的：倘若有一天，Bianca Sforza 的肖像以 Leonardo 真迹的身份挂进博物馆，所有人都会驻足凝视。”
