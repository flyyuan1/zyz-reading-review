An ingenious invention is set to bring clean water to developing countries, and while the science may be cutting edge, the materials are extremely down to earth.

一项巧妙发明即将为发展中国家带来洁净水；其科学原理或许十分前沿，所用材料却极为朴实。

A handful of clay, yesterday’s coffee grounds and some cow manure are the ingredients that could bring clean, safe drinking water to many developing countries. The simple new technology, developed by Australian National University (ANU) materials scientist and potter Tony Flynn, allows water filters to be made from commonly available materials and fired (or baked) using cow manure as the source of heat, without the need for a kiln (an oven for baking or drying pottery). The filters have been tested and shown to remove common pathogens (disease-producing organisms) including E. coli.

一把黏土、昨天剩下的咖啡渣和一些牛粪，就是可能为许多发展中国家带来清洁、安全饮用水的原料。这项简易新技术由澳大利亚国立大学（Australian National University，ANU）的材料科学家兼陶艺家 Tony Flynn 开发。它让人们能够用随处可得的材料制造净水器，并以牛粪为热源烧制，无须使用烧制或烘干陶器的窑炉。测试表明，这些净水器能够清除包括大肠杆菌（E. coli）在内的常见病原体，即致病生物。

The invention was born out of a project involving the Manatuto community in East Timor. A charity operating there wanted to help set up a small industrial site manufacturing water filters, but initial research found the local clay to be too fine — a problem solved by the addition of organic material. While the problems of producing a working ceramic filter in East Timor were overcome, the solution was kiln-based and particular to that community’s materials and couldn’t be applied elsewhere. Flynn’s technique for manure firing, with no requirement for a kiln, has made this zero-technology approach available anywhere it is needed.

这项发明源于一个涉及东帝汶马纳图托（Manatuto）社区的项目。在当地开展工作的一家慈善机构想帮助设立一个制造净水器的小型工业场所，但初步研究发现，当地黏土颗粒太细；加入有机材料解决了这个问题。虽然在东帝汶制造可用陶瓷过滤器的难题得以克服，但解决方案依赖窑炉，而且只适合该社区的材料，无法用于其他地方。Flynn 使用牛粪烧制的技术不需要窑炉，使这种“零技术”方法可以在任何有需要的地方使用。

Other commercial clay filters do exist, but, even if available, with prices starting at US$5 each, they are often outside the budgets of most people in the developing world. Unlike other water filtering devices, Flynn’s filters are inexpensive and simple to produce. Take a handful of clay, mix it with a handful of organic material such as used tea leaves, coffee grounds or rice hulls, add water in a sufficient quantity to make a stiff mixture and form a cylindrical pot that has one end closed, then dry it in the sun. According to Flynn, used coffee grounds have given the best results to date. The walls of the filter can be measured using the width of an adult finger as the standard. Next, surround the pots with straw, put them in a mound of cow manure, light the straw and then top up the burning manure as required. The filters are finished in 45 to 60 minutes.

市面上确实还有其他黏土净水器，但即使能够买到，每个起价5美元，往往也超出发展中世界大多数人的承受范围。Flynn 的净水器与其他净水装置不同，价格低廉，制作简单。取一把黏土，加入一把用过的茶叶、咖啡渣或稻壳等有机材料，再加足量的水，和成硬实的混合物；将其塑成一端封闭的圆筒形容器，然后放在阳光下晒干。Flynn 说，迄今用过的咖啡渣效果最佳。过滤器壁厚可以用成年人的手指宽度作为标准。接着，用稻草围住陶罐，把它们放入一堆牛粪中，点燃稻草，再按需要向燃烧的牛粪上补料。过滤器会在45至60分钟内烧制完成。

The properties of cow manure are vital, as the fuel can reach a temperature of 700 degrees in half an hour, and will be up to 950 degrees after another 20 to 30 minutes. The manure makes a good fuel because it is very high in organic material that burns readily and quickly. The manure has to be dry and is best used exactly as found in the field; there is no need to break it up or process it any further. In contrast, a potter’s kiln is an expensive item and can take up to four or five hours to get up to 800 degrees. It needs expensive, scarce fuel, such as gas or wood to heat it, and experience to use it. With no technology, no insulation and nothing other than a pile of cow manure and a match, none of these requirements apply.

牛粪的特性至关重要：这种燃料可在半小时内达到700度，再过20至30分钟，温度最高可达950度。牛粪是优质燃料，因为其中有机物含量很高，能够轻易而迅速地燃烧。牛粪必须干燥，最好直接使用田野里找到的原样牛粪，无须打碎或进一步处理。相比之下，陶窑价格昂贵，升至800度可能需要四五个小时；它需要用燃气或木材等昂贵而稀缺的燃料加热，操作起来也需要经验。这里不需要技术，不需要隔热材料，除了一堆牛粪和一根火柴什么也不需要，因此上述要求都不适用。

It is also helpful that, like clay and organic material, cow manure is freely available across the developing world. A cow is a natural fuel factory. Manure is a mixture of vegetable materials of different sizes, and cow manure as a fuel is the same wherever it is found.

还有一点也很有帮助：牛粪与黏土和有机材料一样，在发展中世界各地都随处可得。一头牛就是一座天然燃料工厂。牛粪由大小不一的植物性材料混合而成，无论在哪里找到，作为燃料的性质都一样。

Just as using manure as a fuel for domestic use is not a new idea, the fact that liquid can pass through clay objects is something that potters have always known, and clay’s porous nature is something that, as a former ceramics lecturer in the ANU School of Art, Flynn is well aware of. The difference is that, rather than viewing the porous nature of the material as a problem — after all, not many people want a pot that won’t hold water — his filters capitalize on this property.

用牛粪作家庭燃料并非新构想；同样，液体可以穿过黏土制品这一事实，也是陶艺家一直都知道的。Flynn 曾任 ANU 艺术学院（ANU School of Art）陶艺讲师，自然很了解黏土的多孔性质。不同之处在于，他的过滤器没有把材料多孔视为问题——毕竟，没有多少人想要一只装不住水的陶罐——而是利用了这种性质。

The filtration process is simple, but effective. The basic principle is that there are passages through the filter that are wide enough for water droplets to pass through, but too narrow for pathogens. Tests with the deadly E. coli bacterium have seen the filters remove 96.4 to 99.8 per cent of the pathogen — well within safe levels. The thickness of the clay container needs to be the same thickness as an adult finger for the process to be effective. If this is the case, using only one filter, a liter of water can be obtained in two hours.

过滤过程简单，却很有效。基本原理是，过滤器中存在一些通道，宽到足以让水滴通过，却窄到病原体无法穿过。用致命的大肠杆菌进行的测试显示，这些过滤器能清除96.4%至99.8%的病原体，完全达到安全水平。黏土容器必须与成年人的手指一样厚，过滤过程才能有效。在符合这一条件的情况下，只用一个过滤器，两个小时便能获得一升水。

The use of organic material, which burns away leaving cavities after firing, helps produce the structure in which pathogens will become trapped. It overcomes the potential problems of finer clays that may not let water through and also means that cracks are soon halted. And like clay and cow manure, organic material is universally available in the developing communities that need the most assistance, as tea, coffee and rice are grown in these areas.

烧制时，有机材料会烧尽并留下空腔，有助于形成能够困住病原体的结构。它既克服了较细黏土可能不让水通过的问题，也意味着裂缝很快便会停止延伸。黏土和牛粪如此，有机材料也一样，在最需要援助的发展中社区随处可得，因为这些地区种植茶、咖啡和稻米。

With all the components being widely available, Flynn says there is no reason the technology couldn’t be applied throughout the developing world. He has no plans to exploit his idea financially by registering ownership through a patent. If he did, any commercial copying would legally entitle him to a share in any profits made. Without a patent, there will be no illegality in it being adopted in any community that needs it. “Everyone has a right to clean water, and these filters have the potential to enable anyone in the world to drink water safely,” says Flynn.

所有材料都广泛可得，因此 Flynn 说，没有理由不能在整个发展中世界应用这项技术。他不打算申请专利来登记所有权，借此从自己的构想中谋取经济利益。假如他申请了专利，任何商业仿制在法律上都应让他分享所获利润。不申请专利，任何有需要的社区采用这项技术便不违法。Flynn 说：“每个人都有权获得洁净水，而这些过滤器有可能让世界上任何人都能安全饮水。”
