I 段 集约化方案的取舍 提高效率同时产生福利问题

Some claim that it’s the only way to save our planet from disaster. But is it really that simple?

有人声称，这是拯救地球免遭灾难的唯一方法。但事情真的如此简单吗？

AThe call for people to eat less meat to save the planet is growing louder, so if less is good, wouldn’t none be better? To find out, let’s imagine what would happen if the world eliminated meat, milk and eggs from its diet, then trace the effects. Last year the world consumed 289 million tonnes of meat, 700 million tonnes of milk and 1.2 billion eggs. Environmentally speaking, this came at an enormous cost.

为了拯救地球，要求人们少吃肉的呼声越来越响。那么，既然少吃有益，完全不吃岂不是更好？为了找出答案，不妨设想全世界从饮食中彻底去除肉、奶和蛋，再追踪由此产生的影响。去年，全球消费了2.89亿吨肉、7亿吨奶和12亿枚蛋。从环境角度看，这付出了极其沉重的代价。

BAll agriculture damages the environment. Take for example the felled forests and the common use of irrigation systems. And it may surprise you to know that agriculture creates more greenhouse gases than all methods of transport put together. Livestock farming does the most damage. In part, that is because most livestock eat grain that could be used to feed human populations, and farmers are forced to grow more than we would otherwise need to meet the demand. Altogether, if we switched to a vegan diet, meaning no meat, dairy or eggs, the land currently required for crops would drop by an estimated 21 per cent, about 3.4 million hectares, roughly the size of India.

所有农业都会损害环境，例如砍伐森林和普遍使用灌溉系统。你可能还会惊讶地发现，农业产生的温室气体多于所有交通方式的总和，其中畜牧业破坏最大。部分原因在于，大多数牲畜食用本可供人类食用的谷物，农民因此不得不种植比原本所需更多的作物，来满足这种需求。总的来说，如果我们改吃纯素饮食（vegan diet），也就是不吃肉、奶制品或蛋，目前种植作物所需的土地据估计会减少21%，约340万公顷，大致相当于印度的面积。

COne environmental impact that would also lessen through a reduction in animal farming would be that of the nitrogen emitted from agricultural processes, which spreads into both waterways and land. According to environmental scientist Allison Leach, if everyone eliminated dairy products and eggs, this kind of pollution would fall by 60%. Livestock production has another serious environmental impact. Global statistics are hard to come by, but in the US at least, livestock account for 55% of erosion, mostly from forests being cut down to make way for grazing land. On top of this, half of all antibiotics manufactured are fed to livestock as part of their normal diet, a practice that is leading to drug-resistant bacteria.

减少畜牧业还会减轻另一项环境影响：农业过程排放的氮会扩散到水道和陆地，造成污染。环境科学家 Allison Leach 说，如果人人都不再食用奶制品和蛋，这类污染将下降60%。畜牧生产还会造成另一种严重环境影响。全球统计数据很难获得，但至少在美国，牲畜造成了55%的土壤侵蚀，主要原因是人们砍伐森林，为放牧用地腾出空间。除此之外，生产出的全部抗生素中有一半会作为日常饮食的一部分喂给牲畜，这种做法正导致耐药细菌出现。

DA meat-free world, then, would be greener in many ways. However, if everyone opted to give up meat there would be significant costs too. For most of human history, livestock grazed on land that wasn’t suitable for ploughing, and in doing so they converted inedible grass into edible meat and milk. Even today, a flock of sheep or goats can be the most efficient way to get food from marginal land. In a world in which over a billion people do not have enough to eat, using this land for crop production would contribute to food insecurity.

因此，一个没有肉食的世界会在许多方面更加环保。然而，如果人人都选择戒肉，也会付出重大代价。在人类历史的大部分时期，牲畜都在不适合耕作的土地上吃草，从而把人不能吃的草转化为可食用的肉和奶。即使在今天，在贫瘠土地上获得食物的最高效方式也可能是饲养一群绵羊或山羊。世界上有超过10亿人食不果腹，如果把这种土地用于作物生产，反而会加剧粮食不安全。

EIn some parts of the world today, livestock like chickens, for instance, can subsist on leftovers and whatever they find. Tara Garnett, who heads the Food Climate Research Network at the University of Surrey, points out the usefulness of these animals. By giving them your leftovers, she says, they deal with your rubbish, and you get meat. Garnett admits, though, that if this kind of approach were generally adopted, it would require a major adjustment in food preferences; people would have to get used to chicken, for example, with less fat.

如今在世界一些地区，鸡等畜禽可以靠剩饭剩菜和自行找到的食物存活。萨里大学（University of Surrey）食品气候研究网络（Food Climate Research Network）负责人 Tara Garnett 指出了这些动物的用处。她说，把剩饭给它们，它们就替你处理了垃圾，而你能得到肉。不过，Garnett 也承认，若普遍采用这种方法，就需要大幅调整人们的食物偏好；例如，人们将不得不习惯脂肪更少的鸡肉。

FAnother downside to a meat-free world would be the disappearance of animal by-products. Such a world would have to replace the 11 million tonnes of leather and 2 million tonnes of wool that come annually from livestock farming and which are turned into clothing. Furthermore, even ardent vegetarians acknowledge that dairy products and meat may be a good thing in poorer countries. ‘Whilst there’s no doubt that considerable reduction of meat consumption would have an environmental benefit, we do have to be careful about saying it would be the best solution if the whole world went vegetarian,’ says Annette Pinner, chief executive of the UK Vegetarian Society. For many of the world’s poorest rural residents, an animal may represent their only realistic hope for a little extra income, and a little animal protein can make a big difference to a marginal diet.

无肉世界的另一个缺点，是动物副产品会随之消失。这样的世界必须寻找替代品，取代畜牧业每年提供、用于制作服装的1,100万吨皮革和200万吨羊毛。此外，即使坚定的素食者也承认，在较贫困国家，奶制品和肉可能是好东西。英国素食协会（UK Vegetarian Society）首席执行官 Annette Pinner 说：“毫无疑问，大幅减少肉类消费会有益于环境，但若说全世界都改吃素就是最佳解决方案，我们确实必须谨慎。”对世界上许多最贫困的农村居民而言，一只动物可能是他们获得一点额外收入唯一现实的希望；在营养勉强够用的饮食中，少量动物蛋白也能带来巨大差别。

GWhat if we decided on a no-meat vegetarian diet, rather than a vegan diet? After all, milk and eggs are very efficient ways of producing animal calories. ‘It’s difficult to switch to a no-meat-but-milk diet,’ says Helmut Haberl, a social ecologist. ‘Dairy cows must calve every year to keep producing milk, and only half their offspring will be female. While many vegetarians see moral reasons not to kill and eat the males, there is surely no practical reason to waste so much meat.’ Similar arguments apply to chickens kept for eggs.

如果我们决定采用不吃肉的素食饮食（vegetarian diet），而不是纯素饮食呢？毕竟，奶和蛋是生产动物性热量的高效方式。社会生态学家 Helmut Haberl 说：“改成不吃肉但喝奶的饮食很困难。奶牛必须每年产犊，才能持续产奶，而它们的后代只有一半是雌性。虽然许多素食者认为，不杀死、不食用雄性动物有道德上的理由，但浪费这么多肉肯定没有实际理由。”类似的论点也适用于为产蛋而饲养的鸡。

HSo even though a meat-free world sounds good on paper, it is likely that a utopian future will still have some animal products in it. The real questions, then, are how much meat do we want, and how will we produce it? The answers depend on how you approach these questions. The most straightforward approach is to assume that the world will continue to want evermore meat. The United Nations’ best guess is that by 2050, the world will need to more than double its production of meat, an increase that would be environmentally disastrous.

因此，没有肉食的世界听来虽然很好，但理想的未来很可能仍会包含一些动物产品。真正的问题就变成：我们想吃多少肉，又要如何生产这些肉？答案取决于你从什么角度处理这些问题。最直接的做法，是假定全世界对肉的需求将不断增加。联合国（United Nations）最合理的估计是，到2050年，全球肉类产量需要增加到目前的两倍以上，这种增长会给环境带来灾难。

IUnder this scenario the goal will have to be producing the most meat at the lowest environmental cost. According to Walter Falcon, an agricultural economist, this means fewer free range cattle and sheep in green fields. ‘If you’re going to keep some livestock systems, I think the ones you’ll want to keep are the intensive ones,’ he says. Of course this does not take into account animal welfare issues, as intensive farming usually means poor living conditions and the use of growth hormones.

在这种情境下，目标只能是以最低的环境代价生产最多的肉。农业经济学家 Walter Falcon 说，这意味着绿野上自由放养的牛羊会减少。他说：“如果你准备保留一些畜牧体系，我认为应该保留的是集约化体系。”当然，这没有考虑动物福利问题，因为集约化养殖通常意味着恶劣的生活条件和生长激素的使用。

Agriculture causes significant harm to the environment in a number of ways, including the frequent use of 18 irrigation in modern farming and the cutting down of trees.
