Professor John Lovett from New England University, Australia, describes some of the ways in which plants ensure their own survival.

澳大利亚新英格兰大学（New England University）的 John Lovett 教授介绍了植物确保自身生存的若干方式。

AThe general perception of plants is that they are defenceless when faced with threat from animals (including man), insects and other plants. This, however, is not the case. Plants are able to defend themselves against attack from their environment in many ways. There are two major features of plants that ensure their continuation as species. One of these relates to their physical attributes. Spinifex grass, a plant native to Australia, provides a notable example. Adapted to life in the hot, desert inland, spinifex has developed tightly rolled leaves which reduce the effects of drought stress. The hairs, or trichomes, which are found on the inner surface of the rolled-up spinifex leaf are believed to assist in minimising water loss. Trichomes also have defensive roles against predators. Many plants, such as the sunflower, have hairy leaves which are rough to the touch. When the plant is bitten by a grazing animal, the sensation is unpleasant on the tongue and the animal normally moves on to find more palatable food.

人们通常认为，面对包括人类在内的动物、昆虫和其他植物的威胁，植物毫无防御能力。然而，事实并非如此。植物能够以多种方式抵御周围环境的侵袭。植物有两项主要特征可确保其物种延续，其中一项与其物理属性有关。原生于澳大利亚的 spinifex 草就是一个显著例子。为了适应炎热的内陆荒漠生活，spinifex 草长出了紧紧卷曲的叶片，从而减轻干旱胁迫的影响。人们认为，卷起的 spinifex 叶片内表面上的茸毛，即毛状体（trichomes），有助于最大限度地减少水分流失。毛状体还能防御捕食者。许多植物，如向日葵，都长有摸起来粗糙的带毛叶片。当食草动物啃咬植物时，舌头会产生不适感，这种动物通常便会离开，去寻找更可口的食物。

BSome families of plants such as the Solanaceae, which includes the tomato, the potato and other food plants, feature glandular trichomes. In these structures a tiny sac is carried on the hair, which rises from the epidermis, or skin, of the plant. The sac contains chemicals which may perform a range of functions. For example, an insect landing on a tomato leaf and rupturing the sac is exposed to a toxic chemical which will deter it from feeding on that plant in future. In other plants of the same family, the sacs contain a natural adhesive and the unsuspecting insect landing on such a leaf and rupturing a trichome sac is held onto the plant, unable to depart. In the leaves of the nettle plant these trichomes resemble miniature hypodermic syringes. An irritant chemical is stored at the base of the ‘syringe’. If an animal touches it, a shot of the chemical is discharged through the ‘needle’ and into the skin of the animal. The irritation the chemical causes is a deterrent against future threat.

某些植物科的成员具有腺毛（glandular trichomes），例如包括番茄、马铃薯及其他食用植物的茄科（Solanaceae）。在这种结构中，一根从植物表皮，也就是外皮上伸出的茸毛顶端托着一个微小囊体。囊体中含有可能发挥多种作用的化学物质。例如，昆虫落到番茄叶上并使囊体破裂后，便会接触到一种有毒化学物质；这种物质会阻止它日后再取食该植物。在同科其他植物中，囊体内含有天然黏合剂；毫无戒备的昆虫落到这样的叶片上并弄破毛状体囊体后，就会被粘在植物上，无法离开。荨麻（nettle）叶片上的这些毛状体形似微型皮下注射器。“注射器”底部储存着一种刺激性化学物质。如果动物触碰到它，一股化学物质就会经由“针头”射入动物皮肤。这种化学物质引起的刺激会使动物不敢再次来犯。

CThe poisons and glues of the Solanaceae and the irritant of the nettle indicate the other major method of plant survival: that some plants use chemicals to protect themselves, sometimes from each other. Chemical interactions that occur between plants are known as ‘allelopathy’, and in natural plant communities it is one of many factors which determine how plants grow in relation to one another. An example in Australia is the suppression of vegetation growth beneath eucalypt trees, an effect which decreases with increasing distance from the trunk of the tree.

茄科植物的毒素和黏合物，以及荨麻的刺激性物质，表明了植物另一种主要的生存方式：一些植物利用化学物质保护自己，有时防范的甚至是其他植物。植物之间发生的化学相互作用称为“化感作用”（allelopathy）；在天然植物群落中，它是决定植物彼此之间如何生长的众多因素之一。澳大利亚的一个例子是，桉树（eucalypt trees）下方的植被生长会受到抑制，而且距树干越远，这种影响就越弱。

DThe most detailed studies of allelopathy have generally been carried out on agricultural plant communities. In agriculture, allelopathy has been identified particularly with weeds, which compete with desired crop or pasture species, such as wheat, oats and grass, for environmental resources. Competition can be physical, where the growth of one plant maximises the use of water, nutrients and light to the disadvantage of another. Allelopathy, on the other hand, involves plant-produced chemicals which may poison a neighbouring plant. On the whole, weeds are far more physically and chemically aggressive than crop and pasture species. As an example, recent research on the common thorn apple, Datura stramonium, a weed found throughout Australia, has found that two powerful chemicals are released from the seed during the early stages of germination. In fact, it is necessary for the chemicals to be released from the seed coat so that the seed can germinate. Once released, the chemicals then provide a barrier that inhibits the growth of any potential plant competitor near the thorn apple seedling.

对化感作用最细致的研究通常是在农业植物群落中开展的。在农业中，人们尤其在杂草身上发现了化感作用；杂草会同小麦、燕麦和牧草等栽培植物争夺环境资源。竞争可以是物理层面的，即一种植物通过生长最大限度地占用水、养分和光照，从而损害另一种植物。相比之下，化感作用涉及植物产生的化学物质，这些物质可能毒害邻近植物。总体而言，杂草在物理和化学层面的侵袭性远强于作物和牧草物种。例如，近期对澳大利亚各地都有分布的杂草曼陀罗（common thorn apple，Datura stramonium）的研究发现，其种子在萌发初期会释放两种强效化学物质。事实上，这些化学物质必须从种皮中释放出来，种子才能萌发。一经释放，它们就会形成一道屏障，抑制曼陀罗幼苗附近任何潜在植物竞争者的生长。

EAllelopathic chemicals function in quite subtle ways. Generally they are active in small quantities, they are effective only with certain other plants, they act by disrupting the plant’s natural processes and they have no known residual effects. It has also been found that the activity of these chemicals may be synergistic, that is, two or more such substances may combine to produce an effect that is greater than the effects of the substances acting in isolation. These are the very attributes which are being targeted in the current development of synthetic herbicides*. Allelopathy is not restricted to weeds in agriculture, but allelopathic activity in crop and pasture plants appears to have diminished, possibly because plant breeders may have concentrated on improving aspects of crop quality and quantity rather than aspects of defence against natural enemies.

产生化感作用的化学物质以相当微妙的方式发挥效力。一般而言，它们只需少量便会起作用，仅对某些其他植物有效，通过扰乱植物的自然过程发挥作用，而且没有已知的残留效应。研究还发现，这些化学物质可能具有协同活性，也就是说，两种或多种此类物质结合后产生的效果，可能大于各自单独作用时的效果。当前开发合成除草剂*时，希望获得的正是这些特性。化感作用并不限于农业杂草，但作物和牧草中的化感活性似乎已经减弱，这可能是因为植物育种者着重改善了作物质量和产量，而非提高其抵御天敌的能力。

FAllelopathic chemicals may be harvested for development as ‘natural herbicides’, similar to the harvest of pyrethrins from the pyrethrum daisy for use as natural insecticides. Harnessing allelopathy is highly attractive to the farming industry, as at least half of the more than A$200-million annual cost of crop protection in Australia is spent on herbicides. Enhancing allelopathic activity in crop and pasture plants could reduce this enormous bill. Developing and harvesting natural herbicides might also offer real advantages in addressing environmental concerns about the over-use of synthetic chemicals currently used in crop protection. In many parts of the world, communities are showing an increased willingness to pay a premium for products grown with few or no synthetic chemicals, and natural products are becoming increasingly attractive.

人们可以提取产生化感作用的化学物质，用来开发“天然除草剂”，就像从除虫菊（pyrethrum daisy）中提取除虫菊素（pyrethrins）、将其用作天然杀虫剂一样。利用化感作用对农业极具吸引力，因为澳大利亚每年超过2亿澳元的作物保护成本中，至少有一半用于除草剂。增强作物和牧草中的化感活性，可以降低这笔巨额开支。开发和提取天然除草剂，也可能有助于缓解人们对目前作物保护中过度使用合成化学品所带来的环境问题的担忧。在世界许多地区，社会大众越来越愿意为种植过程中很少使用或完全不使用合成化学品的产品支付溢价，天然产品也变得越来越有吸引力。

*herbicides: chemicals that kill plants, used mainly to control weeds

*除草剂：杀死植物的化学物质，主要用于控制杂草
