Is it art? Or is it a crime?

它是艺术，还是犯罪？

AThe term ‘graffiti’ derives from the Italian word graffio meaning ‘scratching’, and can be defined as uninvited markings or writing scratched or applied to objects, built structures, and natural features. It is not a new phenomenon; examples can be found on ancient structures around the world, in some cases predating the Greeks and Romans. In such circumstances, it has acquired invaluable historical and archaeological significance as it provides a social history of life and events at that time. This is not true of more recent graffiti, which has become a pervasive problem particularly over the last 50 years, as a result of the availability of cheap and quick means of mark-making. These include the ubiquitous spray paints.

“涂鸦”（graffiti）一词源自意大利语 graffio，意为“刮划”，可以定义为：未经许可，在物品、人工构筑物和自然景观上刻画或添加的标记或文字。涂鸦并非新现象；世界各地的古代建筑上都能找到实例，有些甚至早于古希腊和古罗马时期。在这种情况下，涂鸦具有极其宝贵的历史和考古意义，因为它记录了当时生活和事件的社会史。较近时期的涂鸦却并非如此：尤其在过去50年里，由于廉价而快捷的留痕工具容易获得，涂鸦已经成为普遍的问题。这些工具包括随处可见的喷漆。

BGraffitists, the people who are responsible for creating graffiti, are indiscriminate about the surfaces they choose. Wherever graffiti appears, it is usually considered a priority to remove it as quickly as possible. One reason for this is to prevent ‘copy-cat’ emulation, which can occur rapidly once a clean surface is defaced, and so many companies and councils have a policy of removing graffiti within an hour or two of it being reported. Another reason is that as paints, glues, and inks dry out, they can become increasingly difficult to remove. A more important factor, however, is that graffiti can lead to more serious forms of vandalism and, ultimately, to the deterioration of a neighborhood area, and in this way contributes to a general social decline. For this reason, the police like to be notified of new graffiti incidents. To aid in achieving a successful prosecution, they encourage owners of properties to take photographs of any new graffiti, as there could be other related incidents occurring locally. An incidence pattern can help to identify possible culprits, as can their ‘tag’—the distinctive mark graffitists leave to show the graffiti is their work.

涂鸦者，也就是创作涂鸦的人，在选择表面时并不挑剔。无论涂鸦出现在哪里，人们通常都会优先尽快清除。一个原因是防止他人仿效；干净的表面一旦被涂污，模仿行为可能迅速出现，因此许多公司和市政机构规定，涂鸦在接报后一两个小时内就要清除。另一个原因是油漆、胶水和墨水干燥后，可能会越来越难清除。不过，更重要的因素在于，涂鸦可能引发更严重的破坏行为，最终造成街区环境恶化，并以此加剧整体社会衰败。因此，警方希望接到新涂鸦事件的通知。为帮助成功起诉相关人员，警方鼓励业主拍下任何新涂鸦，因为当地可能还发生了其他相关事件。事件的分布规律有助于找出可能的肇事者，他们留下的“签名标记”（tag）也有同样作用；这种独特标记用于表明涂鸦是其作品。

CAny proposal to remove graffiti has to be carefully considered because techniques designed for robust or utilitarian surfaces may result in considerable damage to older, more historic buildings. Consequently, as no two sites are the same, there is no one treatment which will be suitable for all situations; each site must be looked at individually. The owners of some graffiti-affected sites may wish to attempt their own treatment, whereas others may prefer to contact a specialist contractor. However, whichever course is decided on, it is important to follow a systematic approach. The first step is to carry out cleaning trials to see which method is suitable for the removal of the graffiti media used, and also which method has the least impact on the site surface. These trials should be carried out on a small unobtrusive area if possible and should always start with the least aggressive cleaning method, usually water, and stop once a successful method has been found.

任何清除涂鸦的方案都必须慎重考虑，因为适用于坚固或侧重实用功能的表面的技术，可能会严重损坏年代较久、更具历史价值的建筑。因此，既然没有两个地点完全相同，也就没有一种处理方法适用于所有情况；每个地点都必须单独考察。有些受涂鸦影响地点的业主可能希望自行处理，另一些则可能更愿意联系专业承包商。然而，无论选择哪一种做法，都应遵循系统化流程。第一步是开展清洁试验，判断哪种方法适合清除所用的涂鸦材料，同时确定哪种方法对现场表面影响最小。如果可能，试验应在一小块不显眼的区域进行，而且始终要从侵蚀性最低的清洁方式开始，通常先用水；一旦找到有效方法，就应停止试验。

DApart from water, a variety of methods can be used to remove graffiti; broadly, these are divided into chemical and mechanical systems. When using either of these systems, care must be taken with regard to protecting both passers-by and the person carrying out the graffiti removal. Chemical preparations are based on dissolving the graffiti media; these solvents can range from mild to potentially dangerous chemical ‘cocktails’. When using chemicals, it is important to wear protective clothing, take measures to minimize any run-off of potentially harmful chemicals, and avoid the drifting of aerial mists. Mechanical systems are often in the form of wire-brushing and grit blasting, which attempt to chip off the graffiti media. Operators of mechanical systems must also wear protective clothing to avoid being hurt by hard airborne particles. This method often leaves marks on a surface.

除水以外，还有多种方法可以清除涂鸦，大致分为化学系统和机械系统。使用任何一种系统时，都必须注意保护路人和清除涂鸦的操作人员。化学制剂通过溶解涂鸦材料发挥作用；这类溶剂既可能很温和，也可能是具有潜在危险的化学“混合剂”。使用化学品时，必须穿戴防护服，采取措施尽量减少可能有害的化学品流淌外泄，并防止雾状颗粒飘散。机械系统通常采用钢丝刷洗和喷砂，试图把涂鸦材料从表面剥落。机械系统操作人员同样必须穿戴防护服，以免被空气中的坚硬颗粒击伤。这种方法往往会在表面留下痕迹。

EDifferent measures can be adopted to combat a recurring problem of graffiti. It is clear that protection measures will ultimately be more successful and less damaging than multiple removal treatments. Popular places for graffiti are often readily accessible flat surfaces where the graffiti will have an instant impact, but this is not always the case, and graffitists may scale bridges to give their work greater prominence. Possible protection measures may include a combination of floodlighting to illuminate dark areas and surveillance systems such as closed-circuit television. Research has shown that prominently placed cameras can reduce the incidence of graffiti in areas with a recurring problem. Unfortunately, the cost of any of these measures may be too high, and so physical obstacles such as fences, railings, doors, or gates can be introduced to discourage unauthorized access, though in some cases, these obstacles simply provide a new surface for graffiti.

对于反复出现的涂鸦问题，可以采取不同措施加以应对。显然，与多次清除相比，保护措施最终会更有效，对表面的损害也更小。容易到达、能让涂鸦立刻引人注目的平坦表面，往往是涂鸦的热门地点，但情况并非总是如此；涂鸦者也可能爬上桥梁，让作品更加醒目。可采用的保护措施可能包括泛光照明与监控系统的组合：前者照亮黑暗区域，后者可使用闭路电视等设备。研究表明，在反复发生涂鸦的地区，把摄像头放在显眼位置能够减少涂鸦事件。遗憾的是，这些措施的成本可能过高，因此可以设置围栏、栏杆、门或大门等实体障碍，阻止未经许可的人员进入。不过在某些情况下，这些障碍反而只是为涂鸦提供了新的表面。

FAnother strategy is to apply a removable anti-graffiti barrier-coating as a form of preventive measure. Although this will not stop graffiti being applied, it will make its removal much easier and usually only involves using water, which reduces the possibility of damage to the site surface. Barrier coatings are usually applied by brush or spray, leaving a thin veneer that essentially serves to isolate the graffiti from the surface. So, although there is no prescription for dealing with every graffiti incident, there are some steps that can be taken to minimize the impact of graffiti and to protect vulnerable surfaces.

另一种策略是涂上一层可移除的防涂鸦隔离涂层，作为预防措施。虽然它无法阻止人们留下涂鸦，却会使清除工作容易得多，而且通常只需用水，从而降低损坏现场表面的可能性。隔离涂层通常用刷子涂刷或喷涂，形成一层薄膜，实质上把涂鸦与表面隔开。因此，虽然没有适用于每起涂鸦事件的统一处理方案，人们仍可采取一些步骤，尽量减少涂鸦的影响，并保护容易受损的表面。
