Why good ideas fail

好创意为什么会失败

As part of a marketing course, two marketing experts comment on a hypothetical case study involving TF, a fictional retail giant specializing in home furnishing. The experts give concrete solutions and advice to assist students.

在一门营销课程中，两位营销专家点评了一个涉及 TF 的假想案例，TF 是一家虚构的家居用品零售巨头。两位专家为学生提供了具体的解决方案和建议。

Hypothetical case study:

假想案例：

TF became a retail success in the 1970s when it succeeded in spotting homeware trends and meeting the needs of its then trendy young customers. However, by 2004, the TF stores were failing and a rethink was clearly necessary. Tibal Fisher, TF’s founder and CEO, decided to change its focus under a new brand name, TF’s Nextstage. His aim was to recapture the now ageing customers that had given him his early success and target consumers aged 60+ with devices and gadgets specifically designed to assist them with the problems associated with ageing: mobile phones with screens that were easy to read; kitchen gadgets with comfortable grips; electronic devices that were easy to set and adjust. TF’s market research proved to be very positive, showing strong consumer support for the products.

20世纪70年代，TF成功发现家居用品趋势，并满足了当时年轻时尚顾客的需求，由此成为一家成功的零售企业。然而，到2004年，TF门店经营不佳，显然必须重新思考方向。TF创始人兼首席执行官 Tibal Fisher 决定以新品牌TF’s Nextstage改变业务重点。他想重新吸引早年帮助公司取得成功、如今已经变老的顾客，把60岁及以上消费者作为目标，为他们提供专门用于解决衰老相关问题的装置和小工具：屏幕易于阅读的手机、握柄舒适的厨房用具、容易设置和调节的电子设备。TF的市场调查结果十分积极，显示消费者非常支持这些产品。

In 2007, the stores were remodelled at a cost of US $40 million, and the new brand was launched. Each store was made more comfortable and featured a coffee shop to help increase traffic — Tibal had predicted that if they could get customers into the stores, then the products would sell themselves. However, by 2009 it was clear that the idea was a failure, and the stores consistently remained empty. Customers complained that the new stores felt like a senior center and reminded them that they were growing old.

2007年，公司耗资4,000万美元改造门店并推出新品牌。每家门店都变得更舒适，还设有咖啡店以增加客流。Tibal 曾预测，只要能把顾客吸引进门店，产品自然就能卖出去。然而，到2009年，这一构想显然已经失败，门店一直空空荡荡。顾客抱怨新店感觉像老年人活动中心，会提醒他们自己正在变老。

Feedback from experts:

专家反馈：

Expert 1: Donna Sturgess, global head of innovation, GlaxoSmithKline

专家1：葛兰素史克（GlaxoSmithKline）全球创新主管 Donna Sturgess

The team’s customer research efforts are a classic case of missing the subconscious associations at work in consumers’ minds. Tibal and his executives looked only at surface attitudes. Since those attitudes make up a relatively small part of the total consumer response, the executives are clueless about the reason for the poor sales. It’s critical for companies to understand that every customer relates to a brand emotionally, and it’s those emotions that trigger — or block — purchases.

这个团队开展的顾客研究，是忽略消费者心中潜意识联想的典型案例。Tibal 和高管只看到了表面态度。由于这些态度在消费者整体反应中所占比例相对很小，高管根本不知道销量不佳的原因。公司必须明白，每位顾客都会在情感上与一个品牌建立联系，而正是这些情感会促成或阻止购买。

That’s why we’ve focused on using emotional strategies behind branding for a number of years now. A great example is Alli (pronounced ‘ally’), a drug to aid weight loss. The product deals with a highly emotional issue, so in marketing it, we faced the same challenge that the new TF stores are facing: the very thought of buying the product reminds customers that they have problems they feel negatively about. In the case of TF’s Nextstage, the problems are age and infirmity. In the case of Alli, the problems are excessive weight and its consequences. There’s always a risk that consumers’ negative feelings will discourage them from starting or staying on a diet. So, after extensive market research, we took a number of steps to inject emotions into the whole process of using the product.

因此，多年来，我们一直注重运用品牌背后的情感策略。一个很好的例子是减重辅助药物 Alli，其读音与英文 ally（盟友）相同。这款产品涉及一个十分牵动情绪的问题，所以营销时，我们面临与 TF 新门店相同的挑战：顾客只要想到购买产品，就会想起那些让自己感到消极的问题。对TF’s Nextstage而言，问题是年龄和身体衰弱；对Alli而言，则是体重过重及其后果。消费者的负面情绪始终可能阻止他们开始节食，或使他们无法坚持。因此，经过广泛市场研究，我们采取了多项措施，把情感融入使用产品的整个过程。

First, we came up with a name that sounds like a helpful partner. We also aimed to make the container both beautiful and functional — something that didn’t just hold pills but could later be used to store diet guides and recipes. Traditional market research is unlikely to uncover ideas like this, so we use a wide variety of techniques. Even simple techniques, such as one-on-one interviews, or ethnographic observation that involves going into people’s houses to examine their behaviour, can provide valuable data.

首先，我们想出了一个听起来像乐于助人的伙伴的名称。我们还力求使容器既美观又实用：它不只用来装药片，之后还能存放节食指南和食谱。传统市场研究不太可能发现这样的构想，因此我们会使用多种技术。即使是一对一访谈等简单方法，或是走进人们家中观察其行为的民族志观察，也能提供宝贵数据。

Expert 2: Alex Lee, president of OXO International, maker of OXO Good Grips household products

专家2：OXO Good Grips家居用品制造商OXO International总裁 Alex Lee

This retailer can get back on track by remembering a principle that applies to consumers in general, and those aged 60+ in particular: they’re attracted by brands they associate with the type of people they’d like to be — not the type they really are. That’s why marketing campaigns for surf gear feature surfers, not the city dwellers who will wear the products while doing their shopping.

这家零售商只要记住一项适用于消费者总体、尤其适用于60岁及以上消费者的原则，就能回到正轨：吸引人们的是让他们联想到理想自我形象的品牌，而不是让他们联想到实际自我形象的品牌。正因为如此，冲浪装备的营销活动会展示冲浪者，而不是那些穿着产品出门购物的城市居民。

I was reminded of this principle a few years ago when we wanted to find out how far we could apply our design philosophy of making things easier to use in order to move from our core business, kitchen tools, into other products. We conducted what are known as focus groups, where participants were asked to look at photos of people and pick those they perceived to be users and non-users of our products. Consistently, they picked people who looked fit as the sort who would use our products, and people who looked old and boring as the sort who wouldn’t. Yet the participants, all owners of our products, looked a lot more like the latter than the former.

几年前，我们想知道“让物品更易使用”这一设计理念能应用到多大范围，从而把业务从核心的厨房工具扩展到其他产品；当时，我再次想起这项原则。我们开展了所谓的焦点小组研究（focus groups），让参与者查看一些人物照片，并挑出他们认为会使用和不会使用我们产品的人。他们每次都认为，看起来身体健壮的人会使用我们的产品，而看起来年老乏味的人不会使用。然而，这些参与者全都拥有我们的产品，他们自己的外表却更像后一类，远不像前一类。

Although the needs of elderly users and those with deteriorating vision or dexterity are very much taken into consideration when we develop new designs, we try to offer products that appeal to 20- and 30-year-olds. We believe that referring to these products as helping tools would serve only to harm the brand in our customers’ eyes. That’s why our philosophy of universal design, which involves creating products that are comfortably useable by the largest possible range of people, is never explicitly stated as part of our marketing position.

我们开发新设计时，会充分考虑老年使用者以及视力或手部灵活性下降者的需求，但仍努力让产品对20多岁和30多岁的人具有吸引力。我们相信，把这些产品称为辅助工具，只会损害顾客心目中的品牌形象。因此，我们从不在营销定位中明确说明自己的“通用设计”（universal design）理念；这一理念是要创造尽可能让最广泛人群都能舒适使用的产品。

We’ve found that market research does not need to be very sophisticated. For instance, we have conducted simple surveys in the lobby of our building, offering free products in exchange for people’s opinions. Some may call this unscientific, but we have uncovered great insights this way. Sometimes the most important signals come from an executive’s own instincts. In Tibal Fisher’s case, this could have told him what his surveys and focus groups didn’t: 60-plus-year-olds won’t support a business that expects them to act their age.

我们发现，市场研究不必非常复杂。例如，我们曾在公司大楼门厅进行简单调查，用免费产品换取人们的意见。有人可能认为这不科学，但我们用这种方法获得了非常深刻的见解。有时，最重要的信号来自高管自己的直觉。对 Tibal Fisher 而言，直觉本可以告诉他调查和焦点小组没有发现的事实：60岁及以上的人不会支持一家要求他们表现得符合自己年龄的企业。
