Q14 · 正确标题错误

Current pressures forcing companies to address CSR

迫使企业处理社会责任问题的当前压力

你的标题

The past shows us businesses are responsible for future outcomes

历史表明企业应对未来后果负责

AIn the 1980s, Norwegian Prime Minister Gro Harlem Brundtland gave the following definition of corporate social responsibility (CSR): ‘Meeting the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.’ Nowadays, governments, activists and the media are quick to hold companies to account for the social consequences of their actions. As a result, corporate social responsibility has become a priority for business leaders around the world. When a well-run business applies its vast resources and expertise to social problems that it understands and in which it has a stake, it can have a greater impact than any other organization. Although many companies have already done much to improve the social and environmental impact of their activities, these efforts are not always as productive as they could be.

20世纪80年代，挪威首相 Gro Harlem Brundtland 对企业社会责任（CSR）作出如下定义：“在满足当代人需求的同时，不损害后代人满足自身需求的能力。”如今，政府、活动人士和媒体会迅速要求企业为其行为造成的社会后果负责。因此，企业社会责任已经成为全球企业领导者优先考虑的问题。一家管理良好的企业如果把庞大的资源和专业能力用于自己了解且利益相关的社会问题，所能产生的影响可能超过任何其他组织。尽管许多企业已经为改善自身活动对社会和环境的影响做了大量工作，这些努力并不总能发挥其本可达到的成效。

Q15 · 正确标题正确

The reasons business and society depend on each other

企业与社会相互依存的原因

你的标题

The reasons business and society depend on each other

企业与社会相互依存的原因

BTo advance CSR we need first to acknowledge the interrelationship between a corporation and society. Successful corporations need a healthy society. Education, health care and equal opportunities are essential to a productive workforce. Safe products and working conditions not only attract customers but also lower internal costs as there are fewer accidents, while good government and strong regulatory standards protect both consumers and competing companies from exploitation. Ultimately, a healthy society creates demand for business as more human needs are met and aspirations grow. At the same time, a healthy society needs successful companies to create the jobs, wealth and innovation that improve social conditions over time.

要推进企业社会责任，我们首先需要承认企业与社会之间相互依存。成功的企业需要健康的社会。教育、医疗保健和平等机会，是形成高效劳动力的必要条件。安全的产品和工作条件不仅能吸引顾客，还能因为事故减少而降低企业内部成本；良好的政府治理和严格的监管标准，则能保护消费者和相互竞争的企业免受剥削。归根结底，随着越来越多的人类需求得到满足、愿望不断增长，健康的社会会创造商业需求。与此同时，健康的社会也需要成功企业创造就业、财富和创新，从而逐步改善社会状况。

CA company’s impact on society also changes over time, as social standards evolve and science progresses. Asbestos, now understood to be a serious health risk, was thought to be safe in the early 1900s, given the scientific knowledge then available. Evidence of its risks gradually mounted for more than 50 years before any company was held liable for the harm it can cause. Many firms that failed to anticipate the consequences of this evolving body of research have been bankrupted by the results. No longer can companies be content to monitor only the obvious social impacts of today. Without a careful process to identify evolving social effects, firms may risk their very survival.

随着社会标准演变和科学进步，企业对社会的影响也会发生变化。石棉如今被认为会严重危害健康，但以20世纪初当时已有的科学知识来看，人们曾认为它是安全的。有关石棉风险的证据逐渐积累了50多年，之后才有企业因其可能造成的伤害而被追究责任。许多公司未能预见这些不断累积的研究证据会带来何种后果，最终因此破产。企业再也不能满足于只监测当今显而易见的社会影响。如果没有一套审慎流程来识别不断变化的社会影响，企业可能会危及自身生存。

Q17 · 正确标题错误

Companies should be selective in their choice of CSR

企业应审慎选择其社会责任项目

你的标题

Lack of action by the state in social issues

政府在社会问题上缺乏行动

DHowever, no business can solve all of society’s problems or bear the cost of doing so. Instead, each company must choose issues that intersect with its particular business. Other social agendas are best left to companies in other industries or government institutions that are better positioned to address them. The essential test that should guide CSR is not whether a cause is worthy but whether it presents an opportunity to create shared value – that is, a meaningful benefit for society that is also valuable for the business. Each company should try to identify the social problems that it is best equipped to help resolve. Addressing social issues in this way will lead to self-sustaining solutions that are not dependent on government subsidies.

然而，任何企业都无法解决社会的全部问题，也无力承担这样做的成本。每家公司应当选择与自身特定业务有交集的问题。其他社会议题最好留给其他行业的企业，或更有条件处理这些问题的政府机构。指导企业社会责任的关键检验标准，不是某项事业是否值得支持，而是它能否创造共享价值——也就是既能给社会带来切实利益，又对企业有价值。每家公司都应努力找出自己最有能力协助解决的社会问题。以这种方式处理社会问题，将产生可以自我维持、不依赖政府补贴的解决方案。

Q18 · 正确标题正确

A CSR initiative without direct financial gain

不带来直接经济收益的企业社会责任举措

你的标题

A CSR initiative without direct financial gain

不带来直接经济收益的企业社会责任举措

EThe best corporate citizenship initiatives involve far more than simply donating money; they specify clear goals and measure their effectiveness over time. A good example is the manufacturing company GE’s program to adopt public high schools in poorer areas near several of its major US facilities. The company contributes between $250,000 and $1 million over a five-year period to each school. GE’s managers and employees take an active role by working with school administrators to assess needs and tutor students. GE conducted a study of ten schools in the program between 1989 and 1999 and nearly all showed significant improvement in their academic standards. Programs such as this one create goodwill and improve relations with local governments. Their effect is inherently limited, however. No matter how beneficial the program is, it remains incidental to the company’s manufacturing business, and the direct effect on GE’s recruiting and retention is modest.

最好的企业公民行动远不只是捐款；它们会明确具体目标，并持续衡量成效。一项良好范例，是制造企业 GE 在美国几处主要设施附近的贫困地区结对帮办公立高中的计划。公司在五年内向每所学校投入 $250,000 至 $1,000,000。GE 的管理人员和员工会积极参与，与学校管理者一起评估需求，并辅导学生。1989至1999年间，GE 对参与该计划的十所学校进行了研究，结果几乎所有学校的学业水平都有显著提高。此类计划能积累好感，并改善与地方政府的关系。然而，其作用本身有限。无论该计划带来多少益处，它对公司的制造业务而言仍只是附带活动，对 GE 招聘和留住员工的直接影响也不大。

Q19 · 正确标题错误

How CSR may help one business to expand

企业社会责任可能如何帮助一家企业扩张

你的标题

Companies should be selective in their choice of CSR

FSoftware company Microsoft’s partnership with the American Association of Community Colleges is a good example of a mutually beneficial opportunity. The shortage of information technology workers is a significant constraint on Microsoft’s growth; currently there are 450,000 unfilled IT positions in the US alone. Community colleges with an enrolment of 11.6 million students, representing 45% of all US undergraduates, could provide a solution. Microsoft recognizes, however, that these colleges face special challenges: IT curricula are not standardized, technology used in classrooms is often outdated, and there are no professional development programs to keep teachers up to date. Microsoft’s $50 million, five-year initiative was aimed at all three problems. In addition to contributing money and products, Microsoft sent employee volunteers to colleges to assess needs and contribute to curriculum development. Microsoft’s program benefited many communities while also having a clear impact on company profits.

软件公司 Microsoft 与美国社区学院协会（American Association of Community Colleges）的合作，是互利机会的良好范例。信息技术人才短缺严重制约 Microsoft 的发展；目前，仅美国就有45万个空缺的IT职位。社区学院共有1,160万名学生，占美国本、专科大学生总数的45%，或许能够提供解决办法。不过，Microsoft 认识到，这些学院面临特殊挑战：IT课程没有统一标准，课堂所用技术往往已经过时，也没有帮助教师掌握最新知识的专业发展计划。Microsoft 这项为期五年、投入 $50,000,000 的行动，旨在解决上述三个问题。除了提供资金和产品，Microsoft 还派员工志愿者前往学院评估需求，并参与课程开发。Microsoft 的计划让许多社区受益，同时也对公司利润产生了明确影响。

Q20 · 正确标题错误

CSR in every aspect of a company’s business

企业经营各个方面的社会责任

你的标题

How CSR may help one business to expand

企业社会责任可能如何帮助一家企业扩张

GA final example is the company Whole Foods Market. Its aim is to sell organic, healthy food to customers who are passionate about food and the environment. The company sources all of its products from local farmers and screens out up to 100 common ingredients that it considers to be unhealthy or environmentally damaging. Furthermore, Whole Foods’ stores are constructed using a majority of recycled materials and the company recently bought renewable wind energy credits equal to 100% of its electricity use. Any spoiled produce is trucked to regional centers for composting and all of their vehicles are being converted to biofuels. In this initiative even the cleaning products used in their stores are environmentally friendly. In short, the entire company’s operations reinforce its ideals, helping to distinguish Whole Foods from its competitors.

最后一个例子是 Whole Foods Market 公司。它的目标，是向热爱食物和环境的顾客销售有机、健康的食品。该公司全部产品都从当地农民处采购，并排除多达100种它认为不健康或破坏环境的常见成分。此外，Whole Foods 的门店主要使用再生材料建造；公司最近还购买了相当于其全部用电量100%的可再生风能额度。所有变质农产品都会用卡车运到区域中心制成堆肥，公司全部车辆也正在改用生物燃料。在这项行动中，就连门店使用的清洁用品也对环境友好。简而言之，整家公司的运营都在强化其理念，帮助 Whole Foods 与竞争者区分开来。
