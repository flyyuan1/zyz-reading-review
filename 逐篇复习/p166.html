Q1 · 正确标题正确

A chance discovery

一次偶然发现

你的标题

A chance discovery

一次偶然发现

AThe story of tea began in ancient China over 5,000 years ago. According to legend, the Emperor Shen Nung was a skilled ruler, creative scientist and patron of the arts. His far-sighted edicts required, among other things, that all drinking water be boiled as a hygienic precaution. One summer day, while visiting a distant region of his realm, he and the court stopped to rest. In accordance with his ruling, the servants began to boil water for the court to drink. Dried leaves from a nearby bush fell into the boiling water, and as the leaves infused the water turned brown. As a scientist, the Emperor was intrigued by the new liquid, drank some, and found it very refreshing. And so, according to legend, tea was created.

茶的故事始于5,000多年前的古代中国。相传，神农（Emperor Shen Nung）既是一位能力出众的统治者，也是一位富有创造力的科学家和艺术赞助人。他颇有远见的法令规定，其中一项是所有饮用水都必须煮沸，以此作为卫生防范措施。一个夏日，他到国土中一个遥远地区巡视，途中与朝廷随员停下休息。按照他的规定，仆人开始烧水供众人饮用。附近一丛灌木上的干叶落进沸水；随着叶片泡开，水变成了棕色。身为科学家的皇帝对这种新液体产生了兴趣，喝了一些，发现十分提神。于是，按照传说，茶就这样诞生了。

Q2 · 正确标题错误

BTea consumption spread throughout Chinese culture, reaching into every aspect of society. The first definitive book was written on tea – a book clearly reflecting Zen Buddhist philosophy – 1,200 years ago. The first tea seeds were brought to Japan by a returning Buddhist priest, who had seen the value of tea in enhancing meditation in China. As a result, he is known as the “Father of Tea” in Japan. Because of this early association, tea in Japan has always been linked with Zen Buddhism. Tea received the Japanese Emperor’s support almost instantly and spread rapidly from the royal court and monasteries to other sections of society.

饮茶在中国文化中广泛传播，深入社会的方方面面。1,200年前，第一部专门论述茶的权威著作问世，书中清楚体现了禅宗佛教哲学。第一批茶种由一名归国佛教僧人带到日本；他在中国见识过茶对增强冥想的价值。因此，他在日本被称为“茶之父”。由于这种早期联系，茶在日本一直与禅宗佛教相连。茶几乎立即得到日本天皇的支持，并迅速从皇室和寺院传播到社会其他阶层。

Q3 · 正确标题错误

Tea-making as a ritual

制茶作为一种仪式

你的标题

A luxury item

一种奢侈品

CTea was elevated to an art form in the Japanese tea ceremony, in which supreme importance is given to making tea in the most perfect, most polite, most graceful, most charming manner possible. Such a purity of expression prompted the creation of a particular form of architecture for tea houses, duplicating the simplicity of a forest cottage. The cultural/artistic hostesses of Japan, the geishas, began to specialise in the presentation of the tea ceremony. However, as more and more people became involved in the excitement surrounding tea, the purity of the original concept was lost, and for a period the tea ceremony became corrupted, boisterous and highly embellished. Efforts were then made to return to the earlier simplicity, with the result that, in the 15th and 16th centuries, tea was viewed as the ultimate gift. Even warlords paused for tea before battles.

在日本茶道中，茶被提升为一种艺术形式；人们极其重视以尽可能完美、礼貌、优雅而迷人的方式制茶。这种纯粹的表达催生了一种专用于茶室的建筑形式，它再现了林中小屋的简朴。日本兼具文化与艺术修养的女侍者艺伎，开始专门表演茶道。然而，随着越来越多的人卷入围绕茶兴起的热潮，原有理念的纯粹性逐渐丧失，茶道一度失去本真，变得喧闹而装饰过度。此后，人们努力回归早期的简朴，结果到了15和16世纪，茶被视为至高无上的馈赠。就连军阀也会在战斗前停下来饮茶。

News of tea reaches another continent

茶的消息传到另一大陆

DWhile tea was at this high level of development in parts of Asia, information concerning the then-unknown beverage began to filter back to Europe. Earlier traders had mentioned it, but were unclear as to whether tea should be eaten or drunk. The first European to personally encounter tea and write about it was Portuguese – Portugal, with her technologically advanced navy, had been successful in gaining the first right of trade with China.

当茶在亚洲部分地区发展到如此高的水平时，有关这种当时尚不为人知的饮品的信息开始逐渐传回欧洲。更早的商人曾提到过它，却不清楚茶究竟应当食用还是饮用。第一个亲自接触茶并撰文记述的欧洲人是葡萄牙人；葡萄牙凭借技术先进的海军，成功获得了最先与中国开展贸易的权利。

Q5 · 正确标题错误

A luxury item

你的标题

Not enough tea to meet demand

茶叶不足以满足需求

ETea finally arrived in Europe in the 16th century, brought to Holland by the country’s navy, and became very fashionable in the Dutch capital, The Hague. This was due in part to tea being very expensive (over $100 per pound), which immediately made it the domain of the wealthy. Slowly, as the amount of tea imported increased, the price fell, and by 1675 it was available in common food shops throughout Holland.

16世纪，茶终于由荷兰海军带到欧洲，并在荷兰首都海牙变得非常时髦。部分原因在于茶十分昂贵，每磅价格超过$100，因此立刻成了富人的专属之物。随着进口茶叶数量逐渐增加，价格慢慢下降；到1675年，荷兰各地普通食品店都能买到茶。

Q6 · 正确标题正确

Is tea a good or a bad thing?

你的标题

Is tea a good or a bad thing?

茶究竟是好是坏？

FAs the consumption of tea increased dramatically in Dutch society, doctors and university authorities in Holland argued as to its benefits or drawbacks. The public largely ignored the scholarly debate and continued to enjoy their new beverage, though the controversy lasted from 1635 to roughly 1657. Throughout this period, France and Holland led Europe in the use of tea.

随着饮茶在荷兰社会中大幅增加，荷兰的医生和大学权威开始争论茶究竟有益还是有害。公众基本无视这场学术争论，继续享用这种新饮品；不过，争论从1635年一直持续到大约1657年。在此期间，法国和荷兰在欧洲饮茶方面处于领先地位。

Q7 · 正确标题正确

In – and sometimes out – of fashion

时而流行，时而失宠

你的标题

In – and sometimes out – of fashion

时而流行，时而失宠

GAs the craze for all things oriental swept through Europe, tea became part of everyday life. Adding milk to the drink was first mentioned in 1680. Around that time, Dutch inns provided the first restaurant service of tea. Innkeepers would furnish guests with a portable tea set complete with a heating unit. The Dutchman would then prepare tea for himself and his friends outside in the inn garden. Tea remained popular in France for only about fifty years, being replaced by a preference for wine, chocolate and exotic coffees. Tea was introduced into England in 1660 by King Charles II and his Portuguese queen, who were both confirmed tea drinkers. Tea mania swept across England as it had earlier spread throughout France and Holland. By 1708, tea importation had risen to thirteen times the 1699 level. Tea was drunk by all levels of society.

随着对一切东方事物的狂热席卷欧洲，茶成为日常生活的一部分。有关在茶中加奶的最早记载出现在1680年。大约同一时期，荷兰客栈首次提供餐馆式的饮茶服务。客栈老板会为客人配备一套便携茶具，其中包括加热装置。荷兰客人随后会在客栈花园里为自己和朋友沏茶。茶在法国只流行了约50年，之后人们转而偏爱葡萄酒、巧克力和异域咖啡。1660年，Charles II 国王和他的葡萄牙王后把茶引入英格兰，两人都是坚定的饮茶者。饮茶热潮席卷英格兰，就像此前传遍法国和荷兰一样。到1708年，茶叶进口量已经增长到1699年的13倍。社会各个阶层都饮茶。

Q8 · 正确标题正确

Difficulties in importing tea

进口茶叶的困难

你的标题

Difficulties in importing tea

进口茶叶的困难

HRussian interest in tea began as early as 1618, when the Chinese embassy in Moscow presented several chests of tea to the Emperor, Czar Alexis. Later in the century, a trade treaty between Russia and China allowed caravans to cross back and forth freely between the two countries. Still, the journey was not easy. The average caravan consisted of 200 to 300 camels, and the 18,000-kilometre trip took over 16 months to complete. Eventually, however, tea became – as it still is – one of the most popular drinks in the country.

俄罗斯对茶的兴趣早在1618年就已开始，当时中国驻莫斯科使团向 Alexis 沙皇赠送了几箱茶。该世纪后期，俄中两国签订贸易条约，允许商队在两国之间自由往返。即便如此，旅程仍不轻松。一般一支商队由200至300头骆驼组成，走完18,000公里的路程需要16个多月。不过，茶最终成为这个国家最受欢迎的饮品之一，并一直延续至今。
