Some common social functions of flowers

鲜花的一些常见社会功能

AWhy do we give people flowers? To offer condolence to those who are grieving. To celebrate. To woo. To ask for forgiveness. We all know intuitively that there is a universal emotional response. In the US alone, the flower industry is now worth about $5 billion a year, which suggests that, at the very least, they service a compelling human need.

我们为什么要送花？向悲痛中的人表示慰问，为喜事庆祝，向心仪之人求爱，或请求原谅。我们都凭直觉知道，人类对鲜花有一种普遍的情感反应。仅在美国，花卉产业如今每年产值约50亿；这表明，鲜花至少满足了人类一种强烈的需求。

Q28 · 正确标题未作答

Becoming more open about personal matters

更愿意谈论私事

你的标题

未作答

BRecent studies at the Department of Psychology at Rutgers State University of New Jersey investigated claims that flowers are unique among living organisms in their ability to induce profound changes in our emotional state. As the first part of their research, the Rutgers team visited 150 women in their homes. Each was presented with a variety of gifts such as flowers, fruit or sweets. The women were unaware that the study was about the effect of the flowers on their emotions. They were told that it was a study about their daily moods, and that they would receive a gift in return for taking part. Following the presentation of the gift, those receiving flowers were assessed as displaying a much more positive mood than those who got other gifts, and this effect lasted for several days. After receiving flowers, they were also more willing to answer questions concerning their social circle and intimate conversations with friends and family. The results suggest that flowers influence our secondary socio-emotional behaviours, as well as having a strong effect on our immediate emotional expression.

新泽西州罗格斯州立大学（Rutgers State University of New Jersey）心理学系（Department of Psychology）近期开展研究，检验了这样一种说法：在所有生物中，鲜花具有独特能力，能够让我们的情绪状态发生深刻变化。在研究的第一部分，罗格斯团队上门访问了150名女性，分别向她们送上鲜花、水果或糖果等礼物。受访女性并不知道研究考察的是鲜花对情绪的影响。研究人员告诉她们，这项研究关注日常心情，而参与者会获得礼物作为回报。送出礼物后，评估发现，收到鲜花的人表现出的心情远比收到其他礼物的人积极，而且这种效果持续了数天。收到鲜花后，她们也更愿意回答有关社交圈，以及与亲友私密交谈的问题。结果表明，鲜花不仅会强烈影响我们即时的情绪表达，还会影响后续的社会情感行为。

Bridging the gap between strangers in an enclosed space

在封闭空间内拉近陌生人之间的距离

你的标题

未作答

CIn the second study, the psychologists observed participants being handed single flowers, or alternative gifts, in a constrained and stressful situation – inside an elevator. Contrary to predictions regarding gender differences, both men and women presented with flowers were more likely to smile, to stand closer and to initiate conversation. Several subjects who were given the alternative gift then learnt that flowers were also being handed out, and returned to the elevator and demanded a flower. The scientists used elevators for this study precisely because the most typical behaviour in sparsely occupied elevators is for people to retreat to opposite corners. The subjects who received flowers, however, closed up that space to a considerable extent – indicating that the flowers not only induced a strong positive mood, but brought a significant affiliation among people who had never previously met.

第二项研究中，心理学家观察参与者在一个空间受限、压力较大的环境——电梯内——收到一枝鲜花或其他礼物时的反应。与关于性别差异的预测相反，收到鲜花的男性和女性都更可能微笑、站得更近并主动交谈。几名收到其他礼物的受试者后来得知现场还在送花，便返回电梯索要鲜花。科学家之所以使用电梯开展研究，正是因为人不多时，电梯内最典型的行为是各自退到相对的角落。然而，收到鲜花的受试者大幅缩短了彼此间的距离。这表明，鲜花不仅带来强烈的积极情绪，还使此前从未见过面的人产生了明显的亲近感。

一些对鲜花出乎意料的强烈反应

DThe third study involved regularly sending flowers to a selected sample of men and women. The researchers found not only a profound elevation of mood, but also reliable improvements in other measures of cognitive function, like memory. In this series of experiments, some participants produced such extraordinary emotional displays that the psychologists were totally unprepared for them. Subjects gave spontaneous hugs and kisses to the people who delivered the flowers, and sent invitations to the psychologists to come to their homes for refreshments.

第三项研究定期向选定的一组男性和女性送花。研究人员发现，参与者不仅情绪大幅改善，记忆力等其他认知功能指标也出现了稳定提升。在这一系列实验中，一些参与者表现出的情感反应极为强烈，完全出乎心理学家的预料。受试者会自发拥抱、亲吻送花的人，还会邀请心理学家到家中吃些点心、喝点饮料。

Q31 · 正确标题未作答

An imperfect theory

一个并不完善的理论

你的标题

未作答

EVarious evolutionary hypotheses attempt to explain the remarkably powerful psychological effect of flowers. One is that our aesthetic preferences for fertile locations and growing things stem from prehistory, when these clues in our environment could mean the difference between starvation and survival. We may have become hardwired to respond positively to flowers because for early man, finding them in a particular location predicted future food supplies and possibly a better place to rear children. Yet the flaw in this argument is that the showy flowers which humans seem to find most visually attractive are generally found on those plants which yield no edible products.

各种进化假说试图解释鲜花为何具有如此强大的心理作用。一种解释认为，我们在审美上偏爱肥沃之地和生长中的事物，这源于史前时代；当时，环境中的这些线索可能决定人们是因饥饿而死，还是能够存活下来。我们可能已经形成了对鲜花作出积极反应的先天倾向，因为对早期人类而言，在某个地方发现鲜花，预示着未来会有食物供应，而且那里或许更适合养育孩子。然而，这一论点有一个缺陷：人类似乎认为视觉上最吸引人的艳丽花朵，通常生长在不会产出任何可食用物的植物上。

Q32 · 正确标题未作答

Sensory appeal versus practical purpose of flowers

鲜花的感官吸引力与实用功能

你的标题

未作答

FThe Rutgers psychologists’ findings show that the various physical attributes of flowers combine to directly affect our emotions through multi-channel interactions. We have evolved preferences for the particular colours, textures, patterned symmetries and specific floral odours which influence our moods. Indeed, previous research has established that popular perfumes, which often have a floral ‘top-note’, will actually reduce depression. The origins of these inclinations may well be as the evolutionary theories suggest: the patterned symmetries of flowers can be detected easily as a recognisable signal within a wide variety of visual arrays, and a response to certain colour tones is important in finding ripe fruit against a leafy background. But, claim the Rutgers team, these preferences have long been separated from their primary evolutionary use, and become rewarding to us more generally. Thus plants with preferred colours, shapes and odours – despite having no other products – would therefore be protected and dispersed.

罗格斯心理学家的发现表明，鲜花的各种物理特征相互结合，通过多重感官通道的互动直接影响我们的情绪。我们在进化中形成了对特定颜色、质地、规则对称图案和某些花香的偏好，而这些特征会影响心情。事实上，早先研究已经证实，通常带有花香“前调”的流行香水确实会减轻抑郁。这些偏好的起源很可能正如进化理论所说：在纷繁的视觉景象中，花朵规则的对称图案很容易被辨认为一种信号；而要在绿叶背景中找到成熟果实，对某些色调作出反应也十分重要。不过，罗格斯团队认为，这些偏好早已脱离最初的进化用途，并在更广泛的意义上给我们带来愉悦。因此，即使没有其他产物，具有受偏爱颜色、形状和气味的植物仍会受到保护并传播开来。

Q33 · 正确标题未作答

A mutually beneficial relationship?

一种互惠关系？

你的标题

未作答

GThe Rutgers study suggests that flowers may have actually evolved to exploit their peculiar impact on humans. The team’s theory proposes a plant-human co-evolution, or even domestication, based on the intense emotional rewards that flowers provide. The idea that flowering plants, with no known food or other basic survival value to man, have co-evolved with us by exploiting an emotional niche instead, is very much like the scenario presented for the evolution of dogs. Flowers may be the plant equivalent of ‘companion animals’. If this is true, then there is a very real sense in which, when you next give flowers, they are using you just as much as you are using them.

罗格斯大学的研究表明，鲜花实际上可能是为了利用自身对人类的特殊影响而进化的。研究团队提出，植物与人类之间存在共同进化，甚至可能存在驯化关系，其基础正是鲜花带来的强烈情感回报。开花植物没有已知的食物价值或其他基本生存价值，却通过利用情感生态位与我们共同进化；这一观点与关于狗的进化情景十分相似。鲜花或许就是植物中的“伴侣动物”。如果这是真的，那么下次你送花时，从一个非常实际的意义上说，鲜花利用你的程度，与你利用它们的程度同样大。
