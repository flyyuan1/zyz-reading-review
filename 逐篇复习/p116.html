AThe English explorer and writer John Franklin (1786–1847) joined the Navy at the age of 14, and fought at the battle of Trafalgar. When peace came, he turned his attention to Arctic exploration, and in particular to solving the conundrum of the Northwest Passage, a mythical clear-water route through the ice which would, if it existed, link the Pacific Ocean on America’s West Coast with the Atlantic on its East. The first expedition Franklin led to the Arctic was an arduous overland journey lasting from 1819 to 1822, in which he and his twenty-strong team covered 8,880 kilometres on foot. Their expedition was a triumph of surveying – they managed to chart hundreds of kilometres of previously unknown coastline – but food ran out on their journey back to civilisation, and the men were forced to eat their belts and their boots (which they boiled up to make leather soup).

英国探险家兼作家 John Franklin（1786—1847）14岁加入海军，并参加了特拉法加海战（battle of Trafalgar）。和平到来后，他把注意力转向北极探险，尤其希望解开西北航道（Northwest Passage）之谜。这条传说中的无冰水道如果确实存在，就能穿过冰区，把美洲西海岸的太平洋与东海岸的大西洋连接起来。Franklin 第一次率队前往北极，是一次从1819年持续到1822年的艰苦陆路远征；他和20人的团队徒步行进了8,880千米。这次远征在测绘方面大获成功——他们绘出了数百千米此前无人知晓的海岸线——但返程途中食物耗尽，队员被迫吃掉自己的皮带和靴子；他们把这些东西煮成了皮革汤。

BThere followed a career as a travel writer and public speaker (‘the man who ate his boots’ was Franklin’s tag-line). Then in 1845, Franklin set off back to the Arctic with two ships – the Erebus and the Terror – and 129 men. Nothing was heard of them for 14 years, although more than 30 expeditions were dispatched in search of them. Eventually it was discovered that Franklin and all his team had perished after their ships were trapped in the ice.

此后，Franklin 开始以旅行作家和公众演说家的身份活动，“吃过自己靴子的人”成了他的标志性称呼。1845年，Franklin 带着两艘船——“幽冥”号（Erebus）和“恐怖”号（Terror）——以及129人再次启程前往北极。此后14年间，他们音信全无，尽管人们派出了30多支搜救队寻找他们。最终，人们发现，Franklin 及其全体队员在船只被困冰层后全部遇难。

CIn his personal correspondence and published memoirs, Franklin comes across as a man dedicated to the external duties of war and exploration, who kept introspection and self-analysis to a minimum. His blandness makes him an amenably malleable subject for a novelist, and Sten Nadolny has taken full advantage of this in his book. Most important, Nadolny has endowed John Franklin with a defining trait for which there is no historical evidence: Langsamkeit (‘slowness’, or ‘calmness’).

从 Franklin 的私人书信和已出版回忆录来看，他全心投入战争和探险等对外职责，极少进行内省和自我分析。他这种平淡无奇的性格，使他成为小说家笔下容易塑造的对象；Sten Nadolny 在书中充分利用了这一点。最重要的是，Nadolny 赋予 John Franklin 一种决定性特质，而历史上没有证据证明他具有这种特质：Langsamkeit，即“缓慢”或“沉静”。

DSlowness influences not only Franklin’s behaviour, but also his vision, his thought and his speech. The opening scene of The Discovery of Slowness depicts Franklin as a young boy, failing to catch a ball because his reaction time is too slow. Despite the bullying of his peers, Franklin resolves not to fall into step with ‘their way of doing things’. For Nadolny, Franklin’s fascination with the Arctic stems from his desire to find an environment suited to his peculiar slowness. He describes Franklin as a boy dreaming of the ‘time without hours and days’ which exists in the far north, a place ‘where nobody would find him too slow’.

缓慢不仅影响 Franklin 的行为，也影响他的视觉、思维和言语。《缓慢的发现》的开篇场景把 Franklin 描写成一个小男孩：由于反应太慢，他没能接住球。尽管受到同伴欺凌，Franklin 仍决心不去迎合“他们做事的方式”。在 Nadolny 笔下，Franklin 之所以迷恋北极，是因为他渴望找到一个适合自己这种特殊缓慢特质的环境。他描写少年 Franklin 梦想着遥远北方那种“不分小时与日子的时间”，梦想着一个“没有人会嫌他太慢”的地方。

EIce is a slow mover. The compressed blue ice inside an Alpine crevasse will have fallen as snow several decades earlier. Polar pack ice takes at least two years to form. The film of crystals which first appears on the surface of the sea thickens into a silkily pliant layer called nilas; this in turn consolidates into young ice, which then deepens during several seasons to become pack. Ice demands a corresponding patience from those who venture onto it. The explorers who have thrived at high latitudes and at high altitudes haven’t usually been men of great speed. They have tended instead to demonstrate unusual self-possession, a considerable capacity for boredom, and a talent for the uncomplaining endurance of suffering.

冰移动得很慢。阿尔卑斯山区（Alpine）的冰川裂隙中压实的蓝冰，应是几十年前降下的雪形成的。极地浮冰至少需要两年才能形成。最先出现在海面的晶体薄膜，会逐渐增厚成一种如丝般柔韧的冰层，称为尼拉斯冰（nilas）；它继而固结成初生冰，再历经数个季节不断加厚，最终成为浮冰。冰要求踏上它的人也具有相应的耐心。那些能在高纬度和高海拔地区顺利探险的人，通常并不是行动极快的人。相反，他们往往表现出非同寻常的沉着，极能忍受无聊，也善于毫无怨言地承受苦难。

FThese were all qualities which the historical Franklin possessed in abundance, and so Nadolny’s exaggeration of them isn’t unreasonable. Even as an adult, Franklin’s slowness of thought means that he is unable to speak fluently, so he learns by heart ‘entire fleets of words and batteries of responses’, and speaks a languid, bric-à-brac language. In the Navy, his method of thinking first and acting later initially provokes mockery from his fellow sailors. But Franklin persists in doing things his way, and gradually earns the respect of those around him. To a commodore who tells him to speed up his report of an engagement, he replies: ‘When I tell something, sir, I use my own rhythm.’ A lieutenant says approvingly of him: ‘Because Franklin is so slow, he never loses time.’

历史上的 Franklin 充分具备所有这些品质，因此 Nadolny 对它们的夸大并非毫无道理。即使成年后，Franklin 仍因思考缓慢而无法流利说话，于是他把“大量词语和成套应答”熟记于心，说出的语言缓慢而东拼西凑。在海军中，他先思考、后行动的做法起初招来同伴嘲笑。但 Franklin 坚持按自己的方式做事，逐渐赢得了身边人的尊重。一名准将催他加快汇报战斗经过时，他回答：“长官，我讲述事情时有自己的节奏。”一名中尉赞许地评价他：“正因为 Franklin 如此缓慢，他从不浪费时间。”

GNadolny also brings his central metaphor of slowness to bear on the novel’s language. The chapters describing Franklin’s early years are a medley of separate fragments, rhetorical questions, associative jumps and exclamation marks. In the later sections recounting Franklin’s first Arctic expedition, Nadolny brilliantly sets the narrative pace to the rhythms of the frozen landscape, and to the ‘slowness which is bred by hunger’.

Nadolny 还把“缓慢”这一核心隐喻运用到了小说语言中。描写 Franklin 早年的章节，由彼此分离的片段、反问、联想式跳跃和感叹号混合而成。在后来讲述 Franklin 首次北极远征的部分，Nadolny 巧妙地让叙事节奏与冰冻景观的韵律，以及“饥饿孕育出的缓慢”相一致。

HSince it was first published in Germany in 1983, The Discovery of Slowness has sold more than a million copies and been translated into 15 languages. It has been adopted as a manual and manifesto by European pressure groups and institutions representing causes as diverse as sustainable development, management science and motoring policy, even becoming involved in the debate about speed limits on German roads. The various groups that have taken the novel up have one thing in common: a dislike of the high-speed culture of Post-modernity. Nadolny’s Franklin appeals to them because he is immune to ‘the compulsion to be constantly occupied’, and to the idea that ‘someone was better if he could do the same thing fast’. It’s easy to see where the attraction lies for those in management. The novel is crammed with quotations about time-efficiency, punctiliousness and profitability: ‘What did “too late” mean? They hadn’t waited for it long enough, that’s what it meant.’

《缓慢的发现》自1983年首次在德国出版以来，销量已超过100万册，并被译成15种语言。欧洲一些游说团体和机构把它当作手册和宣言，这些组织所关注的议题多种多样，从可持续发展、管理科学到机动车交通政策；这本书甚至进入了德国道路限速的讨论。采用这部小说的不同团体有一个共同点：都不喜欢后现代社会的高速文化。Nadolny 笔下的 Franklin 不受“必须始终忙个不停”的强迫观念影响，也不认同“同一件事做得快的人就更优秀”这一看法，因此受到这些团体欢迎。不难看出，管理领域人士为何会被它吸引。小说中充满了关于时间效率、一丝不苟的态度和盈利能力的引语：“‘太迟’是什么意思？是他们等得还不够久——就是这个意思。”
