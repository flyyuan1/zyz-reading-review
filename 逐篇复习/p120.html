Does class size matter?

班级规模重要吗？

AOf all the ideas for improving education, few are as simple or attractive as reducing the number of pupils per teacher. Unlike competing proposals for reform, class-size reductions rarely elicit huge outcries or involve structural change. The testing of educators, by contrast, generally arouses the anger of unions. Similarly, establishing special ‘charter’ schools involves privileging some schools over others, with the credits provided usually coming out of the budgets of struggling local schools. With its uncomplicated appeal, class-size reduction in the U.S. has lately gone from being a subject of primary academic interest to a policy juggernaut with over twenty states aiming at decreasing class sizes.

在所有改善教育的构想中，很少有哪一种像减少每名教师所教学生人数那样简单而有吸引力。与其他改革方案不同，缩小班级规模很少引发强烈抗议，也很少涉及结构性变革。相比之下，对教育工作者进行考核通常会激起工会的愤怒。同样，设立特殊的“特许学校”（charter schools）意味着让部分学校享有优于其他学校的待遇，而拨给它们的资金通常来自本已举步维艰的当地学校预算。由于这种做法简单易懂、颇具吸引力，缩小班级规模近来在美国已从一个主要由学术界关注的议题，发展成势不可挡的政策潮流；已有20多个州计划减小班级规模。

BDo small classes improve school achievement? To answer this, investigators have attempted to analyse existing data, such as records at the U.S. Department of Education. These reveal that there were steep drops in pupil-teacher ratios between 1969 and 1997, but no significant gains in academic performance.

小班教学能提高学业成绩吗？为了回答这个问题，研究人员尝试分析美国教育部（U.S. Department of Education）记录等现有数据。这些数据表明，1969年至1997年间，每名教师对应的学生人数大幅下降，但学业表现并没有显著提高。

But do these findings mean that class size makes no difference? Not necessarily. For instance, schools strive for more than just high test scores; they also usually try to keep their drop-out rates low. And, indeed, the drop-out rate for older students fell considerably over that period. Because drop-outs generally come from the low end of the achievement distribution, a reduction in the drop-out rate could be expected to pull down average test scores.

然而，这些发现是否意味着班级规模毫无影响？不一定。例如，学校追求的不只是高考试分数，通常也会努力降低辍学率。事实上，在上述时期，年龄较大学生的辍学率大幅下降。辍学者的成绩通常位于成绩分布的较低端，因此可以预期，辍学率下降会拉低平均考试分数。

Another reason for discounting those data is the difficulty of ensuring a level playing field. In a perfect world, U.S. students would all come from well-off families, with two highly educated English-speaking parents who are involved in their children’s schooling. Teachers would all be creative and have complete mastery of the subject matter. The reality is very different.

对这些数据持保留态度的另一个理由，是很难确保比较条件完全公平。在一个理想世界里，美国学生都会来自富裕家庭，父母二人都受过良好教育、会说英语，并参与子女的学校教育。所有教师都会富有创造力，并完全掌握所教内容。现实却截然不同。

COver the past 35 years, some studies of existing data have produced evidence that smaller classes benefit students, but most of these studies were poorly designed. The exception was the Tennessee study called Project STAR (Student Teacher Achievement Ratio). Frederick Mosteller of Harvard University has called it ‘one of the greatest experiments in education in United States history’.

过去35年间，一些利用现有数据开展的研究提供了小班对学生有益的证据，但其中大多数研究设计不佳。例外是田纳西州（Tennessee）一项名为 STAR 项目（Student Teacher Achievement Ratio，学生—教师成就比）的研究。哈佛大学（Harvard University）的 Frederick Mosteller 称它是“美国历史上最伟大的教育实验之一”。

Students entering kindergarten were randomly assigned to one of three kinds of classes: a small class of 13 to 17 students, a regular-size class of 22 to 26 or a regular-size class with both a teacher and a full-time teacher’s aide.

刚进入幼儿园的学生被随机分到三类班级之一：13至17人的小班、22至26人的普通规模班级，或同时配有一名教师和一名全职助教的普通规模班级。

The students remained in whatever category they had been assigned to throughout the third grade, after which they joined a regular classroom in the fourth. To ensure that teaching quality did not differ, teachers were randomly assigned to small and regular-size classrooms. Few teachers received any special training for working with small classes, and there were no new curricular materials.

学生从入学到三年级始终留在最初分配的班级类别中，之后在四年级进入普通班。为确保教学质量没有差异，教师也被随机分配到小班或普通规模班级。很少有教师接受过任何针对小班教学的特别培训，也没有提供新的课程材料。

DAt the end of STAR, researchers analysed the data. Jeremy Finn of New York University and Charles Achilles of Eastern Michigan University found evidence for ‘an array of benefits of small classes’. They calculated that students in smaller classes were outperforming their counterparts in regular-sized classes by the first grade and that this advantage persisted even after students returned to larger classes. They also found that the effect was stronger for black and Hispanic minority groups – a significant finding for policy-makers.

STAR 项目结束后，研究人员分析了数据。纽约大学（New York University）的 Jeremy Finn 和东密歇根大学（Eastern Michigan University）的 Charles Achilles 找到了“小班带来一系列益处”的证据。他们计算发现，到一年级时，小班学生的表现已经优于普通规模班级中的同龄学生；即使学生后来回到更大的班级，这种优势仍然存在。他们还发现，这种效果在黑人和西班牙语裔少数群体中更强，对政策制定者而言，这是一项意义重大的发现。

Eric Hanushek of Stanford, however, criticises some of STAR’s key conclusions. He argues that STAR does not prove that gains persist long after students return to regular classes. It was debatable how much later improvement stemmed from other factors, such as a supportive home. Nor does he accept that the benefits accumulate, with participants widening the gap with their peers in larger classes year by year.

不过，斯坦福大学（Stanford）的 Eric Hanushek 批评了 STAR 项目的一些关键结论。他认为，STAR 项目并未证明学生回到普通班很久以后，成绩优势仍能持续。后期成绩改善有多少来自良好家庭支持等其他因素，仍有争议。他也不接受“小班益处会不断累积、参与者与大班同龄人的差距逐年扩大”这一说法。

Hanushek and others have also shown that during the study too many children moved from regular to small classes, probably because school personnel caved in to parent demands. And Hanushek also asserts that STAR failed to ensure good randomisation of teacher and student assignments. However, these points do not undermine STAR’s basic findings.

Hanushek 等人还指出，研究期间有过多儿童从普通班转入小班，原因可能是学校人员向家长的要求让步。Hanushek 还断言，STAR 项目没有确保教师和学生的分配充分随机化。不过，这些问题并不会动摇 STAR 项目的基本发现。

EThe largest public class size reduction programme so far, California’s, stands more as a warning than as worthy of emulation. That state is trying to reduce classes in kindergarten through grade three despite a shortage of teachers that is most acute in low-income areas.

迄今规模最大的公立学校缩班计划是加利福尼亚州（California）的计划；它与其说值得效仿，不如说是一则警示。该州尽管教师短缺，而且低收入地区的短缺最为严重，仍试图缩小幼儿园至三年级的班级规模。

This is exacerbating the disparity in resources available to rich and poor schools in California, because more affluent areas can attract the best teachers. Indeed, some of the extra teachers needed are being recruited from the poorer schools. Researchers found a statistically significant achievement advantage in reading, writing and mathematics for students in classes that had been reduced to 20. What is more, the effect did not vary for students of different backgrounds.

这正加剧加利福尼亚州富裕学校与贫困学校之间的资源差距，因为较富裕地区能够吸引最优秀的教师。事实上，所需的部分新增教师正是从较贫困学校招来的。研究人员发现，班级人数降至20人后，学生在阅读、写作和数学方面取得了具有统计显著性的成绩优势。此外，这种效果并未因学生背景不同而有所变化。

FWisconsin’s Student Achievement Guarantee in Education (SAGE) was a five-year pilot study to do some of the groundwork for a major project. Class sizes were reduced in only 14 schools, but it was noteworthy for targeting schools at which 30% of the students were below poverty level, compared with California’s across-the-board approach. SAGE lowered the average pupil-teacher ratio in kindergarten through third grade to 12–15:1 from 21–25:1. Analysts have studied the results of first-grade students in these schools and similar first-grade students elsewhere and found the results accord with those from STAR.

威斯康星州（Wisconsin）的学生学业成绩保障计划（Student Achievement Guarantee in Education，SAGE）是一项为期5年的试点研究，旨在为一个大型项目开展部分基础工作。该计划只在14所学校缩小班级规模，但值得注意的是，它把30%的学生处于贫困线以下的学校作为目标，这与加利福尼亚州全面推行的做法不同。SAGE 把幼儿园至三年级平均每名教师对应的学生人数从21—25人降至12—15人。分析人员研究了这些学校一年级学生与其他地方情况相似的一年级学生的成绩，发现结果与 STAR 项目一致。

STAR and SAGE have made it hard to argue against reducing class sizes. But the California initiative shows that reductions made with too little forethought can yield minuscule gains. Administrators need solid information before they can make sensible policy decisions.

STAR 和 SAGE 项目使人们很难再反对缩小班级规模。但加利福尼亚州的计划表明，事先考虑得太少便实施缩班，可能只带来微乎其微的收益。管理者需要掌握可靠信息，才能作出明智的政策决定。
